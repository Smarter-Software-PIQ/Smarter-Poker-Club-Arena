# Engine Cutover Timetable (Every Three Hours)

One published timetable for the certified engine cutover at 01:00, 04:00, 07:00, 10:00, 13:00, 16:00, 19:00 and 22:00 America/Chicago (owner order 2026-11-21; `server/src/maintenance/maintenanceSchedulePolicy.json`). Every lane plans its merges, migrations and Horse switch or settings writes against these windows.

How this page stays true:

- The numbers live in [`engine-cutover-timetable.json`](engine-cutover-timetable.json). The tables below are generated from it by `node scripts/ops/engine-cutover-timetable.mjs --write`; do not edit them by hand.
- `tests/the-cutover-timetable-matches-the-gates.law.test.ts` parses each enforced number from the script, workflow or migration that enforces it (named in the "Enforced by" column) and fails when the JSON disagrees, and fails when these tables are not the rendering of the JSON. A gate change without a timetable change, or the reverse, is a red build.
- Rules that no code enforces yet are listed in section 5 as gaps, with the source that set them. They are lane conventions, not guarantees.
- When the Cutover lane changes a window (its items A and B, section 6), the change is the gate's constant plus one value in the JSON, then `--write`.

Times are Chicago wall clock unless marked UTC or Z. Chicago is UTC-6 in CST (early November to mid March) and UTC-5 in CDT; the slots follow Chicago, so their UTC time moves by an hour at each DST change, while the hourly UTC windows (section 3) and the switch blackout (section 4) do not move.

## Where the sources disagree (read before relying on a window)

1. **Three different hourly windows, not one ":50-:05".** The database refuses migration DDL from :50:00 to :02:59 UTC (`fn_ca_break_window_refuses_migrations`, admits from :03); the applier refuses to send at :50-:03 inclusive (`apply-recorded-migration.mjs`, sends from :04, while its own message says "Dispatch again after :03"); the Horse switch refuses writes at :44-:05 (`WINDOW_MINUTE_MIN` 6, `WINDOW_MINUTE_MAX` 43). The ":50-:05 break window" quoted in coordination posts matches none of them exactly; the switch's is the widest.
2. **The switch's "03:05-04:45 daily preparation" blackout is fixed in UTC (09:05Z-10:45Z).** It equals 03:05-04:45 Chicago only in CST. In CDT it falls at 04:05-05:45 Chicago, so the hour before the 04:00 anchor (03:05-04:05 CDT) is not held and the hold runs past the anchor's thaw. Gap: compute the blackout in America/Chicago, as the dispatcher's `DAILY_GUARD_CHICAGO` already does.
3. **The Backup nightly refresh time.** The header of `.github/workflows/backup-engine.yml` says "00:01 Chicago nightly"; its schedule is 7, 23 and 41 past 05 and 06 UTC, which is 00:07 (00:23, 00:41) Chicago, as `merged-migration-dispatch.mjs` states.
4. **Switch holds from selection to seal are clock-free.** The Cutover lane holds switch and settings writes from selection (slot -30 min) until SEALED; the switch tool enforces only its minute window, the daily blackout, the database freeze and maintenance rows, the engine `/health` maintenance phase and an inbox text parse. Section 5 lists it as a gap.
5. **The daytime cycle deferral binds Club Arena only.** The database refuses migration DDL from every repository only inside the nightly freeze (23:45-04:15, #11906). Between cycles, `[slot -150 min, slot +10 min)` is enforced by Club Arena's dispatcher alone; another repository's migration in that window can still change the copy a candidate was certified on (#11910 makes the selector ignore migrations Club Arena never shipped).
