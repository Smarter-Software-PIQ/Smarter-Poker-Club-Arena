# server/src/services/aSeatHeldForAFriendIsNotGivenToAHorse.law.test.ts

A seat held for an invited friend is not given to a horse, and a private held game is not one of the board's instances (Dan 2026-11-22, from a player's report: "The crazy river is always on the table, even when you invite someone. It doesn't open a new table"). Invite To Game on a Spin or Heads Up opens (or reserves in place) a game whose seats are held for the invited friends (migration 20261123013126); the database refuses a held seat to anyone but its invited holder, a horse by the same rule as a person (20261123013136, native proof scripts/ci/test-private-game-holds-postgres.py). The engine reads live holds through privateGameHolds.ts: no horse fill of a held seat-first board while its hold is live, an unreadable answer holds the fill instead of filling, and a private held game neither covers the board's price point nor counts among the eleven Spins and two Heads Ups the ladder seeds with a horse.

Sit & Go (2026-11-23, migration 20261123071654): Invite To Game on a six or nine seat Sit & Go, a "Starts When Full" satellite run as one included, holds up to seats - 1 seats IN that game (never exclusively), registering the inviter through the normal registration door when needed. Every horse entry into one passes registerHorses, which reads the hold before the union's daily horse plan and before any horse is picked: horses are registered only into the seats neither taken nor held, none when that is none (a deliberate wait, not a miss), and nothing on an unreadable answer; /health publishes privateSitAndGoHolds so the client offers the Sit & Go hold only from an engine that honours it.
