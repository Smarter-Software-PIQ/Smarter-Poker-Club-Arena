# server/src/engine/anOmahaHiddenBetsArePricedAsGiveUps.law.test.ts

omahaHiddenBetsArePricedAsGiveUps (default OFF) prices the hidden part of a PLO4/PLO5 cash heads-up turn bettor's non-value share (people's bets that checked and folded later or mucked, the one-street balanced share behind a thin cell, moved by his own record) as his weakest half of holdings at full realization, inside omahaPeoplePriorCountsHiddenBets' turn bet path. On real turn decisions of Horses facing a person's first turn wager the law holds: it prices heads-up turn first bets only; it only ever turns a fold into a call (never a raise, never a call into a fold); without the hidden-bets prior it decides nothing; the give-up share lies between people's hidden fraction and the one-street balanced share and never exceeds the non-value share; a value-heavy own record shrinks it; a raise, the river and a missing key get none; a re-raised pot is keyed only when its flop checked through; the code reads no identity.
