# server/src/engine/HorseTurnDrawImpliedOdds.law.test.ts

A real Hold'em turn draw facing a wager is priced with the implied odds the stacks can actually pay (2026-11-08, `turnDrawImpliedBonus` in HorseLogic.ts): its outs over 46, times the river bet the stacks behind can pay (capped at 0.7 of the pot after the call) called at minimum defence, over the pot after the call, instead of a flat 0.04. Real HorseLogic.decide, 1/2 cash, 100bb, heads-up 5% rake: Q-J of hearts on K-7-2-4 facing 20 into 28 continues at least 40% of the time where the ablated brain (`turnDrawImpliedOdds: false`) continued at most 15%; the nut flush draw decides exactly as before; with under 1.5 pots behind (V68 no room to be paid), in PLO4 and with no draw the decisions are exactly the ablated brain's. A red law means draws fold to turn bets the stacks would pay them for, or implied odds are granted where the stacks cannot pay them.
