# tests/the-welcome-back-promo-has-one-guard.law.test.ts

The 25-chip signup welcome-back promo (Dan 2026-11-04) is paid by `fn_sp_signup_welcome_back_run`, whose only guard is one `'awarded'` row per player in `sp_signup_welcome_back_awards` plus an op_id derived from the player id. On 2026-11-05 that guard was bypassed - 127 of 160 grants came from management-API backfills calling `fn_promo_disburse` directly with fresh op_ids, leaving 95 of 128 paid members with no awards row - and 32 members were paid twice. The law pins that the runner still checks the awards row and still derives its op_id from the player, that the parallel claim guard added in `20261107124625` (`ca_union_promo_campaign_claims`, `fn_ca_union_promo_grant_once`) stays dropped and is never recreated, that nothing monitors or claws back this promo on a schedule (CLAUDE.md 10.12), that the one-time settlement door `fn_ca_union_promo_reverse_duplicate` remains owner/service gated, skips already-spent chips rather than driving a wallet negative, reverses a leg at most once and is never scheduled, and that the record repair itself moves no chips.
