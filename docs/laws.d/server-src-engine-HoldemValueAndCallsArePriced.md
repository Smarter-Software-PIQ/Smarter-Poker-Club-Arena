# server/src/engine/HoldemValueAndCallsArePriced.law.test.ts

With holdemValueAndCallsArePriced on (default OFF), a heads-up no-limit hold'em cash postflop decision is priced against the bettor's line range: facing a wager the fold, the call or a value raise at a legal priced size of highest value is taken, and the random value-raise gate is never drawn (no value-raise decision reads fastRandom); not facing one a made hand checks or bets by EV, the check carrying his bet and the Horse's call or check-raise, and a river bet is made only when it beats the check. Off (unset, false or a truthy non-boolean) every decision is byte-identical. Fixtures are Auditor D's flagged hands (hvh48-audit-01TLQer7J): red off, green on where the audit and the pricing agree; the stated disagreements are pinned; more than one opponent and tournaments are unchanged.
