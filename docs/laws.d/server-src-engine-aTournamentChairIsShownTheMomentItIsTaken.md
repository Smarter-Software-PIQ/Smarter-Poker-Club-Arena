# server/src/engine/aTournamentChairIsShownTheMomentItIsTaken.law.test.ts

A tournament chair is shown the moment it is taken (Dan 2026-11-18, "THEIR STACK SHOULD SHOW, IT SHOULD NEVER SAY ZERO"): a seat the manager or the database committed during a hand (balancing move, table break, late registration, re-entry) is published at once with its real stack on the live, idle, resync and observer payloads without touching the hand or the adopted roster; the hand roster supersedes it at the boundary and it ends when a later committed read or two rosters no longer hold it; a rostered nonparticipant the database replaced gives up its chair and a hand player never does; a rebuy paid during a hand shows the committed chips over a roster zero, never over a hand player or during a settlement; a cash table reads nothing and the browser route stays refused at a tournament table; reads are coalesced; the manager calls it at all three certified-move sites and at every seat-committing wake, and the show path holds no write.
