# tests/a-lightning-bank-the-win-reading-reads-only-the-session.law.test.ts

The newest fn_lightning_bank_the_win_reading finds a Horse's last hand only inside its open Lightning pool session (cluster, epoch, formed since entry, newest first), never by joining the Horse's whole lightning_hand_player history: that read ran 8.6 s on production and failed 20 to 330 times an hour on 2026-11-14, so a Horse at its bank-the-win line could not leave. Behaviour on real PostgreSQL: tests/unit/lightningBankTheWinReadingSession.pg.test.ts. Two precisions from the exact-head review of #10885: the last hand is the newest by formed_at among settled hands (not by settled_at), and a pool session that continues an existing cash session reads 0 until its first settled hand in the new pool session, which the door floors at two per hand.
