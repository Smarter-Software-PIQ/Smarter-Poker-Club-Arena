# server/src/engine/HorseHoldemLateStreetIsPriced.law.test.ts

With holdemLateStreetIsPriced on (default OFF), every No-Limit Hold'em cash and Lightning turn and river decision in its spot (one to five opponents, one board, not a bomb pot or an all-in-or-fold table) is the priced chooser's answer and never a roll: its module reads no random generator and no hash roll, and in its spot the final action and size are the same whatever the random streams were seeded with; no priced bet has a lower EV than the check, and facing a bet no fold is made where the call prices at or above zero. Blockers and range advantage are terms of the price (a Horse card that removes the bettor's strong combinations raises his fold, one that removes his weak ones lowers it; each term decides some actions across the generated states). Outside its spot nothing changes, off equals unset, tournaments keep their decision unless holdemLateStreetIsPricedInTournaments is on, holdemLateStreetBluffRaiseIsPriced does nothing without the chooser, and the receipt validator admits the chooser's passive answers under its gate (owner 2026-11-24 12:30 / 12:35 AM CST: no unconditioned random play in Hold'em, the best play by math).
