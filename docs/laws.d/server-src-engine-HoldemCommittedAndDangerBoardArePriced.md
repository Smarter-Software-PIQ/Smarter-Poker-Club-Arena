# server/src/engine/HoldemCommittedAndDangerBoardArePriced.law.test.ts

With holdemCommittedNoPairIsPriced on (default OFF), a heads-up no-limit hold'em cash committed decision with no pair and no hole draw facing a wager takes the action of highest expected value among fold, call and all-in jam against the bettor's range calibrated to the engine's equity and the measured fold share of people to Horse postflop raises (HOLDEM_RAISE_FOLD_SHARE_BY_PRICE: under 0.20 0/5, 0.20-0.30 24/55, >=0.30 28/64), replacing unpriced committed jams and calls without drawing from fastRandom. With holdemDangerBoardEquityIsPriced on (default OFF), one pair facing a wager heads-up on a four-flush or four-straight turn or river in no-limit hold'em cash prices its decision equity against the bettor's measured showdown class distribution on such boards (HOLDEM_DANGER_BOARD_BETTOR_SHOWN: 3 no pair, 5 one pair, 42 two pair or better of 50), replacing any unmeasured flat haircut. Off (unset, false or a truthy non-boolean) both options never enter and every decision and random draw is byte-identical.
