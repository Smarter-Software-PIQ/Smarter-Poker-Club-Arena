# server/src/engine/HoldemCommittedAndDangerBoardArePriced.law.test.ts

With holdemCommittedNoPairIsPriced on (default OFF), a heads-up no-limit hold'em cash committed decision with no pair and no hole draw facing a wager takes the action of highest expected value among fold, call and all-in jam against the bettor's range calibrated to the engine's equity, using the smooth empirical all-in raise fold curve anchored to holdemEmpiricalPriors.ts (HOLDEM_EMPIRICAL_RIVER_FOLD_TO_RAISE.all_in_raise: 88/294 at avgVillainCallPrice 0.1855, with quadratic low-price decay eliminating zero-price min-jam arbitrage and optional Empirical-Bayes HUD shrinkage) and backward-induced future-street continuation barrel pricing via estimateHoldemBetWhenCheckedTo when calling non-all-in on flop or turn with chips behind, replacing unpriced committed jams and calls without drawing from fastRandom, with zero PLO4/5/6 reach and dedicated cash6/hu cards in DEVELOPMENT_LEAGUE_CARDS (never LEAGUE_MATCHUPS). With holdemDangerBoardEquityIsPriced on (default OFF), one pair facing a wager heads-up on a four-flush or four-straight turn or river in no-limit hold'em cash prices its decision equity against the bettor's measured showdown class distribution on such boards (HOLDEM_DANGER_BOARD_BETTOR_SHOWN: 3 no pair, 5 one pair, 42 two pair or better of 50), replacing any unmeasured flat haircut. Off (unset, false or a truthy non-boolean) both options never enter and every decision and random draw is byte-identical.
