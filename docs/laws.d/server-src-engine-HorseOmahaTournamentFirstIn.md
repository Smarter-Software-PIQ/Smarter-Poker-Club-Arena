# server/src/engine/HorseOmahaTournamentFirstIn.law.test.ts

In Omaha high tournaments (PLO4, PLO5, PLO6) a Horse does not fold first in short-handed on a Hold'em-scale bar: the small blind against the big blind alone plays every hand, a non-blind seat with two opponents left plays its top 80 percent, and the heads-up big blind defends its top 75 percent against one raise; only when the engine declares the hand a tournament, never in cash, Hold'em or Hi-Lo.
