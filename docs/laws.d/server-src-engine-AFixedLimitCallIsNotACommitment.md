# server/src/engine/AFixedLimitCallIsNotACommitment.law.test.ts

A Fixed-Limit Call Is Not A Commitment: With fixedLimitCallIsNotACommitment On (Default Off), A Fixed-Limit Hold'em Cash Call Has No Stack-To-Pot Term And Is Priced On Its Incremental Cost And The Action Behind, A Closing Call (Nobody Left To Act Or The Street Capped) At The Bare Call Plus Rake And A Call With A Player Left To Act On An Uncapped Street Above It, So Facing One Fixed River Bet A Horse Continues Or Folds The Same With A Short Stack As With A Deep One (Fixed-Limit Lane, 2026-11-14). The raise-behind chance per player is the measured fixed-limit rate (2026-11-15: census D Horse-only, Clopper-Pearson 95% upper bound per street: hold'em 1.4/3.3/4.2%, hi-lo 0.8/1.9/2.7% flop/turn/river), not the earlier 0.12 assumption. Scope (2026-11-16, the lane owner's ruling): the option acts only on fixed-limit hold'em at a cash table; with it on, every flo8 decision (cash or tournament) and every flh tournament decision equals the option off and no F2 receipt fires, while at flh cash it still changes the short-stack calls the commitment surcharge refused.
