# server/src/engine/AFixedLimitCallIsNotACommitment.law.test.ts

A Fixed-Limit Call Is Not A Commitment: With fixedLimitCallIsNotACommitment On (Default Off), A Flh Or Flo8 Call Has No Stack-To-Pot Term And Is Priced On Its Incremental Cost And The Action Behind, A Closing Call (Nobody Left To Act Or The Street Capped) At The Bare Call Plus Rake And A Call With A Player Left To Act On An Uncapped Street Above It, So Facing One Fixed River Bet A Horse Continues Or Folds The Same With A Short Stack As With A Deep One (Fixed-Limit Lane, 2026-11-14). The raise-behind chance per player is the measured fixed-limit rate (2026-11-15: census D Horse-only, Clopper-Pearson 95% upper bound per street: hold'em 1.4/3.3/4.2%, hi-lo 0.8/1.9/2.7% flop/turn/river), not the earlier 0.12 assumption.
