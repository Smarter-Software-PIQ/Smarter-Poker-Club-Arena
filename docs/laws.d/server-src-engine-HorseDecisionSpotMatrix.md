# server/src/engine/HorseDecisionSpotMatrix.law.test.ts

In all nine variants and both modes, a Hybrid Horse resolved by `resolveHorseHybridProfile` and decided by the worker (`DECIDE_FAST`) on states a real `HandController` reached through real actions must answer legally in every kind of postflop spot: facing a bet, a raise and an all-in, first to act, on the flop, turn and river, heads-up, three-handed, six-handed and full ring, short-stacked with the all-in button as its only raise, and on a capped fixed-limit street. Each answer must be a `FAST_RESULT` with a valid receipt and no fallback, on the controller's menu, inside the controller's minimum and maximum (whole chips in tournaments), accepted by `performAction`, with the per-decision upgrade counters the manifest declares (01, 02, 06; 07 in tournaments) fired exactly once. The controller's bounds are checked against the betting structure (no-limit the stack, pot-limit the pot, fixed limit the one street size), and every action the controller offers is executed on a twin of the same hand, so fold, check, call, bet, raise and all-in are each proven offered and accepted in every structure. Guards against a spot type, street or table size where the Horse returns an illegal action or amount, falls back, or silently loses an approved upgrade. The former known gap (reported 2026-11-14): in pot-limit and fixed-limit tournaments, a short-stack shove through the all-in button was not priced by upgrade 07 and was refused as `baseline_not_modeled`. #10793 repaired it, and the law now requires every tournament decision of that spot, the shove included, to be priced with no refusal; it needs at least one shove as witness. Every run has the same inputs and the same sampling seed; wall-clock-budgeted pricing can still vary receipts and, multiway in tournaments, the action, so no assertion reads a wall-clock-budgeted counter or names a sampled action.
