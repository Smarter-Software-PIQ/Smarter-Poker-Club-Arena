# server/src/engine/HorseDecisionSpotMatrix.law.test.ts

In all nine variants and both modes, a Hybrid Horse resolved by `resolveHorseHybridProfile` and decided by the worker (`DECIDE_FAST`) on states a real `HandController` reached through real actions must answer legally in every kind of postflop spot: facing a bet, a raise and an all-in, first to act, on the flop, turn and river, heads-up, three-handed, six-handed and full ring, short-stacked with the all-in button as its only raise, and on a capped fixed-limit street. Each answer must be a `FAST_RESULT` with a valid receipt and no fallback, on the controller's menu, inside the controller's minimum and maximum (whole chips in tournaments), accepted by `performAction`, with the per-decision upgrade counters the manifest declares (01, 02, 06; 07 in tournaments) fired exactly once. The controller's bounds are checked against the betting structure (no-limit the stack, pot-limit the pot, fixed limit the one street size), and every action the controller offers is executed on a twin of the same hand, so fold, check, call, bet, raise and all-in are each proven offered and accepted in every structure. Guards against a spot type, street or table size where the Horse returns an illegal action or amount, falls back, or silently loses an approved upgrade. Carries one deliberately failing test for a known defect: a pot-limit or fixed-limit tournament shove through the all-in button is refused by the tournament utility as `baseline_not_modeled`.
