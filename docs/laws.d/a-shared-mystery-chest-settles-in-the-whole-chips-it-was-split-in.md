# tests/a-shared-mystery-chest-settles-in-the-whole-chips-it-was-split-in.law.test.ts

A mystery chest shared by the players who split the knockout pot is written by `fn_mystery_bounty_reserve` and proved paid by `fn_bounty_obligation_has_complete_marker`, and `fn_mystery_bounty_pay` rolls the whole payment back when the proof is false. The two must cut the chest the same way. Since 20261022170421 the writer cuts in whole chips (`GREATEST(100, unit)`, remainder to the first claimant by user id); the proof was left at the money unit, so an 11-chip chest written 6 + 5 could never prove 5.50 + 5.50, retried forever, stuck the bounty outbox and held every later bust in its event (2026-11-16, Prime Time Free Buy). The law pins that the newest writer cuts in whole chips and that the newest proof accepts exactly that cut, plus the money-unit cut every chest reserved before 2026-10-22 was paid at, claimant by claimant; that the regular and PKO proof stays at the money unit where `fn_collect_bounty` pays it; that the migration replaced only the live body it was qualified against; and that it refuses to install if any already-settled mystery chest would stop proving.
