# server/src/engine/HorsePlanFingerprintActivation.law.test.ts

The decision-readiness layers merged in #9351 are reached by live play, not only by their unit tests (Dan, 2026-11-05: every tool, skill and ability built must be used in real time on every hand and every street). A planned flop or turn barrel records, through the accepted-wager effect sink, the next-street contender set and stack-to-pot exposure it expects (public information only), and the next street compares what arrived against it: a contender the plan never expected, an exposure more than 65 percent from the plan, or a scare card under pressure withdraws the plan before it fires, while V39 keeps the card read and an explicit off arm keeps the unconditional plan. Every completed hand the mind observes, merges from a sandbox or replays at boot folds into the in-process exploit rows under opportunity-scoped decay, so a rare read is never erased by hands that never offered its spot. A thin exact opponent row borrows its sibling table-size bucket at a weight decayed by the hands the player has played since that bucket was last refreshed on the player's own hand clock, tapering to nothing as the exact row thickens. Each layer leaves its receipt (plan*fingerprint_recorded, plan_fingerprint*\*, opponent_exploit_opportunity_scoped_observed, opponent_memory_donor_fresh / stale / expired) and labels never matter.
