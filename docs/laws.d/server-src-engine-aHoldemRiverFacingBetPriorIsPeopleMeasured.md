# server/src/engine/aHoldemRiverFacingBetPriorIsPeopleMeasured.law.test.ts

With holdemRiverFacingBetPriorIsPeopleMeasured on (default OFF, read `=== true`), a heads-up no-limit hold'em cash fold facing the river's first bet becomes a call only when the call's value clears its sampling error against what such a bet is: equilibrium is the anchor (a bet of s pots bluffs s / (1 + 2 s)), people's measured river first bets by size, line and pot type move it (the table and its fit count bets by people only and shown cards only, in five regions of the bettor's standing with the top fifth cut in three), and the bettor's own public record of betting when no bet is to be faced moves it again and dominates as it grows (a frequent bettor's bets are less often top, a rare bettor's more often), each population step shrunk by 20 pseudo-bets and the record by the measured between-player prior strength. The Horse's share of wins against each region is exact. On real recorded nodes (the deciding Horse's own cards only) the option-off brain folds and the option calls on every seed with its receipt where the price says so, and the fold stands with its receipt where it does not; a truthy non-boolean is off; a tournament copy is never touched; a rare bettor's record turns the call back into a fold and a frequent bettor's keeps it. An applicable decision with the option on costs at most 16 times the same decision off, measured in alternating rounds on the same machine (src/testing/relativeCost.ts).
