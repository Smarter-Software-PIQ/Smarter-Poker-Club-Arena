# server/src/engine/aHoldemRiverFacingBetPriorIsPeopleMeasured.law.test.ts

With holdemRiverFacingBetPriorIsPeopleMeasured on (default OFF, read `=== true`), a heads-up no-limit hold'em cash fold facing the river's first bet becomes a call only when the call's value clears its sampling error against what such a bet is: equilibrium is the anchor (a bet of s pots bluffs s / (1 + 2 s)), people's measured river first bets by size, line and pot type move it (the table and its fit count bets by people only and shown cards only, in six regions of the bettor's standing with the top fifth cut in three and the middle in two), and the bettor's own shown river bets of that line and size (HorseHoldemBetRead: what his called heads-up river first bets turned out to be) move it again and dominate as they grow, each population step shrunk by 20 pseudo-bets and his record weighed once against the measured between-bettor prior strength, never pooled across his sizes or lines; how often he bets is not read. The record is public only: a river first bet enters with the cards its bettor showed at the showdown (a mucked hand as lower middle, never as air), a bet that won without a showdown adds nothing, a multiway river, a tournament, a bomb pot, a second board and every other game add nothing, and a hand is learned once; the live learner, the boot rebuild and the grading harness feed it. The Horse's share of wins against each region is exact. On real recorded nodes (the deciding Horse's own cards only) the option-off brain folds and the option calls on every seed with its receipt where the price says so, and the fold stands with its receipt where it does not; a truthy non-boolean is off; a tournament copy is never touched; a bettor shown with air turns a standing fold into a call and a bettor shown with the nuts keeps the fold; a bet somebody else has already folded to (a bet made into more than one player) is never priced. An applicable decision with the option on costs at most 16 times the same decision off, measured in alternating rounds on the same machine (src/testing/relativeCost.ts).
