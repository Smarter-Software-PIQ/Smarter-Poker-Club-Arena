# tests/a-break-never-starts-in-the-middle-of-a-hand.law.test.ts

Dan 2026-11-06: every scheduled or maintenance break calls the last hand and starts only after every hand has finished. The dealing loop parks a table that just played a hand only after the hand-completion display (winner shown, pot pushed, cards mucked), the hand-free broadcast that clears the felt and the arming of the rest; nothing parks between dealHand() and that broadcast, and a player who busted on that hand is published out and woken for elimination or rebuy before the park, so a break never shows a hand frozen on the felt or a busted player waiting on an empty stack.
