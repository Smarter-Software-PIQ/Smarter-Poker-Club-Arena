# server/src/engine/HorseHoldemMultiwayOpeningIsPriced.law.test.ts

With holdemMultiwayOpeningBluffIsPriced or holdemMultiwayOpeningValueBetIsPriced on (both default OFF, read `=== true`), the first bet of the turn or river against two or more opponents in no-limit hold'em cash is priced: every opponent must fold for a bluff to win and the minimum defence is shared across the field, so with no record the field's joint fold equals the bettor's break-even (each of N opponents folding its N-th root) and a pure bluff with no blockers is worth nothing; one opponent's record moves the joint fold by the N-th root of his ratio, never by a product of over-folds; who folds is read without the Horse's cards and the Horse's cards only move how much of the field folds (blockers); a value size is one where the chips the callers add are won on balance; range advantage over the field is the lowest pairwise advantage. On real audited multiway nodes (the deciding Horse's own cards only) the option-off brain checks, each option alone bets at one size on every seed with its receipt, the other option and the heads-up options do not, a truthy non-boolean is off, a tournament copy and a heads-up copy of the node are never touched, and renaming the opponents or flipping their Horse flag changes nothing.
