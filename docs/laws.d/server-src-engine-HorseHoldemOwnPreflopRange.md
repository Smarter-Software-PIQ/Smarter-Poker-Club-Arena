# server/src/engine/HorseHoldemOwnPreflopRange.law.test.ts

With holdemLateStreetIsPriced or holdemFlopIsPriced on (default OFF), the priced chooser's model of the Horse's own range starts from the Horse's own preflop policy, never from the band its opponents read: off, decide is called once and nothing is primed; on, a heads-up flop decision primes per-class weights in [0, 1] that separate classes (some 0, some 1) and always hold the Horse's own class; every rebuilt preflop frame keeps each seat at its own start stack and carries no other seat's hole cards and no board; the policy asks are invisible (the same decision and the same strategy-stream position as a decision whose range was already primed); renaming every seat and flipping every is_horse gives the same weights; a throwaway ask's mind writes never land in an outer capture. The opponent's own record reaches the chooser for each exploit-probe archetype (synthetic records): an always-bettor's bet share is read wide, a station's fold to a bet rare, a nit's fold frequent and his bets narrow.
