# server/src/engine/HorseRiverBluffOtherGames.law.test.ts

Fixed limit, short deck, bomb pots and multi-board pots each have a river bluff path of their own, and nothing switches it off (Dan, 2026-11-06: "CHECK THAT THERE IS NOTHING THAT IS EVER BLOCKING OR PRENENTING, OR 'SHUTTING OFF' RIVER BLUFFS FOR QUALIFIED CANDITATES OR SITUATIONS"). Real HorseLogic.decide on heads-up rivers checked to hero against an unknown opponent: fixed limit bluffs with exactly the one fixed bet at a low but real rate below the no-limit rate of the same spot, blockers more than junk, a station fewer but never zero, and flo8 with no low possible bluffs the high; short deck reads draws and blockers in the engine's 36-card ranking (A-6-7-8-9 is a straight) and bluffs blocker air but never top pair; bomb pots bluff the barrel and the checked-through turn with no preflop action, and a preflop record alone is never a story; a multi-board pot bluffs only with air on every board, at the single-board share divided by the board count (V36 rule 2), including production's double-board PLO5 bomb pot; and each path is its own opts flag, default on, whose false restores only that exclusion. A red law means a river bluff is again shut off for a qualified candidate in one of these games.
