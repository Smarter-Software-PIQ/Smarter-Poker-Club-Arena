# server/src/engine/HorseOpponentStyle.law.test.ts

The opponent style read (`HorseOpponentStyle.ts`) tells a Horse how an opponent has played in this format, on separate axes (entry width, aggression, betting-range evidence) with confidence, and is bounded and default-off. It reads decision-time counters only, never hole cards or a retrospective hand analysis; an unknown opponent is neutral and explicitly uncertain in every variant and never disarms protective logic; its four-way label is telemetry and no consumer reads it; every consumer option is an explicit-true opt-in; the respect options only ever move a Horse toward folding and the value-extraction and fold-bluff options are bounded multipliers; every option stands down at push/fold depths (the Nash tables decide) and in bounty events, and never touches the Omaha presumption; the maniac cut equals the exploit read's width line; Omaha and short-deck consumers are off; and the tournament utility arbiter keeps the last word after the style consumers run. A red law means the read gained a path it was not allowed: remove the path, do not loosen the law.
