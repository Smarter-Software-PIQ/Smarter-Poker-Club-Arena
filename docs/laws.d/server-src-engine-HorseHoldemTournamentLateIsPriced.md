# server/src/engine/HorseHoldemTournamentLateIsPriced.law.test.ts

With holdemTournamentRiverBluffIsPriced or holdemTournamentTurnBluffIsPriced on (both default OFF, read `=== true`), a heads-up no-limit hold'em tournament decision on that street, not facing a wager, where the brain checked, is priced as the cash opening side's bluff with its losses weighted by the Horse's pay-jump bubble factor: with no pay jump live the EV is the cash chip EV exactly, a larger bubble factor never raises a bet's EV nor grows the best size, a short effective stack caps what the bluff risks, a value hand is never a bluff candidate, the payout price is chip EV far from the money, heads-up and winner-take-all, carries a factor above one on a priced bubble, and stands the option down where a live pay jump cannot be priced (no stacks or identity, no trusted context, a satellite, a PKO, a mystery bounty). On real audited tournament nodes (the deciding Horse's own cards only) the option-off brain checks, the street's option alone bets at one size on every seed with its receipt, the other street's option and a truthy non-boolean do not, a cash copy is never touched, and a copy on an unpriceable bubble, or on a live pay jump of a field past the ICM compute bound (more than 60 stacks), stands down.
