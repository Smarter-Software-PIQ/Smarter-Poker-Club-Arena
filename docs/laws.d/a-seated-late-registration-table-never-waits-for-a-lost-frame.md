# server/src/tournament/aSeatedLateRegistrationTableNeverWaitsForALostFrame.law.test.ts

A late-registration (capacity) table is committed by the database together with seated entrants and a `late_registration` manager wake, and its dealer is started by the direct wake admission the moment the Realtime frame arrives. Realtime is the primary delivery, not a guarantee: postgres_changes frames can be lost or throttled while the channel still reports SUBSCRIBED, and the wake drain only re-reads on boot, reconnect or a failed read. When a frame was lost, the table's dealer was started only by a later full elimination sweep, which on a busy field arrived minutes later. Production 2026-11-06 03:07-03:18 UTC, engine 71087373: every capacity table opened after the 03:00 maintenance break was committed by a generation bump of an already-pending wake and waited for that sweep - Six-Card Feature 716f92a0 304 s with three players seated and no hand, 1817ecac 72 s, 40 Chip Bounty Freezeout 4b7f39d4 164 s - against about 1 s for the 30 capacity tables opened earlier that night. This law pins that the process re-reads its pending `late_registration` wakes on a bounded 5 s cadence and runs exactly the same direct admission a frame would have run: it starts the dealer of a committed capacity table with no frame and no sweep, reads only pending late-registration wakes, keeps every gate of the direct path (ready manager, lifecycle, freeze, stopped server, finished event), is idempotent per receipt, never requests or acknowledges a sweep, shares one admission method with the frame path, and is armed with the wake subscription, unref-ed and cleared on stop.
