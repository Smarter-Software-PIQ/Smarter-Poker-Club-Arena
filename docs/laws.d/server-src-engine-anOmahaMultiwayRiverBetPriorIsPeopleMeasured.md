# server/src/engine/anOmahaMultiwayRiverBetPriorIsPeopleMeasured.law.test.ts

The multiway river people prior (omahaMultiwayRiverBetPriorIsPeopleMeasured) ships dark and, on, prices only a PLO4/PLO5 multiway river first bet outside a re-raised pot: heads-up nothing changes, a first encounter reads no record (the price is the people-only multiway river cell), a record of the bettor's shown river bluffs turns folds into calls and never the reverse while a record of shown value never adds a call, no change is ever a raise, and the bettor being a person or a Horse is no input.
