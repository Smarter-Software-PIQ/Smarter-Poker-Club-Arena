# server/src/engine/HorseOmahaReraisedPositionVsEveryone.law.test.ts

With `omahaReraisedPositionVsEveryoneToAct` on (default OFF), a PLO4/PLO5 cash table call of a re-raise by a Horse in position against the re-raiser, with a player still to act who acts after it on later streets, is priced with a realization weighted by the chance it plays the flop out of position (that player's chance to continue: his own fold-to-3-bet record against the anchored prior; 0.52 / 0.50 for an opener with no record): the big blind behind a small-blind 3-bet with the opener still to act gets the weight and nothing else changes; the weighted realization lies between the two; a button behind a cutoff 3-bet is priced as before; Lightning and tournaments keep their own reading. Off (absent, false, non-boolean) pricing and decisions are main's; on, over 600 generated PLO4/PLO5 hands of the audited shape, a decision only ever moves from call to fold, and the option's cost is held relative to the same decisions off.
