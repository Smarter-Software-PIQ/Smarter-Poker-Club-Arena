# tests/a-union-statement-can-carry-a-charge-note.law.test.ts

A union statement can be corrected upward by a charge note (a debit note, the credit note's twin): every production reader that knows the credit note learns the charge note under its captured md5, every reader that subtracts credit notes also adds charge notes, the issuing function keeps the credit note's caller rule, counter and grants; a delivered union statement can be marked paid because the issued-document guard ignores only the four payment keys on union weekly documents; the client behaviour is pinned by its unit and component tests.
