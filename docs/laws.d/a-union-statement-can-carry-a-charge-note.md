# tests/a-union-statement-can-carry-a-charge-note.law.test.ts

A union statement can be corrected upward by a charge note (a debit note, the credit note's twin): every production reader that knows the credit note learns the charge note under its captured md5, every reader that subtracts credit notes also adds charge notes, the issuing function keeps the credit note's caller rule, counter and grants; the client behaviour is pinned by its unit and component tests.
