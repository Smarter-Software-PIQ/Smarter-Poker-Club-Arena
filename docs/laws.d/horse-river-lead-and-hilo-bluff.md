# server/src/engine/HorseRiverLeadHiLo.law.test.ts

Out-of-position river leads and hi-lo river bluffs are played in Hold'em and PLO, and nothing shuts them off for a qualified candidate (Dan, 2026-11-06: "... CHECK THAT THERE IS NOTHING THAT IS EVER BLOCKING OR PRENENTING, OR 'SHUTTING OFF' RIVER BLUFFS FOR QUALIFIED CANDITATES OR SITUATIONS"). Real HorseLogic.decide, heads-up: first to act on the river into the turn bettor, a river that completed the caller's flush or straight is bluff-led (nut blockers most, junk least, never zero), a blank river is led rarely but not never, a credible station is never bluff-led into, the made draw leads the completing river for value while a blank river keeps the value check, and Omaha on a paired board does not lead (Dan's paired-board rulings). PLO8 with a low possible: the nut low with no high bets the freeroll at a bounded rate priced by the quarter risk, air with no low checks rather than bluff into a likely low (the half-pot giveaway), a non-nut low is showdown value, and the low-chance, nut-low, scoop-eligibility and frequency arithmetic holds. A red law means a river lead or hi-lo bluff is shut off again, or a horse bluffs into a likely low.
