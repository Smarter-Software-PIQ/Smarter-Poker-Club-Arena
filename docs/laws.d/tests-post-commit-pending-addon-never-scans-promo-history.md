# tests/post-commit-pending-addon-never-scans-promo-history.law.test.ts

Incidents `053d3341` (`fn_post_commit_stranded_watchdog`) and `515d6f24` (`LightningHandHost.post_commit_obligations_pending`) on 2026-11-22 stalled cash post-commit settlement for 853.5 s because `fn_ca_promo_apply_pending_cash_funding` (called via `fn_ca_process_hand_post_commit_obligations -> resolve_pending_addon -> fn_cash_record_funding_application`) unconditionally locked promo grants and ran `fn_ca_promo_play_through_settle` + `fn_ca_promo_locked_at` even on zero-promo-custody add-ons, while `sp_ca_promo_profit_hourly_sweep` self-invalidated its own cache signature on grant close and skipped the account for 1 hour. This law pins migration `20261122205903` and the native PG guards so `fn_ca_promo_apply_pending_cash_funding` never calls `settle`, `locked_at`, or `move_custody` and skips grant locks when the pending add-on holds zero promo custody; `fn_ca_promo_locked_at`, `fn_ca_promo_nonwallet_entry`, and `fn_ca_promo_leave_principal` short-circuit on open `ca_promo_principal_custody` before scanning fee history; `sp_ca_promo_profit_hourly_sweep` refreshes its cache lock-free after ACT mutations and prioritizes signature-mismatched caches without waiting out the 1-hour cooldown; and `scripts/ci/test-hot-path-no-history-scans.py` traces every intermediate bridge function across the 4-hop post-commit chain.
