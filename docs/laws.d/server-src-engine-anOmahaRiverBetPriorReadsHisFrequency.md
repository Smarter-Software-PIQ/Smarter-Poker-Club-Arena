# server/src/engine/anOmahaRiverBetPriorReadsHisFrequency.law.test.ts

The river frequency prior (omahaRiverBetPriorReadsHisFrequency) ships dark and, on, reads only a PLO4/PLO5 heads-up river bet (never a raise) in a single-raised or limped pot inside the people-measured facing-bet price: the bettor's own heads-up river first-bet frequency is shrunk to people's measured rate by the measured pseudo-opportunities, a frequency at or below people's adds nothing, the non-value share it implies replaces people's share only as the prior mean of his shown-bet record (a long value-heavy shown record outweighs it), without the frequency input or without the people-measured prior nothing changes, on real river decisions a decision changes only from a fold to a call, and the bettor's identity is no input.
