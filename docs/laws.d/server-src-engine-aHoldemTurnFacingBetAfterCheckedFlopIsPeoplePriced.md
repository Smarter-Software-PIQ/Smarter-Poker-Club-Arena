# server/src/engine/aHoldemTurnFacingBetAfterCheckedFlopIsPeoplePriced.law.test.ts

With holdemTurnFacingBetAfterCheckedFlopIsPeoplePriced on (default OFF, read `=== true`), a heads-up no-limit hold'em cash fold facing the first bet of a turn after a checked-through flop becomes a call only when the call is worth more than the fold with the river played to a plan (HorseHoldemTurnRiverPlan): his turn range by turn region from his own turn stabs, shrunk to people's measured turn bets (hidden bets counted) and to equilibrium; on every river card his barrel is the part of that range whose make-up matches his river bets after his own turn bet (his own shown river bets, then people's, then equilibrium), how often he barrels moved by his own record of barrels after a called stab; the Horse calls a barrel exactly where the river price calls and takes its showdown share when he checks; the turn value is taken with his turn shares one standard error toward his top fifth. With no river betting the value is the showdown share of the pot less the call; a barrel he can make costs the call and a barrel of air that the plan calls is worth more than no river at all; a give-up record is worth more to the call than a barrel record. Facing that bettor's river bet after a called stab the river half prices the bet that came on the card that came, as the plan priced it. The record is public only: a mucked stab is spread over the turn regions of the holdings that lose to the one shown winning hand (lower middle when no winner was shown; the mucked cards are never read), and his river after a called stab is a barrel or a check. On real recorded nodes (the deciding Horse's own cards only) the option-off brain folds, the option calls with its receipt where the plan says so and the fold stands with its receipt where it does not, a truthy non-boolean is off, a tournament copy is never touched, and an all-air stabber turns a standing fold into a call on the turn and on the river after a call, read only with the mind on. An applicable decision with the option on costs at most 40 times the same decision off, measured in alternating rounds on the same machine (src/testing/relativeCost.ts).
