# server/src/engine/HorseCashAction.law.test.ts

Dan 2026-11-09, after a real player reported for the fourth time that the cash horses play far too tight: "FOR A 9 HANDED GAME, THESE NUMBERS NEED TO BE INCREASED TO 25-30% VPIP, AND INCREASE THE 3 BETTING ... WHILE MAINTAINING GTO DISCIPLINE ... REAL CASH GAME OPENS ARE 2.5-4.5 BB OR LARGER IF THERE ARE LIMPERS", then "FULLY ADJUSTED FOR 6 HANDED GAMES AND SHORT HANDED PLAY AS WELL". A full table of no-limit cash horses at production depth plays 23-30% VPIP nine-handed (15.9% before) with a 5.5%+ 3-bet and real squeezes, the seats behind an open no longer fold 94%, seven- and eight-handed tables are not left on the tight ranges, six-handed plays 29-36%, every seat removed loosens the table down to heads-up, and the big blind never folds more when a caller improves its price. Cash opens are 2.5-4.5bb plus a big blind per limper, drawn from the spot and never the cards. The discipline that stays: each seat opens the top of its own order and wider than the seat before it, an early open and a bigger open are defended narrower, added 3-bets are value first, and at 50bb or less every range is exactly the prior one.
