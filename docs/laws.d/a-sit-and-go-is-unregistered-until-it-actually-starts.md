A Sit and Go is unregistered until it actually starts (Dan 2026-11-22: "PLAYERS NEED TO BE ABLE TO 'UNREGISTER' FROM A SIT N GO, HEADS UP OR SPIN IF IT HASN'T STARTED YET"). A six or nine seat Sit and Go satellite still REGISTERING at 1 of 9 answered "This Tournament Has Started" because fn_ca_unregister_tournament_player_exact gave the actual-start rule only to Spins and two seat Sit and Gos and judged every other event by `clock_timestamp()>=start_time`, while fn_ensure_fixed_sng_satellite sets start_time two minutes ahead as a fill window. Migration 20261122192807 gives every `sng-v1` event the `sit_and_go_actual_start` authority in the chip core, the receipt reader and the Diamond door: refused only once status has left REGISTERING, started_at is set, a hand is persisted or a launch receipt exists (written under the same tournaments row lock, so a launch and an unregistration cannot both win). The refund path is unchanged and nothing reads is_horse. Scheduled events, Spins, Heads-Up and the seat-first satellite keep their rule.
