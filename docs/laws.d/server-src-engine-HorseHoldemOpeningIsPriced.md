# server/src/engine/HorseHoldemOpeningIsPriced.law.test.ts

With holdemOpeningValueBetIsPriced or holdemOpeningBluffIsPriced on (both default OFF, read `=== true`), a heads-up no-limit hold'em cash decision not facing a wager is priced: his fold comes from his own record shrunk to the equilibrium anchor (no record: his fold equals the bettor's break-even, so a pure bluff with no blockers is worth nothing), who folds is read without the Horse's cards and the Horse's cards only move how much folds (blockers), a value bet needs his calling part to lose chips to it, and range advantage is a symmetric probability. On real audited nodes (the deciding Horse's own cards only) the option-off brain checks, each option alone bets at one size on every seed with its receipt, the other option does not, a truthy non-boolean is off, and a tournament copy of the node is never touched.
