# server/src/engine/anOmahaFlopCallTreeIsFast.law.test.ts

The PLO4/PLO5 flop call tree (omahaFlopRealizationIsPriced, default OFF) scores hands with HorseOmahaFastScore, which orders random PLO4 and PLO5 hands on flops, turns and rivers exactly as HorseEval.scoreOmahaHiPartial (ties included; a river score built from the turn score equals the full one). The committed fixture (32 real 4+-way flop decisions, public snapshot and the deciding Horse's own cards only, and 16 heads-up league decisions) gives the recorded EV and call exactly. Over the multiway fixture, after a warm-up pass, each decision's best of three runs has p95 at most 25 ms and max at most 60 ms on the owner's Mac; on another machine both bounds scale by the measured cost of a fixed scoring workload against its cost on that Mac, never below 1.
