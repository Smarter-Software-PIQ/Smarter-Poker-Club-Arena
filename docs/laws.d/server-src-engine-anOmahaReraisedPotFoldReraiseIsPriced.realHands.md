# server/src/engine/anOmahaReraisedPotFoldReraiseIsPriced.realHands.law.test.ts

On eleven real PLO5 and PLO4 cash nodes where a Horse opened and folded to a person's 3-bet out of position (no user id, the deciding Horse's own cards only), omahaReraisedPotFoldReraiseIsPriced (default OFF) turns a standing fold into the pot re-raise at the largest legal raise-to or leaves it a fold, never a call; fewer are re-raised against a measured tight 3-bettor than a measured wide one; off, every decision is the one it was.
