# server/src/engine/anOmahaRiverBettorRangeIsTextureMeasured.law.test.ts

omahaRiverBettorRangeIsTextureMeasured prices a heads-up PLO4/PLO5 cash river bettor on people's measured weights on five absolute hand classes of the board by texture. The law holds that the generated population is people only (the fit and its export keep the filters: a person's bet, cards he showed with mucked = false, no Horse bet, no HUD population row); that the texture reads when the flush came; that the Horse's blockers are counted by the same formula (the same straight wins a larger share with the nut-flush card in the hand); that every answer is a price (the same hand calls a small price and folds a large one); that the weights are a distribution leaning on pooled people where a cell is thin, with the bettor's own record moving the classes under the key category; and that a bet under 0.4 pot, a six-card hand and a board that is not a river are never priced by it.
