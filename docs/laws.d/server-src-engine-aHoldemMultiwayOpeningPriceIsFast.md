# server/src/engine/aHoldemMultiwayOpeningPriceIsFast.law.test.ts

The multiway opening bet price stays cheap where it applies, measured relative to other work on the same machine: the real audited multiway nodes decided with the node's option on cost under 12 times the same decisions with it off (about 2 now), and the pricing alone against the widest field it takes (six opponents with whole ranges on the turn) costs under 8 times the same pricing against two (about 3 now), because the river cards compared on the turn are held to a fixed score budget.
