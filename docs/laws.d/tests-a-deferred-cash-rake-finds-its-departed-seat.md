# tests/a-deferred-cash-rake-finds-its-departed-seat.law.test.ts

#11256 (2026-11-18) moved cash rake out of the hand's own commit into a stored obligation that a later continuation banks, but `fn_cash_earning_club` still read the earning club only from a live `table_seats` row covering the hand's start. That row was always present while rake banked inside the commit. Now a participant can leave and have their chair reseated first: hand 59989058's horse left two seconds after the hand, the must-move balancer reseated its chair two seconds later, and the obligation failed `cash_earning_seat_provenance_missing_or_ambiguous` on every following hand until `fn_post_commit_stranded_watchdog` paged. This law pins the fix. When no live chair row covers the start, the earning club is the club of the participant's first exit at that table at or after the start, read from the immutable `ca_seat_stack_exits`. A live chair still wins, the union branch still refuses a missing or foreign club, and no later migration may redefine the reader without this fallback.
