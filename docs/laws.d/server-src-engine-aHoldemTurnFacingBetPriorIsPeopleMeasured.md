# server/src/engine/aHoldemTurnFacingBetPriorIsPeopleMeasured.law.test.ts

With holdemTurnFacingBetPriorIsPeopleMeasured on (default OFF, read `=== true`), a heads-up no-limit hold'em cash fold facing the turn's first bet after a flop nobody bet, in a limped or single-raised pot, becomes a call only when the call's value clears its sampling error against what such a bet is: equilibrium is the anchor, people's measured first bets of a heads-up turn on that line by size and pot type move it (bets by people only; shown cards, plus the hidden bets the public record marks as not value: mucked at the showdown as lower middle, given up on the river as bottom; the regions are where the hand stood on the turn), and the bettor's own turn stabs of that size (HorseHoldemBetRead) move it again and dominate as they grow, weighed once against the measured between-bettor prior strength. The record is public only: a turn stab enters with the cards its bettor showed, as lower middle when he mucked at the showdown, as bottom when nobody raised his bet and he checked the river and folded; a bet that was raised and folded, a bet that won without a showdown, a turn after a flop somebody bet and a multiway turn add nothing. The Horse's equity against each region is exact over every river card and is discounted by the engine's realization of a turn call unless the call ends the betting. On real recorded nodes (the deciding Horse's own cards only) the option-off brain folds and the option calls on every seed with its receipt where the price says so, and the fold stands with its receipt where it does not; a truthy non-boolean is off; a tournament copy is never touched; the river option does not touch a turn decision; a bettor shown stabbing with air turns a standing fold into a call and one shown with the nuts keeps it; a bet somebody else has already folded to is never priced. An applicable decision with the option on costs at most 24 times the same decision off, measured in alternating rounds on the same machine (src/testing/relativeCost.ts).
