# server/src/engine/HorseHeadsUpSmallBlindStealsVsTightBigBlind.law.test.ts

The default-off huSmallBlindStealsVsTightBigBlind option (heads-up small blind first in against a big blind the existing decayed counters prove a tight folder) only ever turns a fold or a limp into an open, opens every hand only when the big blind's estimated fold share (1 - 2 x posterior mean VPIP; an estimate, not a strict bound) clears the largest heads-up open's break-even on folds alone, pins the exact-head review (0039d2bc, N5) fold-share estimate's three assumptions (Beta posterior mean VPIP rather than a 90% credible upper bound or raw sample VPIP, fixed 2x VPIP assuming half of counted hands are big blinds with no seat split, and walks omitted plus checked limped pots counted as hands +1 with vpip +0 without separation), keeps the size of every hand that already opened, acts only at 20-150bb in no-limit hold'em with exactly two dealt in (cash, or a tournament whose trusted context says two players are left and that is not a satellite), leaves unknown, sparse, sound and tight-looking-but-defending big blinds alone, and changes no decision when off (2026-11-15, heads-up lane round 2, D6; fold-share assumption cases added 2026-11-21).
