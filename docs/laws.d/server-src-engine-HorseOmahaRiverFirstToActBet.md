# server/src/engine/HorseOmahaRiverFirstToActBet.law.test.ts

With omahaRiverFirstToActBetIsPriced on (default OFF), in PLO4/PLO5 cash (table and Lightning, one board, high only, no bomb pot), on the river heads-up with nothing to call and the opponent still to act, a Horse's final check becomes a bet only where the bet priced on the measured response curve beats the check by more than its standard error: nothing but a check changes, every bet is legal under pot limit (between the minimum bet and the pot, or all in) and admitted by the response validator, the current nuts are never folded or checked into a fold, no identity is read, a measured calling station is not bluffed, and it is out of scope on the flop and turn, multiway, facing a wager, after the opponent acted, in PLO6, PLO8, tournaments, bomb pots and multi-board hands; off, decisions are byte-identical.
