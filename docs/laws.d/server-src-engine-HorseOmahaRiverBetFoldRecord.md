# server/src/engine/HorseOmahaRiverBetFoldRecord.law.test.ts

With omahaRiverBetFoldIsHisRecord on (default OFF), the two river bet rules (omahaRiverFirstToActBetIsPriced, omahaRiverCheckedToBetIsMeasured; PLO4/PLO5 cash, river, heads-up) price the opponent's answer with the population curve whose fold share is multiplied by his own lifetime public fold record at a river's first wager over the population's, shrunk n / (n + k) with the measured k. No record, no provider, another game or a failing reader is the population curve itself; one answer on record moves the price by under two points (no cliff); fewer folds on record means more continues at every size with the raise share of his continues kept; an opponent on record as not folding the river is not bluffed; only the rules' own check into a bet can change; off, on without a record, and on without the river rules decide as today; PLO6 is untouched and no identity is read.
