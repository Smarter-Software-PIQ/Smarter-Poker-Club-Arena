# server/src/engine/anOmahaRiverRecordCountsMuckedBets.law.test.ts

The river record's mucked bets (omahaRiverRecordCountsMuckedBets, default OFF): the read applies only to a PLO4 / PLO5 river bet outside a re-raised pot, never to a raise, the turn or the flop; a river bet that was called and mucked at the showdown is hidden, while one won uncontested or folded to a raise is not; the bettor's river record counts his mucked called bets as bets that were not value only when the option asks; in the price they raise his non-core share above a stranger's and leave the people cell untouched; and on committed real heads-up river facing-bet states the engine changes nothing while his record holds no mucked bet, names its receipt only when it does, and only ever moves a fold to a call.
