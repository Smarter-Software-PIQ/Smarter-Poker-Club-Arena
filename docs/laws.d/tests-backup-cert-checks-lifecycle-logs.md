# tests/backup-cert-checks-lifecycle-logs.law.test.ts

Backup Engine certification (`server/standby/standby-engine.sh` `cmd_certify`) must fail closed on candidate lifecycle crashes and unintended between-hand delays during soak before `backup-engine/certified` is granted (incident 075013fd, Wave 2 Hardening). `check_engine_lifecycle_logs` always queries `public.engine_lifecycle_logs` (when the table exists on the Backup copy) across the candidate's boot and soak window, prints the exact error and stack of any `fatal_error`, `shutdown_failed`, `shutdown_deadline`, unexpected `leadership_shutdown`, or `level = 'fatal'` row to stderr, and fails closed (`lifecycle_fatal_events:N` or `lifecycle_logs_unreadable`). `check_soak_hand_gap_delay` queries `fn_ca_hand_gap_unintended_delay` over the steady-state soak window (when installed) and fails closed (`hand_gap_unintended_delay_exceeded` or `hand_gap_check_unreadable`) if any graded shape with at least 5 graded hands exceeds `budget_ms` (250 ms) at p95. Both structured check summaries (`lifecycleLogCheck`, `handGapCheck`) are written to `certificate.json`.
