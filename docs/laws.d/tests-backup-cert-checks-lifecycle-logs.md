# tests/backup-cert-checks-lifecycle-logs.law.test.ts

Backup Engine certification (`server/standby/standby-engine.sh` `cmd_certify`) must fail closed on candidate lifecycle crashes and unintended between-hand delays during soak before `backup-engine/certified` is granted (incident 075013fd, Wave 2 Hardening). `check_engine_lifecycle_logs` always queries `public.engine_lifecycle_logs` (when the table exists on the Backup copy) across the candidate's boot and soak window, prints the exact error and stack of any `fatal_error`, `shutdown_failed`, `shutdown_deadline`, unexpected `leadership_shutdown`, or `level = 'fatal'` row to stderr, and fails closed (`lifecycle_fatal_events:N` or `lifecycle_logs_unreadable`). `check_soak_hand_gap_delay` queries `fn_ca_hand_gap_unintended_delay` over the steady-state soak window (when installed) and fails closed (`hand_gap_unintended_delay_exceeded` or `hand_gap_check_unreadable`) on a sample judge, not on a percentile that a small cell's slowest hand decides (2026-11-24): any single gap more than 5,000 ms over what it owed fails at any sample size; a cell of at least 10 graded hands fails when its count over the 250 ms budget is improbable at a 5 % allowed share (one-sided exact binomial, 95 % confidence); cells under 10 hands are pooled per kind and tested the same way; each verdict names the cell, n, the rule and the slowest hands' ids (the grader's worst_hands, when the copy's grader has it). Both structured check summaries (`lifecycleLogCheck`, `handGapCheck`) are written to `certificate.json`.
