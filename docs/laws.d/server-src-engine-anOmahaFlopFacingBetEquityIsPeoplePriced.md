# server/src/engine/anOmahaFlopFacingBetEquityIsPeoplePriced.law.test.ts

omahaFlopFacingBetEquityIsPeoplePriced (default OFF) makes the heads-up PLO4/PLO5 cash flop call-or-fold check, where the priced flop pressure cap prices the call, read the Horse's equity against the bettor's people-priced flop range (his own record, then people's measured flop bets of this size, then the balanced share behind a thin cell) instead of min(sampler equity, that price), in single-raised pots with the bet made heads-up only. On the audited heads-up flop decisions the law holds: it lifts only single-raised pots with the bet made heads-up and only where the priced flop cap priced; limped and re-raised pots are identical; a decision it does not lift is identical; every change is a fold that becomes a call and never a raise; off, explicitly false, or without the priced flop cap it decides nothing; the scope excludes tournaments, other hole-card counts, multiway decisions and bets made into three or more players; the check equity is the people price in scope and the lower of the two otherwise; the code reads no identity.
