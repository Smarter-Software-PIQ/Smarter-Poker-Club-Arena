# server/src/tournament/aMoveTakesAChairThatIsFreeWhenItLands.law.test.ts

The table balancer plans every destination chair from one snapshot and then moves players one at a time, while late registration keeps seating new entrants into the same new tables at the lowest free chair. On 2026-11-25 every burst of "tournament move destination seat is occupied" lined up with freshly created tables being filled by late registrants. The law pins that the engine reads the destination when the move is sent: a planned chair that is still free is kept, a taken one is replaced by a free chair chosen by the planner's own blind-position rule (`chooseOpenSeat`), a destination that filled up waits for the next balance pass without an error, a failed read keeps the planned chair so the database door still decides, and one definite "occupied" refusal (a clean rollback) gets one fresh read and one new request id. A second refusal of a chair the fresh read called free is still reported, and no other refusal is retried.
