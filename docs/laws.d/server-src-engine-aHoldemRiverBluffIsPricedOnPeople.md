On real audited nodes (the deciding Horse's own cards only) the option-off brain checks and the option bets one size on every seed with its receipt where the audit found a missed river bluff against a person; a truthy non-boolean is off; a tournament copy is never touched; a calling station's own river record turns the bet back into a check and an over-folder's keeps it; a bet that neither blockers nor the range advantage support, and a river check the audit judged correct, stay checks. The provider the decision worker installs serves his NLH heads-up river cells by size from the installed NLH packet, and no packet, another game's packet or another player is no record. An applicable decision with the option on costs at most 16 times the same decision off, measured in alternating rounds on the same machine (src/testing/relativeCost.ts).
