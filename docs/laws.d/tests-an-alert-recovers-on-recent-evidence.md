# tests/an-alert-recovers-on-recent-evidence.law.test.ts

On 2026-11-19 two alerts that look back over days kept paging after their fixes were live. `fn_ca_cron_failure_watch`'s majority rule (24 h lookback) re-raised drift incident c44da3ce for `sp-daily-metrics-snapshot` on every tick although the job had succeeded every run since 11:45 UTC, and `StatsAllInEquityCoverageLow` read only the 7-day all-in equity coverage (96.9%) while the equity writer had been fixed since the 10:00 UTC cutover. This law pins the repairs: migration 20261119173000 treats a job whose newest finished runs are a clean streak of at least three, longer than any clean stretch it had between failures that day, as recovered (the three-in-a-row and streak rules are untouched, so a still-failing or flapping job still pages); migration 20261119173100 records the same coverage over the last 24 hours on every witness audit run and publishes it as `evCoverage24h`, and both the engine alert and the Prometheus rule keep the 7-day bar and sample floor but stand down when the last 24 hours are back over 99% on at least 50 seats (proven by `tests/monitoring/stats-equity-coverage-recovers.test.yml`).
