# tests/charles-rule-countdown-resumes.law.test.tsx

The Charles Rule countdown resumes where it left off (owner 2026-11-13): losing the profit pauses the count and keeps the hands played, the hands added by doubles, the doubles and the doubling reference; back in profit it picks up at the same number and the returning hand is not counted. The player is told when it pauses and resumes and sees the paused count beside the seat and between Lightning hands, and Club Data lists paused players apart from players who owe hands.
