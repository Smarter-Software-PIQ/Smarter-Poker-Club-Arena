# tests/a-shorter-soak-judges-memory-growth.law.test.ts

The Backup soak is 900 s (workflow call, `standby-engine.sh` defaults and the selector floor agree) only because memory growth across it is a judged gate: `judge_soak_memory_growth` samples the engine RSS (`/health` `memory.rssMb`, else `docker stats`) every loop pass and refuses a build whose median pairwise slope is over 141 MiB per minute across the whole soak and over half of that in each half (`memory_growth_exceeded`); 141 is (26,624 MiB container limit minus the 1,243 MiB largest measured healthy start) / 180 minutes. It passes all 73 real certificates measured 2026-11-21 to 2026-11-26 even with their whole 30-minute growth squeezed into the soak, ignores an end-of-soak spike, a burst, a sawtooth and a one-time step, reports too few readable samples as `memory_growth_unreadable` (never a pass), writes the full `memoryGrowthCheck` into the certificate, and the selector refuses a soak under 1800 s without that check at `pass` while still accepting an earlier 1800 s certificate that has no such key.
