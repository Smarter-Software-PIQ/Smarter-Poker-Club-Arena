# server/src/engine/AHoldemTournamentAllInCallIsPriced.law.test.ts

With holdemTournamentAllInCallIsPriced on (default OFF), every all-in call decision of a two-card no-limit hold'em tournament hand (preflop at 25 big blinds or less or by a committed stack, any seat, lone jam, re-jam, jam over the Horse's own open, multiway, with callers; postflop only facing a jam where the call closes the action; a river bet that is not all in is not priced) is decided by the Horse's equity against the ranges in the pot versus the exact price (dead money, side pots, the bubble factor where a pay jump is live); the Phase 7 arbiter and Phase 8 may not turn it around beyond 5 equity points (any positive margin for a committed call); the audit E fixtures fold without the option and call with it; with the option absent or false the decision and the generator state are unchanged.
