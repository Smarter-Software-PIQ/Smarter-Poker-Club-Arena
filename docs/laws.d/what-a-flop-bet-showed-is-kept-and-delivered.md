# server/src/engine/horseDecision/whatAFlopBetShowedIsKeptAndDelivered.law.test.ts

What an opponent showed after betting the flop, what calling his flop bets returned, and the price behind his fold-to-raise cells are kept durably, from public information only, and reach the decision on the paths live decisions take (2026-11-19). MEASUREMENT ONLY: no decision reads them. The accepted-hand database learner keeps two records beside the exact opponent profile (migration 20261119192551): `horse_exact_profiles.flop_shown` (shown first flop bets by line, flop texture and the hand's class on the flop; calls of those bets by the caller's kind, class and position with their price and their return; integers, each cell aged by its own evidence; forward only) and `horse_exact_profiles.street_counters_price` (per street, the sum of the prices of the raises he faced after betting or raising, and how many had a price). A hand is read only when the hand's public record shows it: the revealed holdings name the player and the showdown lists him as not mucked; a folded or mucked hand is never read, and the learner is never passed `hand_history.players`. An Omaha decision carries detached copies in its own state and its read frame (`horse-decision-reads-v20`); `HorseMind.omahaFlopShownOf` and `HorseMind.omahaFoldToRaisePriceOf` answer them and nothing calls either. Unknown stays unknown: no record, a null record, a column not yet installed and a malformed record all read null and leave the frame byte for byte what it was; a Hold'em decision carries nothing; cash and tournament stay apart. Companions: `HorseOmahaFlopShown.test.ts` (the hand class held to the evaluator and to the flop verdict's own class), `HorseOmahaStreetReadPrice.test.ts`, `HorseOmahaFlopShownChangesNoDecision.test.ts` (recorded flop, turn and river decisions byte-identical with the columns empty or full), `aFlopShownRecordThroughTheWorker.test.ts` (FAST, DEEP, exact replay) and `scripts/ci/test-horse-exact-public-memory.py` (the SQL reducers equal to the engine's on `scripts/ci/fixtures/horse-flop-shown-vectors.json`).
