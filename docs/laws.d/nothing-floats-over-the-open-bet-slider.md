# tests/nothing-floats-over-the-open-bet-slider.law.test.ts

Dan 2026-11-09, two placement defects from a live Lightning table. The time-bank tile painted on the 33% preset while the bet slider was open, even though `.ca-raising` was working on the chat bubble and the previous-hand card beside it, because that tile carries `position: relative` and a `backdrop-filter` and is therefore a stacking context on a compositor that need not honour an inherited `visibility`. The law pins the structural answer: `ActionPanel` reports its raise mode up through `onRaiseModeChange` (and clears it on unmount), `TablePage` unmounts the whole bottom-left corner while the overlay is open, and the `.ca-raising` block stays beside it. It also pins that the Lightning "Leave Next Hand" control is in the upper-left stack rather than TableHUD's `centerTop` (which sits on the top villain), that it is NOT gated on `embeddedTableId` - every Lightning table is embedded, so that gate would delete it - and that the stack wrapper is pointer-transparent so a 180px control cannot become an invisible slab over the seats.
