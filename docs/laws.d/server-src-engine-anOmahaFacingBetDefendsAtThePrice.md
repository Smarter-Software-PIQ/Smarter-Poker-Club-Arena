# server/src/engine/anOmahaFacingBetDefendsAtThePrice.law.test.ts

With the corrected PLO4/PLO5 cash facing-bet price on (omahaFacingBetRangeIsMeasuredByLine, omahaFacingBetIndifferenceCalls, omahaFacingBetMinimumDefencePrior, omahaFlopRealizationIsPriced, omahaFacingBetFieldIsJoint, omahaMultiwayCallNeedsNutShare; all default OFF) and a bettor with no record, the Horse's whole heads-up range over the committed league state set (HorseOmahaFacingBetPriced.states.ndjson.gz; the heads-up flop 0.4-0.7 pot cells hold every such state of the league capture, about 600 per game) folds to a first flop, turn or river wager of size s under 0.7 pot no more than the mean of s / (1 + s) + 0.05 in every game, street and size bucket. Today's verdicts (omahaFacingBetEv, omahaFlopFacingBetEv) break it on the flop and the river at mid sizes. A bettor whose measured record is value on 59 of 60 shown bets may be folded to more, and only then. Turn bets of 0.7 pot and over (outside the bound: the measured range there is value-heavy) are folded to less, and never more, when the bettor's own record is bluff-heavy (8 value of 40 shown).
