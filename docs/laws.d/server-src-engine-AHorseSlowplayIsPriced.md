# server/src/engine/AHorseSlowplayIsPriced.law.test.ts

The dark zero-migration option horseSlowplayIsPriced replaces the unowned H4 monster slowplay mix with deterministic EV pricing in non-Omaha / non-Fixed-Limit flop and turn spots, stays 100% byte-identical when OFF or in Omaha / Fixed-Limit (including exact fastRandom state), fires priced check and bet receipts, and stays strictly in OPTION_SWITCH_REFUSED. Every pricing input is measured, and no free-card constant remains. Since 2026-11-28 the pricing covers every street left (three on the flop, two on the turn) and not the current street alone: one street priced is the closed form, and the later streets change the verdict (a check against an opponent who folds too much, a bigger lead for the bet against one who does not fold). Its inputs are measured from real people and pinned with their provenance: how often a person bets into an unbet pot by street, by who bet last on the street before and by lead or checked to; the size of that bet as quantiles, each capped by the stack before the mean; how often and how big a person raises. Equity is conserved over every opponent choice, the called branch uses the equity against the continuing range with the measured NLH continuation sharpness, and where nothing is measured the pricing leans to the bet: a wet board is never checked, and an opponent's own record never raises the later streets' betting above the people's rate. The call site reads who bet last from the public action history, the pricer is reachable only behind the option read `=== true`, and one verdict visits a bounded number of tree nodes.
