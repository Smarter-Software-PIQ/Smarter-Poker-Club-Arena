Suited and double-suited aces still continue there, aces facing a 3-bet heads-up in or out of position are called or re-raised as priced and never folded, a habitual 3-bettor's own record raises the price of the same hands and lowers the fold count, both options absent equal both false byte for byte, a tournament is outside the spot, and the receipt validator admits only the exact call and the largest legal raise-to.
