# tests/a-priced-options-prior-is-anchored.law.test.ts

A priced option's prior is anchored. The authority doc of every priced option (an option name containing "Priced", or a doc that declares a "Fitted population table:") carries a "## Prior anchor" section with four labelled lines: "Baseline:" (the equilibrium or break-even rate the no-record prior is anchored to), "Population weight:" (how much weight a measured population rate may carry, reduced when the population is itself on the exploitable side of that baseline), "Own record:" (an opponent's own record dominates once it has a real sample) and "No unshown cards:" (no unshown hole card was read to fit it). The 48 priced options whose doc existed on 2026-11-24 are grandfathered by name in docs/horse-option-prior-anchor-grandfathered.json, a closed list the law prints as a warning on every run; a name leaves it when its doc gains the complete section. Standing requirement of 2026-11-24, after three builds overshot by fitting their prior to a mis-playing Horse field (holdemValueAndCallsArePriced, rolled back audit #191; omahaFlopFacingBetEv, off audit #192).
