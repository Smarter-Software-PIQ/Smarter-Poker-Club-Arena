# server/src/engine/HorseOmahaMultiwayFieldDrawsAreIndependent.law.test.ts

With omahaMultiwayFieldDrawsAreIndependent on (default OFF; PLO4/PLO5 cash), the holding the two joint multiway facing-bet samplers draw for every other player (a caller, an earlier all-in, a player still to act) is independent of the bettor's sampled holding: in every generated flop, turn and river decision (8 per street, one caller and one behind or two behind) the table of the bettor's position in his value region against each field player's position in his range shows no dependence (chi-square under df + 6 sd; 10 x 10 on the flop, 5 x 5 on the turn and river), and the rate a player behind holds the stronger half of his range is within 4.5 SE of his overall rate in every quintile of the bettor's strength. The walk of today fails the same check on all but at most one decision of every street (the law has teeth). Heads-up counts are the same numbers on and off, a decision on is deterministic, and every role and slot has its own sub-seed.
