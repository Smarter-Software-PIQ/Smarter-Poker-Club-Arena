# Omaha Rule Precedence (PLO4 / PLO5 Cash)

The one place that states the order the Omaha decision rules act in, and which of them price their decision. Source of truth for the code: `server/src/engine/HorseLogic.ts` (the `wager_rules` node of the policy graph and the same chain outside the graph). Source of truth for the classes: `server/src/engine/HorseOmahaRuleSites.ts`, read by the law test `server/src/engine/HorseOmahaNoBlanketRule.law.test.ts`.

Owner order, 2026-11-17 11:49 America/Chicago, verbatim: "there should not be any blanket rules, you need to coordinate and re write any and all "blanket rules" to be fully adaptable to real time decisions, a blanket rule shouldn't be allowed to effect a decision, they must all be done by math, gto, players hand or preceived range of hands as well as the opponents range of hand, equity, EV etc." Earlier the same day: the size of the bet must not make or break a decision.

## What "priced" means here

A rule is priced when its answer is a comparison of the candidate actions (fold, check, call, bet at candidate sizes, raise at candidate sizes, bluffs included where legal) on:

- the Horse's exact hand, against the opponent's range for that action, line and board, counted or sampled with the Horse's own cards, the board and known dead cards removed;
- the opponent's measured rates where his own play supplies them, each a posterior mean that shrinks toward the population figure with few (decayed) opportunities, and the stated population prior where it does not;
- the price (pot odds, chips left for later streets, players still to act, position).

A bet's share of the pot enters only as an input to the opponent's range and to the price. It never switches a rule on.

## The order, innermost to outermost

A later step sees the earlier answer and can replace it. "Priced option" names the default-OFF option that, when ON, makes the step's categorical rule stand aside in PLO4/PLO5 cash (one board, high only, no bomb pot, not Lightning). With the option OFF the step acts exactly as before. The order below is the `wager_rules` node of `HorseLogic.ts` as it runs (checked against the source 2026-11-19, PLO Strategy lane 013U37, independent verifier finding D8).

## With the priced options on

- Step 21 is the priced comparison of fold, call and a raise to each legal size on the exact hand. It is the outermost answer on the turn and river facing a wager: a raise is made when it is worth more than the best other action by the margin (any hand), a raise that is not becomes the priced call or fold, and steps 2 and 21's nut rules do not decide. A nut-class hand keeps the raise or the call it was given.
- Step 13's bar is the sampled comparison of the raise and the call; the big-draw wager is priced out of position and without the combo-draw class.
- `omahaSampledRaiseIsPricedAtEverySize` (default OFF, an input of steps 13 and 1): the sampled comparison prices the fold, the call and a raise to each legal size, each raise's fold share at the price it lays each opponent and its called share against the range that continues at that price, and the draw raise and the pressure raise are made to the size of highest value. Off: one size, its fold share size-blind.
- Step 6a's out-of-position turn check and paired-board checks are the comparison of the bet and the check on the card-impact model against every opponent, whatever his label; the card-impact press stands and is priced again at step 14. Step 5 and the blocker-bluff bar are the same comparison. The turn bet the Horse then FACES after that check is not taken over: the out-of-position rule's facing half (call with nut outs at the price, fold with none) still decides, at step 6a, in `omahaFacingBetEvOf` and again at step 14 (`OMAHA_OPEN_RULE_SITES`; reason below).
- Step 7's cells do not decide: where one would change a call or raise, the exact count judges it.
- Step 9's underpair fold stands only when the multiway call is priced below it.
- Step 1's pressure play is withheld or made by its price.
- Step 12 reads the bet's size continuously, with the bluff prior measured against what the Horse actually won (`OMAHA_PRICED_MEASURED_BLUFF`), and takes the bettor's bluff share from his measured bet rate.
- `omahaPricedMix`: inside a priced rule that is on, two actions closer than the comparison's own uncertainty are mixed by the gap, on a draw from public state only.

## The opponent read

Every priced comparison takes the opponent read through one function, `omahaPricedReadOfUser` (`HorseOmahaPricedWiring.ts`), which returns an `OmahaPricedRead` (`HorseOmahaPricedRules.ts`). Today it is built from the pooled read (`HorseOmahaReadConditional`), without its labels. The per-street, per-texture read (`HorseOmahaStreetRead`) is delivered through `omahaPricedSetStreetRatesSource`: a known cell replaces the pooled figure after shrinking toward it by its own opportunity count; an unknown cell or no source leaves the pooled read. With no read of an opponent, the card-impact comparisons take a person's measured bet rate when checked to by street (`OMAHA_PRICED_PERSON_BET_CHECKED_TO`); the read's rates are posterior means of decayed counters, so a thin or stale read moves a comparison a little and a deep fresh one fully, with no sample threshold.

## Categorical rules not yet replaced

One part of a replaced rule still decides with the options on, listed in `OMAHA_OPEN_RULE_SITES` and pinned by the law test: the out-of-position turn rule's facing half (audit row 10). Its priced replacement is the facing-bet EV verdict, which in the counterfactual replay of the audited PLO5 hands folded strong draws and a set class to turn bets at prices their equity beats (4 of 4 judged changes wrong); that verdict's field factor and turn realization are the WooWoo audit's RC-02b, held by the PLO Strategy pickup (01BNteGY). It moves to the priced side when that is fixed.

Listed in `OMAHA_OPTION_CLASSES` as `open`, with the build that holds each. The law test pins the list so it can only change on purpose: the flop paired-board fold (`ploPairedBoardFold`; it stops deciding the flop fold or call only while `omahaFlopFacingBetEv`, default OFF, is on), the re-raised-pot flop continue, the one-pair cells (off-only), the river bluff into a value leader (off-only), and `omahaBigBetIsPotShare` (below).

`OMAHA_OPEN_RULE_SITES` also names, besides site 10 above: S1b, the bare-overpair flop continuation facing the bet after the check (the bet half is priced by `omahaFlopBetIsPriced`); S6b, the river nut-blocker bluff's offer gates; and site 14, the flop 20 big blind mark. The mark is the reference brain's own big-bet mark in `HorseMind.ts` (a newest-street wager of 20 big blinds or more sets `OppPostflopRead.bigBet`; receipts `range_read_big_bet_counted`, `v16_sizecond_bigbet`). On the flop it still feeds the reference fold or call; only `omahaFlopFacingBetEv` (default OFF) takes that fold or call away from it. Owner: PLO Strategy lane 013U37.

`omahaBigBetIsPotShare` is classed `open`, not `priced` (verifier finding D10): it replaces the 20 big blind mark on the turn and river with another size line (a wager under 70% of the pot it went into is tiered by its pot share), and it gates the limped-pot minimum bet (step 6e). Neither is a priced comparison. With `omahaSmallBetIsPriced` on, the minimum bet acts on a bet that option placed only through its priced size comparison.

## Adding or changing an Omaha rule

1. Register the option in `HorseDataLedger.ts` as before.
2. Class it in `OMAHA_OPTION_CLASSES`. The law test fails until it is classed.
3. A rule that decides by a size, a chip count, a stack share, a hand category, a betting line or an opponent label is `categorical` and must name the priced option that replaces it, with a rule site in `OMAHA_CATEGORICAL_RULE_SITES` (its receipts and the hand-off text). The law test plays the corpus with every priced option on and fails when such a rule's receipt fires.
4. Update the table above in the same change.
