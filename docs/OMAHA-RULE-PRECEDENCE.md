## What "priced" means here

A rule is priced when its answer is a comparison of the candidate actions (fold, check, call, bet at candidate sizes, raise at candidate sizes, bluffs included where legal) on:

- the Horse's exact hand, against the opponent's range for that action, line and board, counted or sampled with the Horse's own cards, the board and known dead cards removed;
- the opponent's measured rates where his own play supplies them, each a posterior mean that shrinks toward the population figure with few (decayed) opportunities, and the stated population prior where it does not;
- the price (pot odds, chips left for later streets, players still to act, position).

A bet's share of the pot enters only as an input to the opponent's range and to the price. It never switches a rule on.

## The order, innermost to outermost

A later step sees the earlier answer and can replace it. "Priced option" names the default-OFF option that, when ON, makes the step's categorical rule stand aside in PLO4/PLO5 cash (one board, high only, no bomb pot, not Lightning). With the option OFF the step acts exactly as before.

## With the priced options on

- Step 18 is the priced comparison of fold, call and a raise to each legal size on the exact hand. It is the outermost answer on the turn and river facing a wager: a raise is made when it is worth more than the best other action by the margin (any hand), a raise that is not becomes the priced call or fold, and steps 2 and 18's nut rules do not decide. A nut-class hand keeps the raise or the call it was given.
- Step 13's bar is the sampled comparison of the raise and the call; the big-draw wager is priced out of position and without the combo-draw class.
- Step 6's out-of-position turn check and paired-board checks are the comparison of the bet and the check on the card-impact model against every opponent, whatever his label; the card-impact press stands and is priced again at step 14. Step 5 and the blocker-bluff bar are the same comparison. The turn bet the Horse then FACES after that check is not taken over: the out-of-position rule's facing half (call with nut outs at the price, fold with none) still decides, at step 6, in `omahaFacingBetEvOf` and again at step 14 (`OMAHA_OPEN_RULE_SITES`; reason below).
- Step 7's cells do not decide: where one would change a call or raise, the exact count judges it.
- Step 9's underpair fold stands only when the multiway call is priced below it.
- Step 1's pressure play is withheld or made by its price.
- Step 12 reads the bet's size continuously, with the bluff prior measured against what the Horse actually won (`OMAHA_PRICED_MEASURED_BLUFF`), and takes the bettor's bluff share from his measured bet rate.
- `omahaPricedMix`: inside a priced rule that is on, two actions closer than the comparison's own uncertainty are mixed by the gap, on a draw from public state only.

## The opponent read

Every priced comparison takes the opponent read through one function, `omahaPricedReadOfUser` (`HorseOmahaPricedWiring.ts`), which returns an `OmahaPricedRead` (`HorseOmahaPricedRules.ts`). Today it is built from the pooled read (`HorseOmahaReadConditional`), without its labels. The per-street, per-texture read (`HorseOmahaStreetRead`) is delivered through `omahaPricedSetStreetRatesSource`: a known cell replaces the pooled figure after shrinking toward it by its own opportunity count; an unknown cell or no source leaves the pooled read. With no read of an opponent, the card-impact comparisons take a person's measured bet rate when checked to by street (`OMAHA_PRICED_PERSON_BET_CHECKED_TO`); the read's rates are posterior means of decayed counters, so a thin or stale read moves a comparison a little and a deep fresh one fully, with no sample threshold.

## Categorical rules not yet replaced

One part of a replaced rule still decides with the options on, listed in `OMAHA_OPEN_RULE_SITES` and pinned by the law test: the out-of-position turn rule's facing half (audit row 10). Its priced replacement is the facing-bet EV verdict, which in the counterfactual replay of the audited PLO5 hands folded strong draws and a set class to turn bets at prices their equity beats (4 of 4 judged changes wrong); that verdict's field factor and turn realization are the WooWoo audit's RC-02b, held by the PLO Strategy pickup (01BNteGY). It moves to the priced side when that is fixed.

Listed in `OMAHA_OPTION_CLASSES` as `open`, with the build that holds each. The law test pins the list so it can only change on purpose: the flop paired-board fold (`ploPairedBoardFold`; it stops deciding the flop fold or call only while `omahaFlopFacingBetEv`, default OFF, is on), the bare-overpair flop check (flop street plan build), the made-hand defence's bet half, the re-raised-pot flop continue, the one-pair cells (off-only), the turn set size, and the equity line of the river nut-blocker bluff.

Not in the register, and so not pinned by the law test: the flop 20 big blind mark (audit row 14). It is not an option; it is the reference brain's own big-bet mark in `HorseMind.ts`. `omahaBigBetIsPotShare` replaces it with a pot-share line on the turn and river only (itself a size line, classed `priced` in the register by its own declaration). On the flop the mark still feeds the reference fold or call, and only `omahaFlopFacingBetEv` (default OFF) takes that fold or call away from it. Until the mark has an entry of its own in `OMAHA_OPEN_RULE_SITES`, nothing in the build fails if it decides a flop action.

## Adding or changing an Omaha rule

1. Register the option in `HorseDataLedger.ts` as before.
2. Class it in `OMAHA_OPTION_CLASSES`. The law test fails until it is classed.
3. A rule that decides by a size, a chip count, a stack share, a hand category, a betting line or an opponent label is `categorical` and must name the priced option that replaces it, with a rule site in `OMAHA_CATEGORICAL_RULE_SITES` (its receipts and the hand-off text). The law test plays the corpus with every priced option on and fails when such a rule's receipt fires.
4. Update the table above in the same change.
