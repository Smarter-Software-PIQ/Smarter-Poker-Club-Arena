## Mechanism

MET. HorseOpponentExploit.withManiacShortHandedReach attaches a maniac width to a short-handed or heads-up no-limit
hold'em read that has none, when bet-when-checked-to is credibly beyond every Horse at 0.55 or more, fold-to-bet is
0.36 or less on at least 1.2 decayed opportunities, and the read rests on at least 15 decayed hands. The width is
max(0.35, PFR posterior mean). HorseMind.opponentRead applies it after the maniac sample gate, only with the option
on; maniacRaiseWidth returns it. Every number is an existing constant.

## Reach

NOT MET (not measured on production). On synthetic counters: a player who bets 0.9 of his checked-to spots and
folds 0.1 of the bets he faces is reached at 15.56 decayed hands (about 19 played); at 0.8 and 0.2 by 30 decayed
hands. Regulars who bet 0.5 to 0.65 and fold 0.35 to 0.45 short-handed (0.5 to 0.6 and 0.4 to 0.45 heads-up) are
not reached at any sample. A heads-up player who bets 0.65 and folds 0.35 is reached at a saturated window.

## Scope

No-limit hold'em, short-handed and heads-up scopes only. Full ring, Omaha, fixed limit, presumed reads and reads
that already have a width are passed through unchanged.
