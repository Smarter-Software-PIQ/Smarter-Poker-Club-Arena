## Mechanism

Two read sites, both inside `HorseLogic.decidePostflop` (no preflop range changes: preflop is out by code), both behind `fixedLimitCallIsNotACommitmentInScope(opts, vi, gs)` = option `=== true`, fixed-limit structure, two hole cards, full deck, not Omaha (in production exactly flh), and `!isTournamentMode(gs)` (the test the W5 options use; it trusts the engine's explicit `gameMode`):

1. The call price (`flCallPrice`, `HorseFixedLimitCallPrice.ts`): a call facing a bet costs the chips to call plus the expected further wagers of the street while a live player left to act can still raise before the cap, at the raise-behind rate measured on recorded Horse-only hands (census D, 95 % upper bounds, hold'em flop 1.4 / turn 3.3 / river 4.2 %); a closing call is the bare raked price. The excess is a shade on the equity the V38 verdicts are handed. Receipt `fixed_limit_call_priced_remaining_action_seen`.
2. The commitment test (`fixedLimitCallNotCommitted`): a fixed-limit call is never priced in the committed branch (no stack-to-pot or invested-share term, so no V20 multiway commit bar). Receipt `fixed_limit_call_not_a_commitment_seen`.

Law `AFixedLimitCallIsNotACommitment.law.test.ts` (11 tests, real HandController spots): the price formula, the measured-rate table, the remaining action read from a real street (capped included), the priced and the closing call at flh 4 seats, a flh 4-seat one-bet river call judged the same at 40 chips as at 1,000 (and on main the short stack folds calls the deep stack makes), the commitment receipt at flh 6 seats, and the scope cases (see Scope). `AFixedLimitFixKeysOnTheBettingStructure.law.test.ts`: F2 engages on a two-card fixed-limit state shown as nlh card rules (keyed on structure and cards, not the name) and leaves the four-card fixed-limit state shown as plo4 exactly as off. Still missing: the price values are printed at constructed and harness nodes, not at a recorded production node (same scope as F1's accepted file). MET.

## Limits

- Value is from scripted opponents and Horse identities with empty durable profiles, against one favourable range (the maniac); no person has played fixed limit since 2026-11-03, so nothing here measures a person.
- The league gain comes from both directions against the maniac (first-divergence counts, not a money split); on recorded Horse-only hands the option changes about 0.16 % of flh trials, almost all calls it folds. A production gain is not shown.
- The predeclared 0.21 pool included flo8, which the gate now excludes; the flh-only reading is post hoc. No value cell ran at this exact head.
- Heads-up and VPIP-floored flh cash tables have no value or harm cell; 65 % of recorded decisions and 19 of the 21 changed replay decisions are on floored tables.
- Raise-behind rates are Horse-only upper bounds per street; the hold'em row would also price non-hi-lo fixed-limit Omaha if such tables existed, but the scope gate keeps the option off for every Omaha table.
- The replay does not reproduce the worker's opponent reads, the authored persona, the second look, the decision hour or the phase13 joint policy.
- With the gate, the flo8 and tournament short-stack commitment mispricing that first motivated F2 is addressed by no option; hi-lo and tournament tables keep main's behaviour.
- This file states the limits; no ON pull request carries them yet, because none is proposed.
