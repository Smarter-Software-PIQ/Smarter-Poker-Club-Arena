## What it does

PLO4/PLO5 cash (tables and Lightning), high only, one board, facing a bet or a raise. Code: `server/src/engine/HorseOmahaFacingBetValueShareMeasured.ts` (pure) and `HorseLogic.omahaFacingBetEvCount` / `omahaFacingBetEvRule` / `omahaPricedDeps`.

1. THE STREET RECORD MOVES THE VALUE SHARE (turn and river, any bettor of a known kind, mind on). The facing-bet verdict's value region is the strongest share V of all holdings (`OMAHA_EV_VALUE_SHARE`, stated) times 0.8 per earlier street the bettor bet. With the option, g = (his first-bet rate on a street / his kind's rate) ^ e, the rate the posterior mean of his decayed street cell against the kind's rate with the kind's measured pseudo-opportunities (`HorseOmahaFlopBettorRead.OMAHA_FLOP_BETTOR_PRIOR`, `omahaFlopBettorRate`); e is the measured elasticity of a bet range's width in its rate, derived from the flop slopes already in the engine (slope of other first bets / half the kind's mean flop-bet rate: person 0.95, Horse 0.47). This street's V is multiplied by g (a raise has no cell: not moved); every earlier street on which he made the first bet narrows by 0.8^(1/g) instead of 0.8. No opportunity: exactly the stated share.
2. A PERSON'S HEADS-UP TURN OR RIVER BET IS PRICED ON HIS LINE-CONDITIONED MEASURED RANGE. His holdings are drawn from the unseen cards (the Horse's cards removed; generator seeded by the cards), cut to the width of a 3-bet or more preflop (`omahaPreflopDefenceRangeWidth` at the measured person re-raise rate, as `HorseOmahaFacingBetValueRange`); each holding's strength is its percentile in that range on this board; the weight of a strength is the MEASURED density of people's bets of that street, by earlier aggressor streets and bet size (river `OMAHA_VALUE_RANGE_FIT`, 1,201 bets, see `omahaFacingBetValueIsRangeRelative.md`; turn `OMAHA_TURN_RANGE_FIT`, below), tilted by his street record as density^(1/g). His bluffs are inside the density: no separate bluff prior, no value percentile. A raise takes the most aggressive measured row (STATED, not measured). River: the Horse wins the holdings it beats. Turn: its equity against each holding is counted exactly over every river card not in the holding, times the base verdict's realization when chips stay behind. EV(call) at the actual price against the base verdict's one margin. This part needs no opponent memory (the kind is public) and takes the place of `omahaFacingBetValueIsRangeRelative` when both are on.
3. The flop verdict reads the bettor exactly as `omahaFlopVerdictReadsTheBettor` does (no new flop model).
4. PRECEDENCE: the priced facing-bet verdicts decide wherever they price, as with `omahaFacingBetEv` and `omahaFlopFacingBetEv` on (the option switches both verdicts on where it is on), and the priced raise sites (`omahaTurnRiverRaiseIsPriced`, `omahaPressureIsPriced`, `omahaBigBetCallsArePriced`) take their call or fold from that verdict. So the paired-board fold (`ploPairedBoardMustFold`), the big-bet cells (v81, v87) and every earlier call-discipline fold are inputs there, never the decider. The turn plan's look-ahead veto switches it off inside, as it does `omahaFacingBetEv`.

PLO6 is untouched: the six-card river verdict already exists behind `omahaFacingBetEv` and its PLO6 gate, and is not measured; the option adds nothing for six cards (the count's part 1 and 2 require four or five cards; the flop verdict is PLO4/PLO5 only). Tournaments, hi-lo and multi-board are untouched. Bet size never decides by itself: it selects the density row and the price. Nothing is keyed to an account.

Receipts: `omaha_facing_bet_value_share_measured` (a street record moved the turn/river value share), `omaha_facing_bet_measured_range_priced` (part 2 priced the verdict). League cards `plo4_` / `plo5_omaha_facing_bet_value_share_is_measured` (arm A production's live set plus the option, arm B the live set; the league seats no person, so part 2 is not dealt there).
