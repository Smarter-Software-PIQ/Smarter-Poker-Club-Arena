## What It Does

PLO4/PLO5 cash (tables and Lightning), high only, one board, no bomb pot, the flop, heads-up, facing a wager, where the rule chain's answer is a RAISE (`server/src/engine/HorseOmahaFlopRaisePriced.ts`; in the wager_rules node after the river opening rules and before the river current-nuts floor and the nut size band). The raise is priced at the legal minimum, the chain's own size, half way to the pot raise and the pot raise, beside the call and the fold, against the bettor's flop range built from people's play and his own record only (since 2026-11-27, omahaFlopRaisePeopleRange: his value region, the flop verdict's value share for the bet and line narrowed by his own tighter shown record, carries 1 - q, every weaker holding q, q the people-only flop non-value share for the size blended toward the balanced share; before, the pooled HorseOmahaMeasuredBettorRange table of every player's shown wagers): EV(raise to T) = f P (1 - rake) + (1 - f)(R' e_c (P + A + X)(1 - rake) - A), with f his fold share, the equilibrium minimum defence of the pot he is offered X / (P + A + X), he folds the weakest share of his range and continues with the rest, e_c the Horse's two-card-runout equity against it (a card-seeded pool of the unseen holdings ranked on the flop, per strength bin), R' = 1 when the call leaves no stack behind, else the street's realization; EV(call) = R eq (P + C)(1 - rake) - C. RAISE OR NOT: the call or the fold replaces the chain's raise only when it prices above it by more than the equity estimate's error (the realization discount and the margin apply). WHICH SIZE (2026-11-26): when the raise stays, two raise sizes are compared on the same footing, the same equity sample with no realization discount between them and no margin, and the size of highest price is taken among the sizes that survive the raise-or-not question as the chain's own size did (the call or the fold does not price above them by more than the margin). It never makes a raise out of a call or a fold, so the owner rules on draws (never raised out of position; in position only combo draws) keep holding. Receipts `omaha_flop_raise_priced_resized`, `_called`, `_folded`.

### Why The Size Rule Changed (Daily Audit 2026-11-26, Root Cause D2)

On 8 real heads-up flop raises against a person the first build priced the pot raise highest on all 8 and resized 3: every raise row that leaves a stack behind is multiplied by the flop realization factor (0.75 out of position, 0.85 in position), which shrinks the difference between two sizes of the same raise below the error margin. The realization discount and the margin answer "raise or not"; between two sizes of a raise already chosen they do not belong (the equity sample and its error are common to both sizes). With the change all 8 are resized to the pot raise through the engine's own decide (receipt `omaha_flop_raise_priced_resized` on every seed the chain raises): c4c7aa49 79.5 -> 102 bb, a774e1b3 328 -> 420, b3705d47 182.5 -> all in, ad4551e4 117 -> 150, 1b7ab774 46 -> 59, bf2d4a5d 19 -> 24, efdffea9 35.6 -> 44, b0023f31 58.5 -> 75. The ninth row (9fb142d2, an all-in over an all-in) is a call the audit mislabels; it is untouched.

## Prior anchor

- Baseline: with no record, his fold share against a raise is the equilibrium minimum defence of the pot he is offered, X / (P + A + X) (people's measured folds to a Horse's heads-up flop raise, 26.7 to 37.0% by size, are within one standard error of it); behind a thin people cell, the balanced non-value share for the price (omahaFlopBalancedShare).
- Own record: his shown flop bets (HorseOmahaBetRead's per-opponent flop cell) move the non-value share q as a Beta-binomial ratio with 8 pseudo-bets and narrow his value region where they are tighter than people's (omahaFlopPressureCapRegion); a raise faced reads no record. The OPPONENT-HUD feed holds no per-opponent flop fold-to-raise count, so the fold share is the equilibrium prior for every opponent.
- Population weight: the PEOPLE-ONLY flop non-value share for the size (HorseOmahaFacingBetPeoplePrior.data.ts, people's shown flop bets with mucked = false), blended toward the balanced share at 30 pseudo-bets. No pooled every-player table enters (law 6); until 2026-11-27 the pooled HorseOmahaMeasuredBettorRange table did.
- No unshown cards: the people table is fitted from cards people showed at showdown; the decision reads the public state, the Horse's own cards and the known dead cards.
