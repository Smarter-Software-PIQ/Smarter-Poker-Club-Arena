# omahaSampledRaiseIsPricedAtEverySize: authority for the live switch

Owner order of 2026-11-17 11:49 America/Chicago (no blanket rules; every decision by math, ranges, equity and EV) and of earlier the same day (the size of the bet must not make or break a decision: it may only feed the range and the price). Built by the PLO lane on 2026-11-19 for the verifier defect on `omahaPricedSampledRaise` (HorseOmahaPricedRules.ts). Evidence NOT MET: no league and no replay yet, and a price-indexed cell is required before switch-on (see "Required before switch-on"). **Do not switch on.**

## What it does

PLO4/PLO5 cash, high only, one board, no bomb pot, not Lightning; default OFF (read `=== true`). It is an input of `omahaDrawRaiseIsPriced` and `omahaPressureIsPriced` and acts only where one of them is on (both are OFF in production today). With it on, the sampled comparison of the flop and turn raise (`omahaPricedSampledRaise`):

- prices the fold (0), the call and a raise to each legal size: the minimum, the middle and the maximum legal raise (`OMAHA_PRICED_RAISE_STEPS`, the pot raise or the stack at most, no more than the deepest opponent holds) and the size proposed;
- reads each opponent's fold to a raise AT THE PRICE THAT RAISE LAYS HIM (`omahaPricedSampledFoldAtPrice`). His price is HIS call (the raise-to less what he has in on this street, his stack permitting) over the pot it is laid into: the raise increment for the bettor, the whole raise for a player still to answer the wager. The population figure (his fold to a bet times 0.42) is the fold at the reference price (`OMAHA_PRICED_SAMPLED_REFERENCE_FOLD`) and is carried to this price in proportion. The street read's fold-to-raise cell is carried from the price IT was measured at (`OmahaPricedCell.price`); a cell with no price is taken as measured at the reference price. As in `omahaPricedRaise` (#11345) the fold priced is the smaller of the two readings of a cell (the cell as measured, shrunk toward the prior at this price; the cell carried to this price);
- takes the Horse's share when called against THE PART OF HIS RANGE THAT CONTINUES AT THAT PRICE (`omahaPricedSampledContinuingShare`). The range model is the one `omahaPricedRaise` counts on the turn and river: the weakest holdings fold first, so the part that continues is the strongest (1 - fold) share of the range he wagered with. On the sample that range is the hands that continue against a bet, its strongest class the hands that continue against a raise, each class one figure (no order inside a class: that order would be read off the Horse's own cards). A share no larger than the strong class is that class (the one figure every size had before); a larger share adds the rest of the betting range in proportion. A larger raise is folded to more often and so is continued against by a stronger range. With two opponents the share is each one's, weighted by how often he continues;
- names the action of highest value: the raise of highest value when it is worth more than the call by 2 percent of the pot and more than the fold, else the call when it is worth more than the fold, else the fold. The legal sizes are priced whatever the size proposed is;
- MAKES the raise at the priced size. At the draw-raise rule (`omahaPricedDrawRaiseRule`) a raise that stands is made to the size of highest value (receipt `omaha_priced_draw_raise_resized`; `omaha_priced_draw_raise_mixed_resized` when `omahaPricedMix` took a raise that was NOT priced above the call) and the size given is kept when it is that size (receipt `omaha_priced_draw_raise_kept`, or `omaha_priced_draw_raise_mixed_kept` where the mix took a raise that was not priced above the call; a raise priced above the call that the mix turned into the call fires `omaha_priced_draw_raise_mixed_called`). The pressure raise of `omahaPressureIsPriced` is admitted when the priced size of highest value is worth more than the call, and the raise the brain then makes in that decision, a draw or not, is made at a priced size (`omahaPricedPressureRaiseSized`, receipt `omaha_priced_pressure_raise_resized`); before, it kept the brain's own size. The admission carries the size it priced (`sampledTo`): the raise is priced again and made at the size of highest value, and where it cannot be priced again or no longer stands above the call it is made at the admitted size, never at the brain's own unpriced size (law `AnAdmittedPressureRaiseIsMadeAtItsPricedSize.law.test.ts`). The admission is carried with the decision and nowhere else: the brain's pressure block, where it asked the price, writes HorseLogic's per-decision memo (`omahaPressureAdmittedAtPriceMemo`: the state object of the decision, the seat and the admitted size), `omahaDrawRules` reads it for the same state object, and it is cleared with the other per-decision memos when a decision starts and when it ends. The pricing module holds no decision state (its one module-level value is the injected street-rates reader, which carries no decision data), so a later decision on the same public spot inherits nothing. At the three draw-raise sites the raise is admitted the same way and sized by the draw-raise rule. A changed amount drops the notes written for the old one (valueSize, continuationGuard);
- has a dedicated admission in the receipt validator (`omahaPricedSampledAdmitsResize`, responseValidation.ts `omahaSampledRaiseResized`) that admits only the exact chips of a priced size: the sizes priced depend on the public state alone, so the validator re-derives them; an all-in only where the whole stack is one of them. Validation as a whole is not narrowed by it: the validator's generic wager clause, unchanged, still admits any wager_rules change that ends in a bet, a raise or an all-in, of any size. The dedicated admission is not gated by the decision's option (the receipt context carries no options); it is what remains if the generic clause is ever narrowed.

Off (absent, false or a truthy non-boolean): one size is priced with its fold share size-blind, exactly as #11212; every decision is byte-identical (HorseOmahaSampledRaiseAtEverySize.test.ts).

## The defect it repairs

`omahaPricedSampledRaise` took one raise-to amount and a fold share that did not depend on it. A 20-chip minimum raise into 120 was priced with the fold share of an average raise (0.18 with no read; the cell's figure with one), and no raise size was compared with another. With no read the old comparison raises the minimum with no pair and no draw facing 20 into 100 (2c3c7s8sKc on Qh9h3d: raise +4.2, call -4.0); priced at the price it lays, that raise is folded to 0.06 of the time and every action there is worth less than the fold.

## The measurement behind the reference price

Archive plo-handoff-review-01a1cbbd/full-sweep-20261115/private/decisions_full.csv (PLO4/PLO5 cash, heads-up, 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-15, a Horse's raise and the response to it; persons and Horses together), computed by sampledraise-01TLQer7J/fold-by-price.py (output fold-by-price.txt): flop 65 raises, mean minimum-defence fold 0.369, folded to 11 (0.17); turn 39 raises, mean 0.353, folded to 9 (0.23). By the price laid, flop and turn together: below 0.30, 1 of 21 folded; 0.30 to 0.40, 5 of 32; 0.40 and above, 14 of 51. The fold rises with the price laid; the option carries the population figure and each read along the minimum-defence line through the measured point.

## Required before switch-on

A price-indexed cell is required before switch-on. The reference price (flop 0.369, turn 0.353) is the mean price of the raises Horses made through 2026-11-15 (`OMAHA_PRICED_SAMPLED_REFERENCE_MEASURED_THROUGH`). Every fold-to-raise cell stored today is exactly {hit, opp} (HorseOmahaStreetRead, horse_exact_profiles.street_counters) and carries no price, so it is read as measured at the reference price. That holds only while Horses raise at the sizes they raised then. With this option on they raise to the size of highest value (often the pot), the cells go on being measured against those larger raises, and a cell read at the reference price would then have its size counted twice. So before any switch-on:

- the street read must keep, beside each fold-to-raise cell, the decayed sum of the minimum-defence price of the raise faced at each opportunity (increment / (pot + call + increment)), so the cell's mean price is that sum over `opp`. This needs a counter migration (the stored row, its SQL twin `fn_horse_street_read_add`, the read-frame row and `validStreetRead`); none is written here. The lane's next counter migration carries it;
- the read must deliver it as `OmahaPricedCell.price` (the code that reads it is in place and tested: `omahaPricedSampledFoldAtPrice`);
- a cell measured before the price was kept is still read at the reference price; it decays out at the street read's half-life (30 opportunities).

## Limits

- The line through the measured point is the minimum-defence shape, not a fitted curve: 104 raises do not support more. The three price bands rise faster than the line below the reference price (1 of 21 against 0.09 to 0.12 priced), so a small raise is, if anything, still priced with too much fold.
- The evidence for the shape is pooled: persons and Horses together as responders, and a Horse responder follows engine logic.
- A cell with no price is read at the frozen reference price (see "Required before switch-on"). Above the reference price the smaller-of-two-readings rule already takes the cell as measured (not scaled up); below it the cell is scaled down from the reference price, which is too little once the cell was measured over larger raises.
- The continuing range is two sampled classes (continues against a bet; continues against a raise), each one figure. It tightens with the fold share, not hand by hand; with a class under 40 sampled hands it is the one raise-continuing figure for every size. DIRECTION OF THE BIAS: toward raising. The Horse's share when called is never below the old single figure (the share against the raise-continuing class) and is above it for every size whose fold share is below one minus the strong class's part of the betting range, while the call's value is unchanged. So against the first commit every such raise is worth more, more raises are admitted, and small raises (the least folded to) gain the most. And the weaker class is priced at its AVERAGE share although the part of it that continues is its strongest part, so the Horse's share when called is overstated. The cumulative sample counts give each class's total only, not an order inside a class, so the share of the continuing part of the weaker class cannot be had from them; it needs the per-hand shares ordered by a strength that is not read off the Horse's own cards. Not built here; a league must be read with this bias in mind.
- For a player still to answer the wager the price is his real call over (pot + the Horse's call + his call). It leaves out the Horse's raise increment, which is in the pot when he acts: his real call, not his exact price (monotone in the size and close, his fold slightly overstated). With two opponents the share when called is each one's weighted by how often he continues; the case where both continue is not priced apart.
- The pot when called holds the bettor's increment for every caller; a player still to answer who calls puts in more than that.
- The pressure raise is sized in a decision whose pressure raise the sampled comparison admitted (flop, and the turn where the exact count did not admit it). The brain does not mark which of its raises needed the pressure read, so every raise it makes facing the wager in such a decision is sized, a value raise with a made hand included (its valueSize note is dropped); the sample model was built for draws. A turn or river pressure raise admitted by the exact count is sized by `omahaTurnRiverRaiseIsPriced` where that is on, else by the brain.
- No rake and no chips won or lost on later streets in either value.
- The call is the realized share of the pot now, with no chips won on later streets.
- The rule sites apply the raise or the call. The fold the comparison names is reported in the verdict (`action`) and is not applied by `omahaPricedDrawRaiseRule`: a draw raise that is not priced above the call becomes the call, as with the option off; the fold or call of the hand stays with the facing-bet owner.
- One or two opponents only; three or more, or one all in, keep the decision given (unpriceable, as before).
