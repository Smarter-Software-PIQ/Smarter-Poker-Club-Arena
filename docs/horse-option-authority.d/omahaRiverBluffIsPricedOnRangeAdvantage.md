## What It Does

PLO4/PLO5 cash river, heads-up, nothing to call: the price of omahaRiverBluffIsPricedOnBlockers (`HorseOmahaRiverOpeningBet.ts`, his fold from his own record, then people's population for the line and size, then equilibrium) with the RANGE ADVANTAGE as the second leg instead of blockers: the Horse's measured range for its line beats his on this texture and the hand is in the weakest share of its range that its value carries at the size.

Nothing is keyed on an account, a name or a person/Horse flag; public actions, the Horse's own cards and the known dead cards only. It changes only a final check into a bet, never a raise or a fold (owner rules: a hand that is not nut class never raises the turn or river; draws are never raised out of position). PLO4/PLO5 cash, high only, one board, no bomb pot, heads-up; PLO6, hold'em and tournaments are untouched (`server/src/engine/HorseOmahaOpeningBluff.ts`; law `anOmahaOpeningBluffIsPricedOnPeople.law.test.ts`).

## 2026-11-26 Review Fixes (t3, t4, t5)

An independent review of PR #12127 found three bugs; each is fixed at the root and held by a law (`anOmahaOpeningBluffIsPricedOnPeople.law.test.ts`, items 6 to 8; speed `anOmahaTurnBluffPriceIsFast.law.test.ts`).

- t3 (turn price): the Horse's equity when called and EV(check) were read on the big-draw sampler's own tiers, whose holdings are uniform random hands, so the turn price did not depend on how strong his range was. The sampler now also counts each sampled holding by its strength bin on the board now (the same cut as his range), and the bins are weighted by his measured, card-removed range: e_c(B) = sum p_b kappa_b e_b / sum p_b kappa_b (the bins that continue against the size), e_all = sum p_b e_b. Same draws, same seed; cost 1.03 times the work it replaces.
- t4 (owner order, people only): the range table pooled Horse and person shown hands. Refit (`scripts/ops/omaha-opening-bluff-range-fit.mjs --horses`, `HorseOmahaOpeningBluffRangeTables.json` v2-2026-11-26-people, Nov 19 18:00Z to Nov 26 17:48Z, 201,898 hands read): HIS range (`cells`, hisCell) from PEOPLE's shown hands only; the Horse's OWN range (`heroCells`, heroCell, the range-advantage leg) from the Horses' own shown hands. Decision: the Horse's own range is not a statistic about the opponent (it is what the deciding Horse holds in the role), so it is measured on the Horses that play the role and never prices his fold, his range or his answers. People cells are thin (for example plo4 turn oopcheck chk 21 shown, plo5 turn ip agg 72): each role cell shrinks by 30 pseudo-hands to a random holding (no information), never to Horse play.
- t5 (people fold fit): the fit filed an answer under 'agg' when the bettor made ANY wager on the street before; the engine reads the LAST wager. The fit now uses the engine's rule (`lineOf`, the same as `omahaPeopleFoldLineOf`) and drops answers whose recorded origin is the Horse policy (the pull now carries the origin; over the fit window no turn or river action by a non-Horse account had it, so the Horse-list filter already held). Refit `HorseOmahaPeopleFoldTables.json` v2-2026-11-26-lastwager: 27 answers moved between the agg and vsAgg cells (for example plo5 turn vsAgg c70-110 53 -> 60 answers, fold 0.396 -> 0.417); same 48 cells.
- River law (57 real audited nodes, `HorseOmahaRiverOpeningBet.realHands.law.test.ts`): bets 6, all the audit's bet (4 / 0 before the refit); the scored replay rows below are after the fixes (daily 2 fixed / 0 newly wrong, was 1 / 1; 2026-11-20..24 10 / 0, was 5 / 0).
- Probes re-run after the fixes (same harness, 2 seats, 100 bb, 16,000 deals, seed 11, his public answers as his lifetime record): all four bluff options on together, calling station PLO4 +0.44 +/- 0.38, PLO5 -0.07 +/- 0.16; over-folder PLO4 +10.11 +/- 2.09, PLO5 +9.15 +/- 3.69 bb/100 (before: +0.44 / -0.07; +10.28 / +14.18). With the river bet set (omahaRiverFirstToActBetIsPriced, omahaRiverCheckedToBetIsMeasured, omahaRiverBetFoldIsHisRecord, omahaRiverBetFoldIsPeopleMeasured) also on: station +0.40 +/- 0.39 / -5.03 +/- 1.17 (the PLO5 loss is that set's, unchanged by the fixes), over-folder +13.87 +/- 2.49 / +25.52 +/- 4.62. ## Prior anchor

- Baseline: equilibrium, the fold a minimum-defence player gives a bet of B into P, B / (P + B); a thin people cell reads it.
- Population weight: the PEOPLE-ONLY measured fold multiplier for the game, street, the Horse's line and the size (`HorseOmahaPeopleFoldTables.json`, fit by `scripts/ops/omaha-people-fold-fit.mjs`: answers by people only, Horse answers never enter, no HUD population rows), each cell shrunk to equilibrium by the street's measured between-player k (27.8 turn, 75.9 river answers).
- Own record: the opponent's own lifetime public answers (OPPONENT-HUD x.foldToBet by size, live through omahaOpponentLifetimeHud) shrunk to the people multiplier by the same k, so his own record dominates as it grows; a player who never folds is priced as one (probe: calling station ON minus OFF +0.4 +/- 0.4 PLO4, -0.1 +/- 0.2 PLO5).
- No unshown cards: the range tables are fitted on hands shown at showdown (mucked = false), his range on people's hands only and the Horse's own range on Horses' hands only (review t4); at decision time the option reads the public table state, the deciding Horse's own cards and the opponent's public record.
