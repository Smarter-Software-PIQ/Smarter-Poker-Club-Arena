# omahaFlopRealizationIsPriced: Authority For The Live Switch

Built 2026-11-24 by PLO-OWNER lead-6 (session 01BNteGX) from the PLO4/PLO5 over-fold root cause (plo-overfold/PLO-OVERFOLD-ROOT-CAUSE.md section 4) and the multiway audit (multiway/MULTIWAY-AUDIT-AND-PLAN.md section 5). ## What It Does

F3. PLO4/PLO5 cash flop facing a bet or a raise (tables and Lightning). EV(call) is a two-street tree on `HorseOmahaFlopCallTree.ts`'s own runouts (every turn card, 8 rivers each heads-up, 5 with a field): the bettor's holding is drawn from his measured flop range, the field dealt on the same cards; his turn barrel and his river wager (after a barrel, or after a turn checked through) come from his measured rate (PLO4 turn 0.427, river after a barrel 0.549, after a check 0.383; PLO5 0.406 / 0.559 / 0.417, shrunk toward his public per-street counters) and his measured range FOR EACH SIZE, conditioned on the runout: each sampled holding's chance to wager is set by its own strength on that turn and that river so the wagering holdings match the measured wager range (tables `later` and `bettor`). The Horse's turn and river decisions are made on samples that are not the ones scored (river: its own river strength against his measured river range for the size). No realization factor, no fitted multiplier (`OMAHA_FLOP_EV_REALIZATION_FIT`, median 0.42, circular), no person / Horse split. It decides the flop whether `omahaFlopFacingBetEv` is on or off; outside its spot the flop decides as with it off. Receipts `omaha_flop_call_tree_call` / `_fold` / `_changed`.

Re-test 2026-11-24 (v2): the first version called 84% of the old verdict's folds (flop folds 10.7% / 13.8% to 0.2-0.5 pot, under the 17-33% band). Two causes, both fixed: (1) his later bets were independent of the runout (only a fixed bluff-share shift), so a turn barrel never carried the hands the turn card improved, against a measured big turn barrel range with 40% in the nut bin; (2) each river call was decided on the same two or three sampled holdings it then scored (a clairvoyant call). After the fix: heads-up flop folds 21.2 / 41.2 / 42.6% (PLO4) and 25.4 / 42.1 / 46.7% (PLO5) at 0.2-0.5 / 0.5-0.7 / 0.7-1.1 pot against bands 17-33 / 33-41 / 41-52. An independent two-street Monte Carlo of 80 calls it now makes (separate code and random stream, 3,000 / 300 / 200-holding ranking pools, 1,200 deciding and 1,200 scored samples each) finds 1 of 80 negative (-0.015 pot, inside 1 SE).

Latency (measured 2026-11-24): about 100 ms per PLO4/PLO5 flop facing-bet decision (median 96 ms, max 143 ms over 80 spots) on the shared Mac Studio at load average 16-19; a replay of 9,322 decisions took 9 min with it on against under 2 min with it off. The tree scores about 29,000 hands per decision (flop, turn and river ranking pools of 1,024 / 96 / 48 holdings, 8 rivers per turn card, 8 holdings per runout). It may only be shadowed with the shadow-stage LATENCY BREAKER watching (HorseOptionShadowStage: one re-decision over 150 ms, or three over 50 ms within the last 20, self-disables the option on that worker until restart; at the measured ~100 ms the breaker is expected to trip within a few flop decisions unless the production worker is much faster, so a shadow run is first a latency measurement), and it may not be switched on before its latency is measured on the production worker.

Untouched: PLO6, tournaments, hi-lo, bomb pots, multi-board hands, hold'em and every other game (byte-identical; `scripts/ops/horse-flag-off-check.sh origin/main HEAD` 0 differences with every new option off). No input reads whether an opponent is a person or a Horse, a user id, a name or another seat's cards (`anOmahaFacingBetPriceNeverKeysOnIdentity.law.test.ts`). Scopable per game (`horse.omahaGameScope.<option>`); staged for shadow re-decision on the listed streets.

Tables: `server/src/engine/HorseOmahaFacingBetRangeTables.json` (v1-2026-11-24.2: `later` and the showdown-corrected caller ranges added 2026-11-24), fitted by `scripts/ops/omaha-facing-bet-range-fit.mjs` from PLO4/PLO5 cash hand_history Nov 17 06Z - Nov 24 07Z (232,468 hands read, 113,115 reaching a flop on one board), hole cards only of players the showdown lists as not mucked, every player pooled.

## Prior anchor

- Baseline: break-even defence, s / (1 + s); the law test holds the heads-up range to s / (1 + s) + 0.05 (flop 0.4-0.7 pot: 34.6% PLO4 / 34.4% PLO5 against 38.2% / 37.0% on about 600 states each).
- Population weight: the flop range and his later-street bet rates, sizes and ranges are measured population tables; no realization factor and no multiplier fitted on the Horses' own results is used (the circular fit of `omahaFlopFacingBetEv`). With `omahaFacingBetMinimumDefencePrior` on, the no-record flop prior is bounded the same way (calibration: no flop bound needed). His later bet rates are shrunk toward his own public per-street counters.
- Own record: the bettor's own shown record of the cell (`HorseOmahaBetRead`, from 4 shown bets) replaces the table's weak share through a Beta-binomial shrink with 8 prior bets (`OMAHA_MEASURED_RECORD_PRIOR`), so it dominates once it has a real sample; a record is never bounded by F6.
- No unshown cards: the tables are fitted by `scripts/ops/omaha-facing-bet-range-fit.mjs` from `hand_history` hole cards only of players the showdown lists as not mucked; at decision time the option reads the public table state and the deciding Horse's own cards only (`anOmahaFacingBetPriceNeverKeysOnIdentity.law.test.ts`).
