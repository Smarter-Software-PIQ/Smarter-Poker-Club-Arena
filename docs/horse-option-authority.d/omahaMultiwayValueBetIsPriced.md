## What It Does

M3 (v2). PLO4/PLO5 cash, flop/turn/river, nothing to call, two to four live opponents who have ALL checked this street (the Horse acts last; a player still to act is not capped by a check and his range is not measured, so that spot is not priced), the chain's final check (`omahaOwnerRuledFinal`, the same place as `omahaMultiwayCheckedToBetRule`; owner-ruled checks below still stand). EV(check) = the showdown share against the k players' measured CHECKED range (tables `checker`: players who checked a multiway street nobody wagered, showdown-bias corrected) x pot. EV(bet b) = A x pot + sum over j callers of P(j) x [R_j x (-b) + (1 - R_j) x (share against j measured calling ranges without the nut bin x (pot + (j + 1) b) - b)], A = the measured all-fold share anchored to equilibrium (never above b / (pot + b)), R_j = 1 - (1 - r)^j with r the calling range's nut-bin mass (a nut caller raises and the Horse gives up the bet). Sizes a third, a half and three quarters of the pot; the best replaces the check where EV(bet) - EV(check) > 2% of the pot and it is a VALUE bet: the callers put chips in behind on balance (sum over j of P(j) x (share_j x (j + 1) - 1) > 0); it never bluffs. Later streets are not priced. It never turns a bet into a check. Receipts `omaha_multiway_value_bet_priced_bet`, `omaha_multiway_value_bet_check_kept`.

Why v1 was wrong (diagnosed on the real cards above, v1 bets +1.31 +/- 1.61 bb, river -13.1 +/- 18.4): (1) its check was priced against a UNIFORM field, not the much weaker range players actually check with; (2) the all-fold share came from over-folding Horses with no equilibrium anchor; (3) players still to act were counted as capped callers (rebuild before the scope fix: no one behind +6.3 +/- 0.9 bb, one behind +2.6 +/- 1.4, two behind +0.6 +/- 4.9); (4) the value gate compared the hand to ONE caller's range, not to the j callers who pay; (5) raises over the bet were ignored.

Untouched: PLO6, tournaments, hi-lo, bomb pots, multi-board hands, hold'em and every other game (byte-identical; `scripts/ops/horse-flag-off-check.sh origin/main HEAD` 0 differences with every new option off). No input reads whether an opponent is a person or a Horse, a user id, a name or another seat's cards (`anOmahaFacingBetPriceNeverKeysOnIdentity.law.test.ts`). Scopable per game (`horse.omahaGameScope.<option>`); staged for shadow re-decision on the listed streets.

Tables: `server/src/engine/HorseOmahaFacingBetRangeTables.json` (v1-2026-11-24; the `checker` key added by `omaha-facing-bet-range-fit.mjs fit-checker` from the same pull), fitted by `scripts/ops/omaha-facing-bet-range-fit.mjs` from PLO4/PLO5 cash hand_history Nov 17 06Z - Nov 24 07Z (232,468 hands read, 113,115 reaching a flop on one board), hole cards only of players the showdown lists as not mucked, every player pooled.

## Prior anchor

- Baseline: a check becomes a bet only where every opponent has checked, the callers put chips in behind on balance, and EV(bet) - EV(check) > 2% of the pot.
- Population weight: the measured checked ranges and calling ranges at full weight; the measured all-fold share never above the equilibrium b / (pot + b).
- Own record: it reads no opponent record.
- No unshown cards: the tables are fitted by `scripts/ops/omaha-facing-bet-range-fit.mjs` from `hand_history` hole cards only of players the showdown lists as not mucked; at decision time the option reads the public table state and the deciding Horse's own cards only (`anOmahaFacingBetPriceNeverKeysOnIdentity.law.test.ts`).
