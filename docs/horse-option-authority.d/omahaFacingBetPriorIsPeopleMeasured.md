## The Defect It Answers

Horses over-fold to turn and river bets in PLO4/PLO5 cash (daily audit 2026-11-26; `plo-lead7-01TLQer8/audit-rootcause/REPORT.md` section B; `plo-lead7-01TLQer8/woowoo/REPORT.md`). Facing a winning regular's bets the Horses fold 64 to 81% where a pure bluff breaks even at 39 to 54%. 99% of those folds come from one verdict, `omahaFacingBetEv` (`HorseLogic.omahaFacingBetEvRule` -> `HorseOmahaFacingBetEv.omahaFacingBetEvVerdict`). Root causes, both in that verdict's bettor model:

1. The non-value share `OMAHA_EV_PRIOR_BLUFF` is a constant fitted on 41 to 179 hands of Nov 10 to 15 (turn 0.22 at 0.7 pot and over, river 0.13 to 0.19, raises 0.08 / 0.15), then moved by public reads measured against the HORSE mean, a field that almost never bluffs (1 bluff among 148 shown Horse bettors in the audited cell). People's own big heads-up turn bets are 0.39 non-value (n 297); their turn raises 0.33 (n 150).
2. On the river the bettor is modelled as a value region plus a bluff pool (the weakest half). The hands between them (one pair, weak two pair) are absent: a measurement that counts them as value makes every hand that beats them fold. People's big heads-up river bets are 55% value region, 22% middle, 22% weakest half (n 156).

Why earlier fixes did not stick: they were layered on top of this verdict and either re-fit on the Horse field (which never bluffs) or were keyed on whether the bettor is a person; and the grading league plays Horses that never bluff, so any correct defence loses there and was never switched on.

## What It Does

PLO4/PLO5 cash (tables and Lightning), turn and river, heads-up (multiway untouched), a bet or a raise, inside `omahaFacingBetEv` (`HorseOmahaFacingBetPeoplePrior.ts`):

- The bettor's non-value share is the PEOPLE-ONLY measured share for the street, size bucket and bet-or-raise (`HorseOmahaFacingBetPeoplePrior.data.ts`, generated by `server/scripts/omaha-facing-bet-people-prior-fit.ts` from `omaha-facing-bet-people-prior-export.sql`: a person's bet, a person's answer, cards a person showed with `mucked = false`; no Horse play, no HUD population rows; 26,823 hands Oct 27 to Nov 26). A called river bettor is always shown on this platform (7 days: 0 of 106 called person river bettors mucked), so the shown sample is not biased against bluffs.
- The balanced share for the price weights only a thin cell: `(n x people + 30 x balanced) / (n + 30)`.
- River: the verdict prices the three measured regions apart (`OmahaFacingBetEvRead.pricedRegions`, `omahaEvHandCounts(...).regions`), so the middle is no longer counted as value.
- The bettor's own shown bets of the street, size and line (`HorseOmahaBetRead`) move the share as a Beta-binomial mean with 8 pseudo-bets; on the river they move the non-core share and keep the people middle : bottom split. A raise has no per-opponent cell and uses the people raise cell.
- The old constant and the reads against the Horse mean do not act while it is on.

The same arithmetic for every bettor: no input reads whether an opponent is a person or a Horse, a name, or an account beyond the record lookup every bettor gets; no unshown card. It does not raise (owner rules on non-nut turn and river raises untouched). Multiway, the flop, PLO6, tournaments, hi-lo, bomb pots and hold'em are untouched (law test). Receipts `omaha_people_prior_priced`, `omaha_people_prior_record_read`, `omaha_people_prior_changed`. Staged for shadow re-decision on the turn and river.

## Prior anchor

- Baseline: the balanced non-value share for the price, bet / (pot + 2 bet) on the river and 1 - (1 - b)^2 on a turn bet with a river bet to come; it is the prior only where no people cell is measured and the weight behind a thin one.
- Population weight: the PEOPLE-ONLY measured share, weight n / (n + 30) (a cell of 150 people bets is 83% people); Horse play never enters any population number (law anOmahaFacingBetPriorIsPeopleMeasured checks the export and fit filters).
- Own record: the bettor's own shown bets of the street, size and line move the share as a Beta-binomial mean with 8 pseudo-bets at the people prior, so his own record dominates from about 8 shown bets.
- No unshown cards: the fit reads only cards people showed at showdown (mucked = false); at decision time the option reads the public table state, the deciding Horse's own cards and the bettor's record of hands shown at showdown.
