## What It Does

PLO4/PLO5 cash (tables and Lightning), river, heads-up, a bet of 0.4 pot or more or a raise, inside `omahaFacingBetEv` (`HorseOmahaRiverTextureClasses.ts`):

- The bettor's holdings are put in five ABSOLUTE classes of the board (score cuts that do not depend on the Horse's hand): A the hands of the board's key category that no other two cards beat inside it, and anything above; B the rest of the key category; C the two categories under it; D the category under those; E weaker. Key category: full house on a paired board, flush on an unpaired three-flush board, otherwise straight when one can be made, else three of a kind.
- The weight of each class is PEOPLE's measured share of shown river bets by texture (paired and three-flush, paired, three-flush by the turn, three-flush on the river, neither), size bucket (0.4 to 0.7 pot, 0.7 and over) and bet-or-raise (`HorseOmahaRiverTextureClasses.data.ts`, generated by `server/scripts/omaha-river-texture-classes-fit.ts` from `omaha-river-texture-classes-export.sql`: the bettor is a person, his cards were shown with `mucked = false`, two players live at the bet; 424 bets; no Horse bet, no HUD population row).
- Evidence order: the bettor's own shown bets (the ratio `omahaPeoplePriorShare` computes moves classes C, D and E) -> the texture cell of people -> people pooled over textures -> the balanced share for the price, 30 pseudo-bets behind each step.
- The verdict multiplies each class weight by the exact share of that class's holdings the Horse's own cards leave (class share after the Horse's cards over class share on the board alone) and by the Horse's exact win share inside the class. A hand holding the nut-flush card removes the nut flush from his range; a hand without it keeps it there. One formula; no hand-class rule; every answer is a price.

The same arithmetic for every bettor: no input reads whether an opponent is a person or a Horse, a name, or an account beyond the record lookup every bettor gets; no unshown card. It does not raise (owner rules on non-nut turn and river raises untouched). Multiway, the flop, the turn, bets under 0.4 pot, PLO6, tournaments, bomb pots and hold'em are untouched. Receipts `omaha_river_texture_classes_priced`, `omaha_river_texture_classes_changed`. Staged for shadow re-decision on the river.

## Prior anchor

- Baseline: the balanced non-value share for the price (`HorseOmahaEquilibriumAnchor`) is the weight of the weakest class where nothing is measured, the rest split as people split their value hands; it is the weight behind every thin cell.
- Population weight: PEOPLE-ONLY shown river bets, a texture cell weighted n / (n + 30) over people pooled over textures, pooled weighted N / (N + 30) over the balanced anchor; Horse play never enters any population number (law `anOmahaRiverBettorRangeIsTextureMeasured` checks the export and fit filters).
- Own record: the bettor's own shown bets of the street, size and line move the classes under the key category by the ratio the people prior computes (a Beta-binomial mean with 8 pseudo-bets), so his own record dominates as it grows.
- No unshown cards: the fit reads only cards people showed at showdown (mucked = false); at decision time the option reads the public table state, the deciding Horse's own cards and the bettor's record of hands shown at showdown.
