# nashCallVsJam: authority for the live switch

Programme option (2026-11-07, Nash push/fold V2), default ON in source (`OPTION_SWITCH_RELEASE_DEFAULT_ON`), read as `(opts.nashCallVsJam ?? true) !== false` (server/src/engine/HorseLogic.ts). It is not in `OPTION_SWITCH_OFF_ONLY` and is not an owner preflop range hold entry: it shipped on by the owner's league rule before that hold, and is registered with states `off`, `shadow`, `on_shadow`, `on` (release default `on`). Unlike the other files in this directory it describes an option that is on unless a switch row turns it off. Evidence NOT MET for this file's full bar: the league evidence it shipped on is recorded below and real-hand audited decision replays on the corrected history (#11556 + #11622) were scored on 2026-11-21 (`HVH48-E`: `fixed 1`, `newlyWrong 1`, `changed 194`; `HU30D`: `fixed 0`, `newlyWrong 0`, `changed 1`; `HVH48-D` cash: `0` changed, inert), meeting the replay build bar (`newlyWrong <= fixed`), with the reverse live-baseline check (`off vs on`) showing turning it off would flip `175` Auditor-E `OK` decisions while fixing `1`. Still missing: a human-field card that reaches the spot (the human-calibrated field never open-jams).

## What it does

Code: server/src/engine/HorseNashPushFoldV2.ts and HorseNashPushFoldV2.data.ts (generated by server/scripts/nash-push-fold/solve.py --v2), server/src/engine/HorseLogic.ts.

No-limit hold'em, hero folded to a lone open-jam (the first and only raise, all in, nobody has called) at 20bb effective or less: hero calls or folds from the chip-EV Nash jam-or-fold table's caller side (first caller only, exact card removal), by the jammer's dealt-in players behind (1..9), hero's caller kind (unposted seat, small blind, big blind), effective depth and dead ante; cash and tournament. The no-ante small-blind jam against the big blind stays with the sb_push chart. A maniac's jam keeps the maniac pricing. Explicit `false` keeps the old strength bar. Deeper jams, called jams, Omaha, short deck, pot and fixed limit unchanged. Tournament attribution reads the call as a `chart_bb_defend` reference route. nashPushFoldIcm (off-only, owner preflop range hold) prices this call at a bubble factor; aofClosingSeatNashCall reads the same caller table on All-In-Or-Fold tables.

## Receipts

nash_call_vs_jam (server/src/engine/HorseDataLedger.ts): a seat facing a lone open-jam at 20bb or less called or folded from the Nash push/fold V2 caller table.
