# Omaha: A Preflop Adjustment Against Proven Tight Opponents (Option, Default Off; Declared Evaluation Passed)

Owner order, 2026-11-13: tight-versus-maniac adjustments are mandatory for Phase 1. In Omaha a player whose own hands disprove the shared aggressive presumption is read on evidence alone, and because the exploit read engages the preflop statistics on the high side only, a proven tight player got exactly the baseline preflop. This adds the tight side for two preflop consumers, and nothing else.

## Change

Option `omahaTightOpponent` (HorseOmahaTightOpponent.ts; HorseDecideOpts, DEFAULT OFF; `true` turns it on). Pot-limit Omaha high, cash and ordinary tournament preflop. Never in hi-lo, bomb pots, multi-board, All-In-Or-Fold, or at 25bb effective or less (push/fold zones). Hold'em and EXPLOIT_SIDES are untouched.

- RESPECT: facing an open from a proven tight raiser, the value 3-bet bar moves toward the top 30% of what he raises (at most +0.10), the call bar rises 0.04, and no bluff 3-bet or squeeze is made; facing his re-raise, the 4-bet bar rises 0.03 and the call bar 0.06. Not beside a wide read of the same raiser. Bound to the raiser faced.
- STEAL: first in from late position or the small blind with no limpers and EVERY player left to act a proven tight folder, the open bar comes down by half the fold-equity surplus over a pot-sized open's break-even (risk / (risk + dead money)), at most 0.10.
- Evidence (at least as strict as the disproof): 15+ decayed hands in this game and table size; the presumption disproved; a raiser's pfr (or rfi on 15+ first-in opportunities) credibly at most half the Horse mean, with VPIP not above the mean (a loose-passive player is not tight); a folder's VPIP credibly at most 0.6 of the Horse mean; 90% one-sided on the statRead posterior; no wide read; withdrawn when recent raising is credibly back above the raiser bar. Unknown, sparse, presumed, Horse-like, loose-passive and maniac opponents get the baseline.
- Receipts: `plo_tight_raiser_respected`, `plo_tight_blinds_steal` (the intent changed), `plo_tight_opponent_considered` (applied, same intent), from a rewound counterfactual that restores the RNG, detaches the deep-dive collector and suspends telemetry. League cards `plo4/plo5_omaha_tight_opponent` (a `true`, b today's brain). Benchmark: development persona `omaha_tight_field_v1` of the Omaha development field.

The default nevertheless stays OFF: docs/HORSE-DECISION-CHANGE-EVIDENCE.md (enforced by HorseDefaultOnNeedsEvidence.law.test.ts) requires, before any decision option is on by default, input fidelity, per-node value under at least three opponent ranges, continuation frequencies measured from recorded hands and reach measured with production inputs. ## Limits

Almost all of the measured gain is the steal side (56-71 changed opens per 1,000 hero hands at six seats); respect changed the intent only 1.4-2.3 times per 1,000, so it is close to unmeasured and rests on its law cases. At six dealt the evidence standard admits only extreme nits as folders (VPIP at most about 8.5% PLO4 / 9.4% PLO5 on the full decayed window) and raisers at about 3% or less; the owner accepted this as a stated limit. The tight field is a development persona and the control field misses the fidelity criterion: neither is a qualified human population.
