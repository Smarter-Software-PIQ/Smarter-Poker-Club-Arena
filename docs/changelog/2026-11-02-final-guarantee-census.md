# Final guarantee terms for delayed engine instances

The old engine created five additional unentered future tournaments with old guarantees while the qualified producer policy awaited activation. This finite migration matches exact tournament IDs, schedule IDs and original amounts: two 500 to 250, two 1,000 to 500, and the 2,500 freezeout to 1,500 under the confirmed rounding rule. It preserves all previously corrected board terms and entered, locked, finalized or running events.

The permanent creation policy remains TournamentGuaranteePolicy.ts, delivered in PRs 9264 and 9296. No scheduling loop, table restriction or wallet mutation is added. The existing native PostgreSQL qualification checks exact amounts and labels, repeat installation, unchanged unrelated rows and all seven commitment/boundary guards for each captured event. Deployment and live readback remain required.
