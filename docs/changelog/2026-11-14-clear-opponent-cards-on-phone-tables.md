# Keep Hidden Cards Clear on Phone Tables

On six- and nine-seat portrait tables, opponents’ face-down cards fan toward the felt. The portrait fitter previously reserved space on the opposite shoulder, and a hidden fan could cover a neighboring player's nameplate after chairs locked. Card placement now uses the inward-facing fan and checks its full visible bounds against the board and player displays. The table, avatars, and bottom controls keep their positions when a fan needs clearance.

Dense phone tables also spent seconds repeating the same collision checks while finding their initial chair positions. Chair placement now reuses those calculations within a search.
