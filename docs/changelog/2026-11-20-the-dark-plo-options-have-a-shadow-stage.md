# The dark PLO4/PLO5 cash options have a shadow stage (2026-11-20)

A PLO4/PLO5 cash option that ships dark could not be graded: offline replays find too few decisions with a person in the hand. The option shadow (2026-11-18) already re-decides a committed decision with one option flipped and journals the counterfactual without acting on it, but it was tuned for hold'em: it spends its budget on every game, skips any decision that took over 15 ms (most priced Omaha postflop decisions), has no preference for hands with a person dealt in, and has no automatic stop when a re-decision is slow.

`HorseOptionShadowStage.ts` fits it to the options listed in `OPTION_SHADOW_STAGE` (the dark PLO4/PLO5 names; nothing else changes path):

- **Scope.** A listed option is re-decided only in PLO4/PLO5 cash (table and Lightning), in its listed games and streets. Never PLO6, PLO8, hold'em, a tournament or a bomb pot.
- **Budget.** The acted decision may have taken 50 ms (15 ms for every other option). The shared per-minute budget is unchanged; a hand with no person dealt in takes at most 2 staged re-decisions per worker per minute (the order of a measurement, read by no decision).
- **Latency breaker.** Per option and worker: one re-decision over 150 ms, or three over 50 ms within the last 20, stops that option's re-decisions in that worker until it restarts (`option_shadow_stage_self_disabled_seen`, then `option_shadow_skipped_stage_disabled_seen`). Timed under `option_shadow_stage_redecide`.
- **Companions.** An option that acts only with another on (the math core's street flag with `omahaMathCore`) is re-decided with the companion true as well.
- **Switching.** No new row, state or setting: a listed option is staged by its ordinary `shadow` row. The opponent lifetime HUD is not listed (nothing arms its record in shadow, so its receipt would read "same" falsely).

Proof (`aListedOptionInShadowStageNeverChangesTheActedDecision.test.ts`, production worker runtime): with a staged option in shadow, companion included, the acted decision, RNG positions and plan effects of every recorded FAST decision of the phase 6C journal sample are those of no row; hold'em and tournament decisions run no re-decision and count nothing; PLO6 is out of scope; the breaker trips and stays tripped; an option that is not staged gives the same receipts and counters as without the table. Measured on the shared Mac under load (PLO4 cash fixture, 12 re-decisions each): option alone p50 1.06 ms, max 2.07 ms; with `omahaMathCore` as companion p50 46.64 ms, max 48.89 ms, which is at the 50 ms slow line: the core's street flags are expected to trip the breaker until the core's own cost is lower.

## Follow-up the same night: the staged re-decision runs after the answer (VERIFIER V52)

As first built the staged re-decision ran before the response was built, so a sampled decision answered later by the re-decision's time (up to 150 ms): a latency cost and, with the preference for hands with a person dealt in, a timing tell. Now nothing of it runs before the answer. After the `FAST_RESULT` is sent the pick is handed over; when the job has ended it is dropped if any request is waiting, otherwise it runs on a later event-loop turn of an idle worker on which no job has started since. The gates (acted time, scale, breaker, Horse-only cap, per-minute budget) are taken then. The receipt is journaled as its own record, kind `option_shadow_receipt`, under the decision's hand and turn keys (a reader joins it to the decision by turn key); the decision record of a staged option carries no `optionShadowReceipt`. Options that are not staged are unchanged (their re-decision and receipt stay where they were). What remains: a request arriving while a re-decision is running waits for it to end.

Proof: up to the answer the worker's events (decides, counters, timings) are identical with a staged option in shadow and with no row, for a hand with a person and a Horse-only hand, and identical between the two; a 120 ms re-decision leaves the response at 5 ms; requests queued behind a decision drop its re-decision. Measured on the shared Mac, request to response, production brain: PLO4 cash fixture (n 60 each) no row p50 2.82 / p90 4.80 ms, staged shadow with the core companion p50 2.80 / p90 5.81 ms; every recorded FAST decision (n 42 each) p50 4.82 / p90 22.09 ms against p50 3.78 / p90 19.58 ms.

Cost of one re-decision on the recorded PLO5 postflop decisions (`HorseLogic.decide`, 12 flop / 27 turn / 17 river): as live p50 7.3 / 12.7 / 18.6 ms, max 54.8 / 28.7 / 43.7 ms; with the math core owning the street p50 125.7 / 96.7 / 57.1 ms, max 162.2 / 171.4 / 122.1 ms, over the 50 ms line in 53 of 56. A math-core street flag in shadow therefore trips the breaker almost at once on every worker.

## Second follow-up (VERIFIER V59)

- **The hand review skips the new record kind.** `reconcileHorseJournalHand` took every record that was not one of its named kinds for a decision or an execution of the turn, so an `option_shadow_receipt` (same turn key as its decision) would have counted as a second execution and the hand would have reviewed as conflicted. It is now skipped by name, as a plan receipt is. Proof: `aStagedShadowReceiptIsNeverATurnOfTheHand.test.ts` (with one or two receipts in the hand the review concludes exactly what it concludes without them; the test fails without the skip). The earlier statement that the review already skipped the kind was wrong.
- **The math core's street flag is no longer staged.** `omahaMathCorePlo5Flop` is removed from `OPTION_SHADOW_STAGE`: its re-decision costs 50-125 ms on PLO5 postflop and a request arriving on the same worker would wait behind it. It returns only with a separate shadow worker thread or once the core's cost is lower. The table now lists five ordinary dark options.

## Third follow-up (VERIFIER V63): no new journal record kind

The record kind `option_shadow_receipt` added above is withdrawn. Every engine release before it rejects an unknown kind (`Invalid Horse journal record`): the journal's writer thread catches that and reports the writer unavailable, the publisher marks the journal failed and capture stops for that shard until it re-arms. Nothing reaches the decision worker and nothing crashes, but after a rollback an older engine meeting a receipt in a segment would stop capturing. So the stage adds no kind and no file: `record.ts`, `HorseDecisionJournal.ts` and `review.ts` are exactly as before the stage. A staged receipt is held in the worker (at most 8; an older one is dropped and counted `option_shadow_stage_receipt_dropped_seen`) and rides in the next decision record that worker journals, field `stagedOptionShadowReceipts`: `[{ decisionKey, requestId, decisionTimeMs, receipt }]`, each naming the decision it re-decided. A reader joins on `decisionKey`. Receipts held when a worker stops are lost. Proof (same law file): the next decision record carries exactly the earlier decision's receipt; no record carries the field with no row or for an option that is not staged; the validator refuses `option_shadow_receipt` and accepts a decision record carrying the field; the hand review concludes the same with and without the field.
