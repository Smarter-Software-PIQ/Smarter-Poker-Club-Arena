# Every Built Horse Skill Reaches The Decision

Dan's goal: every built horse skill must be activated and usable by all 1,000 horses. The development pilots of 2026-11-06 (evidence: `FEATURE-AUTHORITY-QUALIFICATION.md`, hybrid-release-20261105) left three skills that could not be activated however a qualification came out. This change repairs each one so it is ready to qualify. All three stay default off; nothing here selects or activates a skill, and no held-out seed was dealt.

## barrelContinuationOutlook

Finding: the carried turn outlook is consumed. A diagnostic pilot (temporary local instrumentation, not committed) showed the river reads the committed outlook and the V7 river continuation multiplies its continuation chance by it; HorseLogic.decide on PLO4 changes a river check into a 23-chip barrel with the flag on. The pilot's zero changed pairs came from reach: of the river spots that read a carried outlook, most were decided first by an earlier river owner (in the NLH two-dealt diagnostic, 8 of 10: four value hands, the rest air or medium hands taken by earlier river branches), and the remaining continuation draws did not land inside the narrow window the multiplier moves. Nothing was silently dropped, so no precedence was invented. What was missing was a way to see this: `barrel_continuation_outlook_applied`, `_considered` and `_superseded` now say what happened to every carried outlook, so the held-out run can count its own reach.

## smallPotNearNutRiverValue

Finding: the code path is reached. Instrumented, it was evaluated at every PLO heads-up cash river facing a bet. The defect was in its development workload: the shared overstab population always opens the river at 0.75 pot, while the candidate's declared domain is an opening bet of at most half the pot before it. In 8,640 pairs its own workload could never produce one in-domain spot. The near-nut target now runs on `near_nut_mixed_river_open_stress_v1`, identical except that the river opening bet is drawn from 0.33, 0.45 and 0.75 pot (out-of-domain sizes remain as controls); its design is `near-nut-physical-development-v2` and its warm history `...-warm-v3-32`. The helper, its thresholds and every other population are unchanged (the shared decision function keeps its exact RNG sequence when no river fraction is supplied). Receipts: `small_pot_near_nut_value_applied` (its raise reached the final action), `_considered` (in domain, the original value gate already raised) and `_superseded` (a later owner replaced the raise).

## v53PloTourneyIcm

Finding: no production code path ever set the flag. `HorseTournamentFlagAuthority.ts` adds the activation route, mirroring the cash feature selection: the release image may ship `docs/evidence/tournament-flag-activation/selection.json`, naming a held-out locked flag plan and the assembled card verdicts (`horseTournamentCardSummarize.ts` output, each `measured` and `wins` with no reasons), bound by sha256 to their bytes and to the running decision source. The worker admits it once per epoch and sets `v53PloTourneyIcm` only for a decision inside an admitted card (PLO4/5/6, MTT or SNG). A request can never turn it on; offline benchmark workers keep their explicit arm. No selection ships, so it is refused as `unselected` and the flag stays off. Receipts: `v53_plo_icm_applied` (the ICM shifts changed the preflop choice, measured against the same context without them on a rewound RNG), `_considered` and `_superseded`.

## Proof

`server/src/engine/HorseSkillActivationWiring.law.test.ts` (law `docs/laws.d/every-built-horse-skill-reaches-the-decision.md`) drives HorseLogic.decide with a pinned decision clock for each skill: the action changes when its condition holds, it is a no-op when off or outside its condition, and the receipt names what happened. The nine receipts are registered in `scripts/horse-measure/sql/activation.sql`. New development numbers are recorded in the evidence file; they are development estimates, not qualification.
