# Promo Fees Have No Free Commission

Fees actually funded with locked promo still count in full toward the 4x
play-through target. They no longer create agent commission or player
rakeback. An unrelated outstanding grant does not affect real-funded play.

The accepted hand's original promo contribution determines cash eligibility.
Each original tournament charge records its actual promo portion before its
fee source is captured, including explicit zero for real-funded charges.
Missing post-launch provenance refuses that original fee transaction. Old
charges keep their historical behavior. Free Buy entries charge no fee.

Mixed funding excludes the corresponding proportion of the original fee.
Excluded cents are rounded once; excluded plus eligible credit always equals
gross. The original bank receipts, gross fee rows, stored gross source credit,
grant fee meter and historical agreement identities stay intact. Commission
tiers cascade the same recorded rates over eligible credit; the excluded gross
remains in the club residual. The canonical payable source exposes eligible
credit to the existing certified rakeback calculator. Later authorized contract
interpretations receive the same frozen funding cut and cannot restore it.

Native PostgreSQL proof executes the actual captured source owners, commission
guards and certified rakeback writer. It reproduces the previous full-gross
commission before the change, then verifies mixed/full/real funding, source
conservation, replay, races, failed second-tier rollback, entry and rebuy fees,
refunds, immutable entry provenance, privileges and no retroactive cut. The
existing promo workflow runs and retains this proof.

The migration can install before custody wiring while enforcement is off;
native proof removes the custody relation during installation and exercises
the disabled path. Programme activation still requires all debit/ticket custody
producers and the remaining acceptance criteria to be installed and verified.

The three effective/payable views are static source declarations guarded by
their original view fingerprints. The existing union-calendar native fixture
loads their actual eligibility helpers and cold dependencies before those same
views, preserving its nine real money-door checks.
