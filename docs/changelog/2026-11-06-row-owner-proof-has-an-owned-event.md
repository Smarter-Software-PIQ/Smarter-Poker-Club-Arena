# Row-owner delivery has an owned event

The production test joined both club membership channels but timed out waiting for natural cash activity to update club_members. A quiet floor cannot prove whether either owner receives committed rows.

The existing reserved-account creator now prepares one additional zero-balance target for this proof, without exporting its identity over the main observer. The observer changes only that fixture's nickname through the normal audited member-record UI, verifies the save response, and requires both channel owners to receive rows. After unmounting one owner it saves another nickname and requires the retained owner to receive another row. The original nickname is restored in finally, and the workflow always invokes canonical fixture cleanup. Staff self-edit refusal, all delivery assertions, participation guards and deadlines remain intact.
