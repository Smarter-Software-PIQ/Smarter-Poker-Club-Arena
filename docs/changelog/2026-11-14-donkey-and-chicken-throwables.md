# Donkey and chicken table throwables

The donkey arrives with a sideways grin and turns toward the seat for one sustained bray and three shorter replies. Its two approved gray donkey expressions share a transparent, high-resolution atlas. The lower muzzle moves with the calls. Reduced motion uses the still grin, and the picker uses the same portrait.

The chicken previously had only a soft arrival pop. A recorded hen now calls during its head bobs and wide-open beak pose. Both animals use the existing throwable audio scheduler, mute settings, and AAC and Opus delivery.
