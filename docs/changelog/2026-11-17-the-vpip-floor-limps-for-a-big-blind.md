# The Table VPIP Floor Limps For A Big Blind, And Nothing Else (vpipFloorLimpOnly, Default Off; Hold'em 20% Tables Only)

Owner ruling, 2026-11-17 14:34 Chicago (audit finding F-10): "Table VPIP must limp fold for a bb. smh nothing else if there vpip drops."

What the floor did before: under its aim (60% on a 50% PLO4/PLO5 table in limp mode, 70.6% on PLO6 and every other non-hold'em variant, 29.5% on 20% hold'em) the table's VPIP floor widened every preflop bar, called any preflop price up to 6 big blinds and 15% of the stack, raises included, and in PLO4/PLO5 limp mode kept some calls of raises. In the journaled builds 28372c9a and 3529e866 the call-down fired 1,942 times, 981 of them calls of a raise, all with the Horse's own judged VPIP between 50% and 59.3%: the code met its own three-hand buffer, and the ruling now forbids those calls.

With the new option `vpipFloorLimpOnly` on, on a floored cash table of a governed variant (tournaments, All-In-Or-Fold, bomb pots and Lightning untouched) every decision is the one the Horse makes without a floor, except one thing: a Horse whose own judged VPIP is under its line (the floor plus the approved three-hand buffer) limps a hand it would fold, in a pot nobody has raised, for at most the big blind (the small blind completes; the big blind checks free). Which hands limp is priced per decision (owner 15:30: never blanket). Raised after the limp, the hand plays as it would without a floor. Above the line, or with no judged sample yet, the floor does nothing. The floor, window, warning, grace, eviction and buffer are unchanged.

Which variants: only hold'em (the 20% floor). The governed variants are a declared table in the code (`VPIP_FLOOR_LIMP_ONLY_VARIANTS`), filled from a declared measurement. On hold'em a limp-only floor evicted no Horse (0 of 8 sittings, 0 at a full table of Horses) and removed every floor call of a raise. On the 50% PLO5 and PLO6 tables it cannot work: more than half of a Horse's first decisions face a raise, where the ruling gives the floor nothing, so limping every other hand tops out at about 51% VPIP against a 50% floor judged on 30 hands; every measured sitting was evicted (8 of 8). PLO4 30% evicted 2 of 8. Those variants, and the ones not measured, are left out: with the option on they play exactly as today, including the floor's calls of raises. Fixing those tables needs an owner decision about the floor or the eviction; the option does not make it.

Default off. Activation is an option-switch row citing `docs/horse-option-authority.d/vpipFloorLimpOnly.md`; rollback is the same row off. Off, every decision is byte-identical to before (tests in `server/src/engine/HorseVpipFloorLimpOnly.test.ts`).
