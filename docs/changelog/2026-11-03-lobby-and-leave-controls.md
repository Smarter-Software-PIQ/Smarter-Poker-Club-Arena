# Lobby And Leave Controls

Repeated selection of Cash Games or Tournaments now preserves the open catalogue. Fixed-limit games are hidden from All until Include Limit is enabled or the Limit tab is selected. When shown with other cash games, FLH/FLO8 remain below them regardless of stakes or heading sort; NLH and PLO remain available by default. Selected controls receive a distinct lit and underlined state.

Rebuy/add-on artwork fits its bay; Turbo has a complete turbine. Classic and Lightning use centered, complete silver-spade and blue-bolt chips, and Stand Up & Leave has a chip in its circular well. The NLH preview uses a transparent exterior and centered two-column details. Lightning results use the existing opaque console. Hand review has four equal footer controls: Close, Link, Share, Replay, preserving their original handlers while removing the upper copy controls.

Tournament leave acknowledgements retain their tournament classification through the service and page, so a successful sit-out preserves the action tab and suppresses turn attention. Explicit Lightning leave stops matchmaking and folds only its captured hand through the existing server pre-action API; it never retries a fold into a newer hand. Pending pre-action retries are superseded on leaving. A refused leave restores attention, and a new buy-in or sit-in clears suppression.

This is a client release using existing engine APIs. Higher cash stakes, horse eligibility and unlimited fixed-limit buy-ins are separate database/engine work and are not established by this change. Unit, source-contract and mobile browser evidence is retained in the task checkpoint; protected publication and live proof are required before declaring this delivered.
