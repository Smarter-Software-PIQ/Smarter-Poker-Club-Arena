# Action Controller And Configurable Bet Presets

The bet-sizing controller uses the owner's selected faceted silver and blue artwork, with a full-width confirmation plate and no Back button. Its vertical slider floats above the right edge. The existing table-scoped outside-tap and Escape dismissal remain; dismissing never submits a wager. Nine-max tables use a taller rail that may overlap the adjacent seat while open. Previous-hand and time-bank controls remain unmounted by the existing raise-mode owner.

Table Studio now offers five free Action Controller colors: Arena Blue, Emerald, Ruby, Amethyst and Gold. Table Settings and account Table & Display settings share that same selector plus four editable preflop multipliers and four editable postflop pot percentages. The preflop defaults remain 2.5X, 3X, 3.5X and 4X; postflop defaults are 33%, 50%, 75% and 100%. POT and All In remain dedicated controls. Percentages apply after the flop even when facing a bet, using the pot after calling. Legal minimums, pot-limit ceilings, actual stack thresholds and wager units remain enforced by the existing sizing path. Number-key presets now use the same configured values and the actual open-panel state.

Preferences reuse the existing account-scoped, revision-checked user_page_preferences owner, with its cache, ordered writes, pending intent recovery and cross-component updates. No schema or engine change is needed. Painted assets use blank faces with fitted live text; optimized WebP derivatives total about a quarter megabyte.

Validation and publication receipts belong to the task checkpoint on SSD2. The owner explicitly requires review of all five colors before publication and the global replacement. This change must not merge to the publishing branch until that approval is received. Development previews are not production evidence.
