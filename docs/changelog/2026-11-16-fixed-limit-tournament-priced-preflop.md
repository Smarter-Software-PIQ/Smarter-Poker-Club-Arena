## `HorseDecideOpts.fixedLimitPricedPreflopTournament` (read `=== true`)

At fixed-limit (flh, flo8) TOURNAMENT tables only (`HorseFixedLimitPreflop.fixedLimitTournamentPricedPreflopIntent`, applied in `decidePreflopV7` after the cash version, which is never built at a tournament table):

- First in (no raise, limper or caller, not the big blind): a persona fold or check becomes a raise when the hand sits inside the seat's reference share (the cash `FIXED_LIMIT_OPEN_REFERENCE`, persona swing at most 5%) divided by hero's bubble factor (`HorseLogic.heroBubbleFactor`, IcmModel on the trusted tournament context; 1 = chip EV), scaled by 0.8 + 0.2 x min(1, (stackBB - 10) / 15) under 25 big blinds. A persona open is kept; only above bubble factor 1.15 does an open outside the share at that factor fold. The flat `riskAdd` survival premium is not added again.
- A blind facing exactly one raise (no limper, no caller, raiser not all in): a persona fold becomes a call when the realized equity against the opener's reference range (cash calibration, flh 0.65 / flo8 0.55, 0.85 per player behind) covers the raw pot price times the bubble factor.
- Stands aside, the persona's answer standing, with a receipt naming why: 10 big blinds or less, All-In-Or-Fold, a locked satellite seat (V37 `satelliteRead`), and a context that gives no bubble factor. - Under the option at a tournament table the phase12 pack's round-3 first-in entry rules retain the reference in fixed limit, as the cash version does at cash tables.
