# A satellite seat never waits on promo winnings

On 2026-11-18 23:02 UTC two satellites into the 25 Chip Bankroll Builder (30e72de6, 6cfaaadc) reached their qualifier boundary with 20 and 19 players and could not settle for 68 minutes. Each seat award writes a `tournament_refund_entitlements` row; its trigger ran `fn_ca_promo_profit_finish_entry_fee`, which ran `fn_ca_promo_profit_settle` for the seat holder inside the settlement. All 39 qualifiers held promo grants; that evaluation alone measured 10.4 s and 9.7 s per satellite on production, so the statement could not fit service_role's 8 s and was cancelled about 500 times, each attempt holding the finish lane and both events' lanes. Nothing was half-applied: every attempt rolled back whole, and both satellites settled exactly once at 00:10 UTC after the executor's 45 s deadline (20261118233000) was installed.

The 45 s deadline lets the settlement finish but it still spends 10 s and more holding the target's lane on promo arithmetic. `fn_ca_promo_profit_finish_entry_fee` now returns for a `satellite_seat` charge right after its fee is consumed. The settle only releases winnings, so nothing locked can get out; the hourly sweep (`sp_ca_promo_profit_hourly_sweep`) and the cash-out path settle the account. Paid entries are unchanged.
