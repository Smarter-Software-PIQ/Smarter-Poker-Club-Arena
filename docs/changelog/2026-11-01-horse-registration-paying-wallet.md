# Horse registration uses its paying wallet and advances past refusals

A tournament Horse with several club memberships was judged on its lowest balance, even when the ordinary registration door charged a different funded wallet. Live Micah Nakamura had 5.63 chips in an unrelated membership and 9,727.40 in the default paying wallet. The new service-only read resolves exactly the same default, funded fallback and existing-entry wallet as the ordinary entry wrapper. It returns that balance without charging or reserving funds. Existing bankroll multiples and atomic purchase guards remain authoritative; balances are never combined and the richest wallet is not substituted when the actual default can pay.

The bounded candidate slice also included deferred purchases, and answered per-Horse refusals cleared the refusal history. A stable first page could therefore keep every later eligible candidate out. Backoff now filters the rotated queue before slicing; per-Horse refusals retain their growing existing backoff and successful admissions clear it. Event closure, full-field and freeze answers still stop the pass. Unexpected registration exceptions reach the existing error reporter.

The database overlay selector also divided the regular guarantee by gross buy-in, incorrectly counting bounty value as regular prize funding. It now uses the ordinary entry split: the Funday PKO contributes20 from a50Chip charge and needs250 funded entries for its5000 guarantee, rather than112. A qualified preimage patch preserves the installed unlimited-admission ABI, scope/window predicates and function metadata; drift aborts the migration.

Service regressions reproduce both failures before their corrections. The native overlay regression reproduces112 before the patch and250 after it, while preserving ordinary-event sizing and capacity. Native PostgreSQL qualification exercises the maintained default/fallback/stamped-wallet resolvers, standalone scope, duplicate inputs, unknown balances and service-only permissions. It runs from the existing Horse registration accounting chain. No new scheduler, financial mutation path or publisher is introduced. CHECKPOINT-horse-registration-audit.md records delivery and live verification separately.

Each event also receives a full SHA256 permutation of candidates seeded by its random tournament UUID and UTC hour. The former shared ID ring had only N rotations and could repeat complete cohorts across events. The new selection remains stable for retries, preserves every eligible candidate and ticket priority, and allows ordinary overlap between events. It changes the lineup, not the randomized field-size and arrival planners already on protected main.
