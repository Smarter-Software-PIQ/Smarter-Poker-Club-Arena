# Keep Nine-Seat Phone Players Around the Rail

Nine-seat portrait tables now place the six side chairs in three evenly spaced, mirrored rows. The two top players sit above the table with their nameplates at the top edge. Shortening the felt from the top makes room for their portraits and preserves the bottom seat and action controls.

Players keep their rail positions across changes of hand, action and wager. Hidden cards, captions and bets fit around those positions. The browser check now detects a side player drifting off the rail or a top player extending down onto the table.
