# Classic games retain waiting lists and grow beyond one table

Physically full Classic games now request eligible Horses for their real waiting list regardless of the random occupancy shape or immediate seating capacity. Queue-only evaluation retains pause, schedule, bankroll and game-limit checks; it cannot buy a seat or advertise forecast buyers. Actual waiting rows drive expansion. A fleet pass without an ask preserves existing waiting Horses until normal patience expiry. Full-ring queue demand reaches the existing four-player feeder opening threshold.

A subset of games selected for a second table can open a third. The database controller and Horse cash admission use matching deterministic choices. The normal full-floor requirement, queue offers, bankroll checks, seated-player exclusions, human priority, and Lightning policy remain in place.

Validation and delivery evidence are recorded in CHECKPOINT-classic-horse-waitlists.md. This entry does not claim production adoption.
