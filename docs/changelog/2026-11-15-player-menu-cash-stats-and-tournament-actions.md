The Player Menu opens from an opponent’s seat. Its close button sits in the upper-right corner. In cash games the app owner and active upline agents can open Player Stats above Add Note to see the selected player’s open games, buy-ins, settled results, hands, VPIP and PFR. Missing hand results display as pending.

All In or Fold tournaments expose a direct All In button. Attempts to check, call or size a raise explain which two actions are available.
