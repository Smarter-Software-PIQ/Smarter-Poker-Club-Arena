# The Midway Free Buy Rotation And Promo Funds Are Watched

Two things went unseen on 2026-11-05 until someone happened to look: Saturday's 8:30 AM Midway Morning Free Buy was never created (a stuck spawn claim) and Sunday's was created in the old PKO format; and the Midway promo wallet's runway was being read two contradictory ways (about 90 days counting only Free Buys, about 8 days counting every outflow but no income).

`fn_sp_midway_free_buy_watch()` (migration `20261105183114`) checks every rotation slot of the next 72 hours (8:30 AM regular, 12:30 PM PKO, 4:30 PM All-In-Or-Fold, 8:30 PM Mystery Bounty, 12:30 AM PKO, Chicago time) and the promo wallet's runway at its last-7-day net spend:

| Rule                             | Warning                                                                                                                               | Critical (paged)                             |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| `midway_free_buy_missing`        | a slot in the next 48 hours has no live event                                                                                         | the slot starts within 12 hours              |
| `midway_free_buy_wrong_format`   | the event is in the wrong format (players already entered)                                                                            | no entries yet and it starts within 12 hours |
| `midway_free_buy_wrong_settings` | the format is right but the guarantee (250 / 0 / 500 / 0 / 0), add-ons (Morning and Prime Time only), 1-chip rebuy or zero fee is not | no entries yet and it starts within 12 hours |
| `midway_promo_runway_low`        | under 30 days of promo funds                                                                                                          | under 7 days                                 |

The only exceptions are the three Thursday 2026-11-05 events that already had entries when the rotation went live (8:30 AM Morning, 4:30 PM Afternoon, 8:30 PM Prime Time): their players keep the PKO terms they joined under. They are named by id, so the exception ends with them. A slot 48 to 72 hours out is checked once the board has spawned it (about three days ahead). The items are appended to `fn_sp_digest_attention`, so they appear under Needs Your Attention in the 7:00 AM Daily Operations Digest and reach the Production Alerts fleet like every other rule, and `fn_sp_critical_scan` pages the critical ones by email and text within five minutes, once each. Both functions are patched in place against their exact live preimage.

The runway is `balance / ((7-day spend - 7-day income) / 7)`. Spend is every promo-wallet outflow except category `promo`, which is discretionary: the weekly Union Promo Disbursement pays out half of the wallet each week (`20261016132743`), so it can never empty it, and one-time credits such as the 2026-11-04 relaunch 25s. Those are reported in the item's detail, not projected. Income is the Bad Beat Jackpot promo share and unplayed Free Buy returns. The BBJ share is written to the ledger with no destination label; on 2026-11-05 the wallet rose by exactly the 2.92-chip BBJ leg at 20:00 UTC and nothing else, which confirms it lands in this wallet. Guarantee overlays are paid from the union bank, not this wallet, so they are not in it. On 2026-11-05 at 20:04 UTC: balance 146,896, 7-day recurring spend 20,264, income 115,019, weekly disbursement 125,413; recurring income exceeds recurring spend, so no runway item is raised.
