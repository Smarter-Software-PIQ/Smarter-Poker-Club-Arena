# Horses Belong To One Assigned Club

Owner order, 2026-11-02 (Dan): Deep Stack Society gets 500 horses, The Felt Society 500, KINGS CLUB none. Horses may no longer play in two clubs. Every chip a horse holds in a club it leaves goes back to that club's main bank. Every horse restarts at exactly 10,000, the clubs funded from the Mint only for a verified shortfall. Kings Club keeps showing +1,500 members and +233 active. Follow-up the same day: the split lives in source and schema with a fixed manifest, is enforced at every admission door, runs only after the week's rakeback is paid under verified maintenance conditions, never as an owner impersonation, and Kings Club's additions are labelled separately from its real counts.

## What Shipped

Migration `20261102141229_horses_belong_to_one_assigned_club.sql` installs inert (phase `inactive`); nothing is enforced and no chip moves until the operation runs.

- **Manifest.** `ca_horse_club_assignments`: the 1,000 canonical horses (`ca_horse_fleet_register.retired_at IS NULL` on 2026-11-02), 500 per club, literal rows, sha256 `22af823bbec6a2dc108fa0ebdc1063589e095abfba095d85d08f762325a0c270` over the sorted `horse_id:D|F` lines, asserted at install. Rule: a horse with a Felt membership and no Deep Stack membership stays in Felt; every other horse goes to Deep Stack; the 18 Felt-only horses with the lowest `md5('horse-club-split-20261102:'||id)` move to Deep Stack so both clubs hold 500. Live at reservation: 416 Deep Stack only, 518 Felt only, 62 both, 4 neither.
- **Enforcement** inside three existing trigger bodies (no trigger DDL on a hot table): `fn_ca_reject_automated_user_club_row` (membership, live seat, tournament entry), `fn_stamp_seat_club` (the stamped wallet club of a live seat) and `fn_guard_membership_lifecycle_write` (rejoin). Once `enforcing`: a horse holds a membership only in its assigned club; plays only where the host is its assigned club or a union containing it; is funded only from its assigned club (covers rebuys, top-ups and union routing, which all charge the seat or entry club); KINGS CLUB admits no horse; a horse off the manifest is admitted nowhere. While the operation runs (`draining`, `executing`) every new horse seat, entry, membership and rejoin is refused.
- **Operation.** `fn_ca_horse_club_split_preflight()` lists blockers; `fn_ca_horse_club_split_begin_drain(op, operator, mint_cap)`; `fn_ca_horse_club_split_execute(op, max_rows)` runs in resumable chunks: sweep, join, mint, fund, enforce. It refuses to move a chip until the 2026-10-26..2026-11-02 weekly payout has released with nothing owed, the fleet is disabled, and no horse holds a live seat, a registered/playing entry, a pending cashout, escrow, an issued ticket or an unreconciled held/locked/promo/credit/diamond balance. Each wallet leg mirrors the Club Bank claim/send journal exactly (declared ledger, correlation, `chip_transactions` row keyed on a deterministic op id) under the service identity, never an owner session; replays are refused by the existing unique indexes and this operation's receipt rows. Agent wallets earned in the assigned club are untouched; agent wallets in a club the horse leaves are claimed into that club's bank. Departures use the records-retained lifecycle (`departed`, status `suspended`), never a DELETE. Horse downlines of a departing horse agent go direct to the club; a human downline stops the operation. The Mint is called only for the shortfall and only within the operator's `mint_cap`.
- **Kings Club display.** Its blended +1,000 member boost is set to 0, so every count it shows is real. `club_display_labeled_additions` holds +1,500 / +233 labelled "Bonus", served by `fn_club_display_labeled_additions(uuid[])`; the lobby card prints each as its own labelled line under MEMBERS and ACTIVE, never added into them.
- **Kings fleet policy.** Club-scoped `enabled = false`, `pause_new_seatings = true`.

## Evidence

Executed end to end on scratch PostgreSQL 16 with the live-shaped membership mix (stubbed ledger, mint and payout): preflight refused (payout, fleet); a horse seat was refused while draining; execute refused until both cleared, then completed in 13 chunked calls: 500/500/0 active memberships, every assigned wallet exactly 10,000, 22 joins, 1,187 departures, 1,965 wallet claims, 5 agent-wallet claims, 10 downlines released, banks + wallets + agent wallets conserved to the cent plus the minted shortfall, zero duplicate op ids, replay a no-op. Under enforcement: Kings membership and seat refused, cross-club membership refused, departed-club rejoin refused, union seat funded from the assigned club admitted and from another club refused, union entry paid by an unassigned club refused.
