# Cash rebuy minimum and Mikey earned-rake reporting

A zero-stack cash seat must fund the full table minimum, at least 40BB. The rebuy transaction accepted every positive amount; the add-on transaction and queued delivery enforced only the maximum. The original locked transactions now reject short purchases before debiting. Original queued delivery applies no short stack after a hand felts the player and returns the original purchase through its existing idempotent refund receipt. Existing funded top-ups on a nonzero stack remain available.

The table cashier enforces the same minimum and explains insufficient chips. Lightning rebuy presentation retains the table minimum instead of reducing it to remaining room.

Mikey receives the existing WooWoo standing 50% earned cash-rake reporting rule in KINGS CLUB, covering his recorded history. Both retain half of subsequent earned cash rake while KingFish receives the other half. These are reported earned-rake credits, not wallet transfers or changes to rakeback/commission contracts. WooWoo's prior VIP redemption and declared historical reporting events remain intact.

Regression evidence lives in CHECKPOINT-cash-rebuy-minimum.md. Native PostgreSQL tests use captured purchase functions and actual seat/wallet/pending writes, plus the maintained real fee/statistics reader harness. No active-player financial testing.
