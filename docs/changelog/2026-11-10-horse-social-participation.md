# Every Horse can occasionally chat and gesture

The personality generator previously assigned a permanent zero talking rate to 25% of identities and a permanent zero gesture rate to 45%. Those identities now use the existing quietest positive frequency. Other personalities, event selection, table permissions, pacing, hourly limits and charged-throw rules remain unchanged.

Regression coverage exercises formerly silent identities through chat and gesture producers, including thrown items separately from quick reactions. Deployment and natural live visual verification remain required.
