# Omaha wave two: the multiway discipline and the street plan layers together

Date: 2026-11-16. Lane: PLO Strategy. Integration of two default-off option sets on one head (no option turned on, no source default flipped): the multiway river call, river bluff and multiway weak-hand bet (`omahaMultiwayRiverCallNeedsStrength`, `omahaNoRiverBluffIntoValueLeader`, `omahaMultiwayWeakHandNoBet`) and the street plan's flop, three-way turn and preflop layers (`omahaStreetPlanFlop`, `omahaStreetPlanThreeWay`, `omahaPreflopStackPlan`).

## Precedence where they meet

The owner-ruled checks act first and are final (`omahaOopCheckRulesCore`, then the discipline checks inside `omahaOopCheckRules`); the street plan layers run only on a bet those let stand; `omahaOwnerRuledFinal` re-applies the discipline checks and `omahaWagerIsChecked` (which now asks the flop and three-way plans too) outermost. Among the new checks any check wins: a bet stands only if every active rule lets it stand. A three-way turn bet with one pair or less is covered by both `omahaMultiwayWeakHandNoBet` and `omahaStreetPlanThreeWay`; exactly one check receipt describes the final check: `omaha_multiway_weak_hand_bet_checked` when the weak-hand rule checks (the plan then never runs), `omaha_street_plan_three_way_checked` only on a bet the weak-hand rule let stand.

## Lazy big-draw pricing (no decision changes)

With every option on, a flop or turn check that an owner-ruled, discipline or street-plan check had made could still be priced by `omahaBigDrawAggression` (its equity count, about 100 ms on a PLO5 flop) for a bet the outermost rule then replaced by the check. The one wager the big-draw verdict can make is now fixed before any equity is counted; when a ruled check stands in the decision, `omahaOwnerRuledFinal` is asked (quietly) whether that exact wager would be replaced, and if so the equities are not counted and the check stands (receipt `omaha_big_draw_aggression_final_check_unpriced` in place of the bet and its `_vetoed` twin). Decisions are identical with every option on (19,200 timed decisions and the four-cell digests over three seeds, before and after).

## One exact count per turn decision (no decision or receipt changes)

The turn pricing rules each took the same exact count again: omahaCardImpact built every river's pair table, the street plan's turn layer (per bet size, and again from the river when its river verdict rebuilds the turn plan) and its three-way turn scored every sampled hand on every river card, and the facing-bet EV built its own river sets. `HorseOmahaRiverTables.ts` takes the count once per decision (the facing-bet EV's rank-pair method, the engine evaluator's values bit for bit): the unseen pairs' scores on the turn board and on every river card. Card impact reads its tables from it, the street plan and the three-way turn read each sampled hand's river score as its best pair there (the value scoreOmahaHi gives), and the facing-bet EV reads its river sets from it (its card order mapped). It is held for one decision only: cleared at the start and the end of every decision with the other per-decision memos. With every option on the PLO5 turn p99 falls from about 175 ms to about 34 ms and the PLO5 river p99 from about 80 ms to about 28 ms.

The lazy big-draw question is now asked at every check the rule would price, not only after a ruled check in the same decision: the bets an outermost check replaces are no longer priced anywhere (the receipt `omaha_big_draw_aggression_final_check_unpriced` replaces the bet and its `_vetoed` twin in those spots too).

## The public bet size with the pricing rules

With `omahaBetSizeByPublicState` on, the public size is final for every own bet that stands, so the rules that decide bet against check price that size: the street plan's turn, flop and three-way layers and omahaCardImpact ask the public size of the bet before they price it (the flop layer prices that one size, no smaller size: the public size would replace it), and a bet they keep carries it, so the public rule finds its own size (`omahaPublicSizedBet`). The decision's one size draw is taken once, on first use, and serves the pricing rules and the final size alike; it is held across the decision's nested decides and cleared when the outermost decision starts and ends. Where the public size commits the stack (60% of the chips or more), the plan prices the committing size, so its check wins before the size and no bet stands that the plan rejects at that size. The plans' records name the public size they priced (`publicSize`); the receipt validator re-runs a plan check at it (a recorded size beyond the seat's chips is refused). One receipt per rule: a plan check fires the plan's, a bet the size's. The sizing law (`aBetSizeIsPublicInformationOnly`) now admits the shared draw: it must be the size module's own stream, and every read of the option is an explicit true.

## The play styles, the big-draw and value wagers, and the 4-bet bar

The play styles (`horsePlayStyles`, personas2) act inside the engine's own choice (the pressure style's fold-equity prior and tie-break band in position); the owner-ruled, discipline and plan checks run after it in the wager rules and stay final for every style; one style receipt fires per decision. With the public bet size on, the big-draw wager and the strong-hand value bet and raise price the public size (the decision's shared draw) instead of being resized afterwards; off, they decide as before. The pressure style's 3-bet widening on the cutoff and button no longer widens its 4-bets: the 4-bet node reads today's tightness and aggression for that style.

## The multiway river call: the bettor's multiway value region

`omahaMultiwayRiverCallNeedsStrength` priced the bettor with his heads-up value region even when he bet into two or more players. The verdict now uses the derived multiway region `Vn = 1 - (1 - V1)^(1/n)` (`omahaMultiwayValueShare`: the weakest value hand is best against all n players as often as the heads-up one is against one, under the module's independence). No new constant; heads-up unchanged; the live facing-bet EV option is untouched. Of the 14 recorded multiway river non-nut calls the verdict priced as calls, four now fold (a nine-high flush with a player behind, trips on a double-paired board, bottom set at a pot-sized bet, top two pair on a four-flush board); full houses and the second-nut and queen-high flushes facing a bet and a call still call. The option stays off.
