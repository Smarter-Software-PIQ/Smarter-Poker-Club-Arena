# A Stuck Spawn Claim Repairs Itself

The scheduled spawner claims an occurrence's spawn key before it inserts the tournament. A spawn that died between the two left a claim with no tournament, and every later poll stood down on it, so that occurrence never existed. Six were on the board on 2026-11-05, including Saturday's 8:30 AM Midway Morning Free Buy.

The spawner's poll now sweeps claims that still have no tournament, at most every ten minutes (`server/src/services/orphanSpawnClaims.ts`). Age alone never clears one: a claim must be older than ten minutes AND have been seen orphaned by an earlier sweep at least ten minutes before, which proves no creation operation still owns it (the only owner is a spawner poll, single-flight in its process and frozen during an engine handover). A claim with exactly one tournament of its schedule created around it is linked to that tournament (only the back-link was lost); a claim with none is released so the next poll spawns the occurrence if it is still due, but only when its schedule still exists and is active (a disabled schedule's claims are left as they are); a claim with several is left alone and reported. Every write is conditional on `tournament_id` still being NULL. Law: `aStuckSpawnClaimRepairsItself.law.test.ts`.

`theMidwayRotationKeepsLocalTimeAcrossClockChanges.test.ts` pins the five Midway Free Buy slots to Chicago wall-clock time across the 2027-03-14 and 2027-11-07 changes, with the 12:30 AM slot on the date it starts.
