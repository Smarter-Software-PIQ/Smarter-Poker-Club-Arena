# Chicago member statistics controls

Player Record and Player Statistics date presets, captions and linked custom ranges use America/Chicago. Relative performance windows advance at Chicago midnight, including both daylight-saving transitions. The member ledger retains its explicit range-selection behavior.

The backend Chicago reporting migration must be installed before this client is published. No engine replacement or financial record mutation is required. Regression coverage retains date-only validation, device-zone independence, spring 23-hour and fall 25-hour boundaries, repeated fall hours and mounted-page rollover.
