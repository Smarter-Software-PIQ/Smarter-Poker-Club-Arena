# Omaha priced rules: every categorical PLO4/PLO5 cash rule gets a priced replacement (ten options, default off)

Date: 2026-11-17. Lane: PLO Strategy. Owner instruction 2026-11-17 11:49 America/Chicago: "there should not be any blanket rules, you need to coordinate and re write any and all "blanket rules" to be fully adaptable to real time decisions, a blanket rule shouldn't be allowed to effect a decision, they must all be done by math, gto, players hand or preceived range of hands as well as the opponents range of hand, equity, EV etc." Earlier the same day: the size of the bet must not make or break a decision.

The audit of the PLO4/PLO5 Horse decision path (2026-11-17) found rules that decide by a bet size, a chip count, a stack share, a hand category, a betting line or an opponent label. Each family of them now has a priced comparison behind its own option. Every option is read `=== true` and is default OFF; with an option off the categorical rule decides exactly as before (it is live production behaviour and stays in source until its replacement is evaluated and switched on). With an option on, in PLO4/PLO5 cash (one board, high only, no bomb pot, not Lightning), its categorical rule does not decide.

New files: `server/src/engine/HorseOmahaPricedMode.ts` (the mode of one decision and the continuous size inputs), `HorseOmahaPricedRules.ts` (the three comparisons, the one opponent-read interface, the mixing probability), `HorseOmahaPricedWiring.ts` (the rule sites and receipts), `HorseOmahaRuleSites.ts` (the register of categorical rules and option classes), `server/src/benchmark/OmahaPricedRangeCalibration.ts` (the range calibration scorer), `docs/OMAHA-RULE-PRECEDENCE.md` (the order the rules act in, in one place).

Nothing here is a second engine: the comparisons are assembled from `HorseOmahaFacingBetEv` (exact counts), `HorseOmahaStrongHandValue` (the raise model, without its hand gate), `HorseOmahaBigDrawAggression` (sampled shares) and `HorseOmahaCardImpact` (the bet and check response model). Every opponent range is drawn from the cards the Horse does not hold. Every opponent rate is a posterior mean that shrinks toward the population with few opportunities; no sample threshold and no label is read. The per-street, per-texture opponent read plugs into one function (`omahaPricedSetStreetRatesSource`); until it is delivered the pooled read answers.

The worker receipt: a raise that the priced comparison turned into a call is admitted only for a decision whose own `omahaTurnRiverRaiseIsPriced` or `omahaBigBetCallsArePriced` was on, from the same gate candidates the client builds for the PLO6 gate (the delivered switch row, the request's options, the previous delivery). With both off the validator is exactly as before.

Not taken over: the out-of-position turn rule's facing half (call with nut outs at the price, fold with none). Its priced replacement, the facing-bet EV verdict, folded strong draws and a set class to turn bets at prices their equity beats in the counterfactual replay of the audited hands (4 of 4 judged changes wrong). It is listed in `OMAHA_OPEN_RULE_SITES` and moves when that verdict's field factor and turn realization are fixed (WooWoo audit RC-02b).

The hardening: `HorseOmahaNoBlanketRule.law.test.ts` fails the build when an Omaha option in the ledger is not classed (priced, categorical with its priced option, open, outside), when a registered rule site no longer hands its decision to the priced rule, when a registered categorical rule fires its receipt on the corpus with every priced option on, or when a size line moves the facing-bet range by a step with the input options on.

Range calibration. Every turn and river bet a person made into a Horse in PLO4/PLO5 cash from 2026-11-13 19:40Z to 2026-11-17 19:40Z (5,379 hands with a person seated; every player's cards from the hand facts, so no showdown selection) was replayed through the facing-bet comparison. Heads-up (995 bets) the comparison predicted a Horse win share of 0.20 to 0.22 against river bets of 40 to 110 percent of the pot where the Horses won 0.29 (n 294), and 0.27 against turn bets of 70 to 110 percent where they won 0.34 (n 337). The continuous range now takes the bluff prior that matches the measured win share, size row by size row, shrunk toward the stepped prior by its 8 pseudo-bets (`OMAHA_PRICED_MEASURED_BLUFF`). The showdown-only sample the first calibration used overstated the bettor's value share (0.69 at showdown against 0.58 over all bets). Where a card-impact comparison has no read of the opponent it takes a person's measured bet rate when checked to (flop 0.289, turn 0.343, river 0.319, with counts) instead of the stated 0.45.

One measured constant replaces an unsourced one: the share of a bettor's range that raises again when his turn or river bet is raised is 0.26 (14 of 54 heads-up raises, PLO4/PLO5 cash, 2026-11-10 to 2026-11-15), not the card-impact model's stated 0.10.

All nine on together lost. A diagnostic on the v2 seeds (PLO5 field, 80 sessions) found the bluff share counted twice when `omahaBluffShareFromBetRate` and `omahaFacingBetRangeIsContinuous` both act (dropping either gained 6.8 +/- 1.9 and 6.5 +/- 1.7); that is fixed (`omahaPricedBluffReferenceRatio`) and closed most of the human-like loss, but the all-on configuration still FAILS, at Horse-only tables above all. Its remaining cause is not found. No option is switched on by this change, and none should be switched on together with the others until it is.
