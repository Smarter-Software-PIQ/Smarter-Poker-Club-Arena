Tournament Horses take at least two seconds of their ordinary action clock to respond to an all-in. A fixed 500 of the 1,000 registered Horse accounts may use quick checks and folds in ordinary tournament spots. The other accounts take a varied beat before checking or folding. A queued Horse Check/Fold clears when a shove arrives. Calls and folds continue to be chosen by the same poker policy. Worker rejections and fallback actions retain their own receipts.
