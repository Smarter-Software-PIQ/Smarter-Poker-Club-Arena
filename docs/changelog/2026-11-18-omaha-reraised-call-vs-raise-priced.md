# 2026-11-18: a priced call-or-raise facing a re-raise (omahaReraisedCallVsRaisePriced, default OFF)

New option, default OFF (read `=== true`); with it unset or false every decision is byte-identical. PLO4/PLO5 cash, high
only, one board, no bomb pot, not Lightning, preflop, facing two or more raises, the re-raiser not all in. PLO6,
tournaments and Hi-Lo are untouched.

Before: no preflop step could turn a call into a raise and EV(raise) was never computed (CALL-VS-RAISE analysis: of 97
folds the re-raise fix turned into calls, 26 were raises on every sensitivity corner).

With the option on, a standing call, or a reference raise the re-raise range rules lowered to a call or a fold, is priced
(`HorseOmahaReraisedRaisePriced.ts`):

- EV(call): the calling rule's `omahaReraisedCallEv` (its constants, not copied).
- EV(raise) = P(fold) x pot + P(call) x [R at the stack-to-pot ratio left x equity against his calling band x final pot x
  (1 - rake) - chips added] + P(re-raise) x max(-chips added, EV of continuing).
- The three rates: the re-raiser's own answers to a raise of his re-raise (`HorseOmahaReraiseResponse`), as a posterior
  against a measured population prior (production hand_history, 30 days, PLO4/PLO5 cash hands with a human: 3-bet
  facing a 4-bet 0.014 / 0.744 / 0.242 on 211 spots of 23 players; 4-bet facing a 5-bet 0.087 / 0.826 / 0.087 on 23
  spots). Never an assumed fold rate.
- Equities against his range, his calling band and his re-raising band on ONE run of the calling rule's sampler (1,600
  samples), so the two actions share their random numbers and the standard error of the difference is known.
- It raises only when EV(raise) > 0 and EV(raise) beats the standing action by more than twice that standard error. It
  never lowers a decision. Nothing is keyed to a hand class, a position, a bet size or an account.

Every number is on the decision (`omahaReraisedRaise`: the spot, the band samples and both EVs). Receipts:
`omaha_reraised_raise_priced_raise`, `omaha_reraised_raise_priced_call`,
`omaha_reraised_raise_priced_reference_raise_kept`.

Switch: registered; the batch whitelist follow-up is regenerated by the integrator for the cutover (no migration on this branch; not
installed). Authority: `docs/horse-option-authority.d/omahaReraisedCallVsRaisePriced.md`. League cards
`plo4_omaha_reraised_call_vs_raise_priced`, `plo5_omaha_reraised_call_vs_raise_priced` (not run).

Rollback: `fn_ca_horse_option_switch_set('omahaReraisedCallVsRaisePriced', 'off', ...)`.

## Revised after review (REVIEW-59ea78d08f3.md), still default OFF
