# Hand-gap root causes: Mystery Bounty & add-on break owed waits, runbook classification, and boundary-balance parking (2026-11-22, incident 1e532243)

## What kept paging `fn_ca_hand_gap_watch` on serving engine `c2d6e45e3bcc`

Even with `#11520`, `#11555`, `#11562`, and `#11647` live on `c2d6e45e3bcc` (booted 01:01 UTC), incident `1e532243` fired from 02:26 to 04:46 UTC (`fe33b45f`, 267 slow `all_in_showdown` hands in 02:30-02:40 UTC) and refired at 05:01 UTC. Engine logs and incident inspection established six root causes and sibling bugs across the engine and database:

1. **Snapshot-stale `parked` set in `TournamentManager.balanceParkedGapSources()`:** `parked` was collected once before `await takePreparedBoundaryBoard` / `await liveTournamentTableIdsWithPlayers` / `await loadBalancerTables` (1-4 s on 56-table MTT `2d444aeb`). Fenced sources that parked during those reads (`cb2d8fa8` parked 02:34:36 before 02:34:39 read finished -> 26.4 s park / 33.5 s gap; `cbe6cd62` parked 02:34:59.603, 63 ms into a 2.3 s read -> 28.7 s park / 32.2 s gap) were absent from `parked`, so they were neither moved nor released and sat frozen through a full elimination sweep after `drainBoundaryBalance` yielded on `boundaryBalanceOwesSweep`.
2. **Serial board-read round trips in `TournamentManager.loadBalancerTables()`:** `tables`, `table_seats`, and `tournament_players` were queried serially instead of in parallel via `Promise.all`, multiplying boundary-balance wait latency ~3x under `EventDbBulkhead` load.
3. **Parked gap-balance sources stranded on sweep settle / budget-expired yield:** `onEliminationSweepSettled()` only drained if `boundaryBalanceRequested` was still true (missing an already-parked fenced source whose flag was cleared), and `checkTableBalance()`'s budget-expired yield always picked `sourceTables[0]` rather than preferring an already-parked source.
4. **Mystery Bounty chest reveals graded as unintended delay:** Between 04:45 and 05:00 UTC, tournament `f99647e1` had six `MYSTERY CHEST` reveals (`a44f0266` 20.26 s, 20.26 s, 20.34 s; `f6bfc10b` 20.55 s). `mysteryChestHoldMs()` (~14.3 s + coalesce) is an intentional Dan 2026-08-21 hold, but `fn_ca_hand_gap_unintended_delay` did not exclude pairs with a reserved `tournament_bounty_awards` row.
5. **Add-on breaks graded as unintended delay:** `scheduleAddOnBreak` holds dealing via `holdDealingUntil(addon_period_ends_at)` for the final `addon_break_minutes` (1..10) without setting `break_started_at`/`break_ends_at`, so gaps < 60 s across an add-on break were graded as unintended delay.
6. **`smarter_private.reliability_runbook_hand_gap_classification` bugs:** Mislabeled tournament busts in `is_rebuy`/`is_reentry` tournaments as intended `'rebuy'` (143 real unintended delays in `2d444aeb`) even though tournament tables never pause for rebuys (`ServerTableEngineDealing.ts`), omitted `'mystery_bounty'` and `'addon_break'`, and scanned 10 minutes instead of 15 minutes.

## Fixes & hardening

- **`server/src/tournament/TournamentManager.ts`:** Re-collects newly-parked fenced sources after the board read in `balanceParkedGapSources()` (moving needed ones and releasing unneeded ones in the same pass, plus lifting still-dealing unneeded fences via `releaseGapFencesNotIn`); runs the three independent chunked reads in `loadBalancerTables()` concurrently via `Promise.all`; drains any already-parked fenced source in `onEliminationSweepSettled()`; prefers an already-parked source on budget-expired yield in `checkTableBalance()`.
- **`supabase/migrations/20261122054826_mystery_bounty_and_addon_break_are_owed_waits.sql`:** Single-transaction anchored edit to `fn_ca_hand_gap_unintended_delay` excluding `tournament_bounty_awards` chest reveals and the clamped final `addon_break_minutes` add-on break window; replaces `smarter_private.reliability_runbook_hand_gap_classification` to scan 15 minutes, classify `mystery_bounty` and `addon_break`, and classify `rebuy` only for cash busts.
- **Regression laws & proofs:** Five new engine law tests in `server/src/tournament/aBoundaryMoveIsPlannedUnderTheHold.law.test.ts`; Phase 4 PostgreSQL 16 proof in `scripts/ci/test-hand-gap-watch.py` (55/55 checks); static/contract law in `tests/mystery-bounty-and-addon-break-are-owed-waits.law.test.ts`.
