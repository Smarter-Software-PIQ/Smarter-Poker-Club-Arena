# PLO witness keeps an arriving buy-in sheet

The funded production witness reached its ordinary assigned table, then its chair click waited behind loading artwork and the purchase sheet opened from the committed offer. The witness now accepts that actual visible sheet if the outstanding click is refused. It never forces a click through the overlay, requests another admission, or purchases twice. A click failure without the real sheet still fails.

The regression renders the real BuyInModal and fails before this correction; it also preserves the original ordinary-chair, already-open-sheet and missing-sheet checks. Engine, schema and client runtime are unchanged. The failed attempt made no purchase and its 400-chip funding was claimed back through the normal wallet owner with both ledger legs verified.
