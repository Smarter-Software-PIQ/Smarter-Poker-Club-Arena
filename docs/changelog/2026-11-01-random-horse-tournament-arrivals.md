# Random Horse tournament registrations

Freerolls and Free Buys can build fields up to six hours before start. Regular MTTs begin with a small random group an hour ahead, then receive an increasingly busy random trickle toward start. A regular prestart field stays small independently of guarantee size; the complete funded plan continues through late registration. Recurring publication lead is one hour so the field can actually begin building then.

Each random tournament UUID seeds stable field and arrival choices, shared by discovery and registration rechecks across retry/restart. Free entry is determined from the actual initial charge or explicit Free Buy contract; a paid all-bounty event with zero net prize share remains paid. A compatible SQL timing helper updates the financial admission window without changing the ordinary funding, duplicate, capacity, freeze, lifecycle or late-registration safeguards. Existing entries are never removed to alter displayed totals.

Focused regression and native PostgreSQL tests cover both windows, gradual arrivals, no hundred-Horse regular opening target, classification, capacity, funding and replay behavior. Delivery and remaining live evidence are recorded in CHECKPOINT-random-horse-entry.md; merged source does not establish runtime adoption.
