# Player Stats

Cash and Tournament reports use the lobby's chrome frame, with compact metric rows and space for the sample, rate and hand-history action at phone widths. The Analysis Window displays its four choices in one row. The selected club and date range and the evidence behind each reported statistic remain visible. The mast subtitle and historical club-ledger notice have been removed.

Background refreshes preserve the current readout while new results arrive. Personal results respond to completed hands and the player's tournament entry transactions. Private rake events refresh the applicable ledger. Repeated connection notices and unrelated wallet activity no longer cause the dashboard to switch into its loading state.
