# Club Data Calendar And Totals

The everyday Club Data screen now offers 1 Day, 7 Days, 14 Days and a Calendar. The existing Pacific accounting day determines the reporting range, including across clock changes. Saved inclusive ranges and older exclusive-end bookmarks retain their meaning. A custom selection shows its dates without local boundary times.

Club Totals shows Total Winnings, MTT Winnings, Fee and Games in that order. Each role keeps its authorized figures and drilldowns. The Game Reports heading links whole-club viewers to their detailed report with the same filters. Club Data retires its header subtitle, report shortcut and inline reconciliation detail.
