# Value bet a checked-to middle or top Omaha full house

Two PLO6 cash rivers ended with a legal check after multiple opponents had checked to a middle boat. Releasing the prior-aggressor initiative restriction still allowed a later strategy to select a check. The final wager rule now prices that check as a half-pot value bet within the controller's bounds and passes it through the normal legalizer. The predicate covers the existing high-only, single-board, non-trip-board PLO4/5/6 domain and requires a prior opponent check and a physically possible weaker full house. Previous value-bet sizes and opening decisions retain their ordinary strategy. The two retained public river orders and own cards exercise the final decision in regression tests. Half pot is a policy sizing choice, with no claim of solved EV.
