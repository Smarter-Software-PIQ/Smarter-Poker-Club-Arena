# Data Explains BBJ And Financial Completeness

Simple Data, Advanced Data and invoice-based Rake snapshots show BBJ collected,
awards paid and net after awards from their existing authoritative snapshot.
Unqualified BBJ history displays unavailable amounts and missing dates, never
zero. Cash and tournament reconciliation expands in place and retains the same
dates, scope and filters as the headline. Neither residual is called BBJ or an
amount owed, and net after awards is explicitly not the current pool balance.

Visible financial status separates posted source totals from final billing.
Finality requires an explicit invoice basis, complete coverage, an explicitly
nonprovisional past window and no missing, failed, inexact or awaiting boundary
days. An awaiting day names its actual following midnight in Pacific time.
Unique game counting is explained; existing game and player drilldowns remain.

Rake CSV exports preserve the frozen reconciliation and coverage metadata and
refuse pages whose metadata changes. The existing API, refresh, authorization,
filter, export and presentation paths remain in place. No new polling or
financial writer is introduced.
