# Player Management Has No Promo Progress

Owner direction, November 17, 2026: promo progress belongs to the cashier, when a player is cashing out or requesting a cash out. It does not belong on Player Management.

The roster rows no longer print a "Check Promo Progress" button, the promo line or a Withdrawable Balance figure, and the roster makes no promo read of any kind. The cash-out request keeps its promo progress and its server-side promo guards unchanged. The member record's staff tools (promo unlock with a reason and source-review release) are unchanged, because they are the only place staff can release a held cash-out.

No migration. Validation: type check and the 33 roster and promo test files.
