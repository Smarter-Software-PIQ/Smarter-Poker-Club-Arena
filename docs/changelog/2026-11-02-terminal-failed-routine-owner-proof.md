# A terminal failed routine owner is released only by a recorded proof

The 04:00Z Phase1 cycle on 2026-11-02 (run 41379310363-1) refused cleanly inside the release transaction: the native heap census timed out at 59 s, rollback was proved, the sealed runtime was restored and the break thawed. Its cycle then stayed reserved with its recorded failure. After #9257 removed generic retirement, every later receiver refused on it, so one clean refusal blocked the release lane until a pinned resolution like #9231 was written by hand.

The existing transaction now runs `resolve-terminal-routine-failures` immediately after #9231's pinned disposition, under the actual engine lock and the seal lock. For each foreign routine owner that is still unresolved it records a `terminal-failure-proved` resolution only when every witness is positively observed, twice, against an unchanged seal: the cycle is reserved with a failure and its window ended at least a minute ago; no transaction is pending or finalizing; the canonical durable failure equals the cycle's copy, names its exact sha and control sha, and the runtime it says was recovered is the sealed desired runtime; the engine container is running exactly that image; the run's request, intent, break deadline, intake request and intent, generation pin and image lease are absent; and its release service, intake service and intake path are loaded or not-found, inactive or failed, with no main or control PID, no queued job, not enabled and no wants link. Any error, timeout or unexpected value leaves the owner authoritative and the ordinary owner check that follows keeps its refusal.

Nothing is retired or rewritten: the cycle, its failure, its consumed hour and the audit history stay exactly as recorded, and `retiredRoutineCycles` remains refused. The owner gate stays read-only and never probes native state; it only validates recorded resolutions, which must match their original cycle exactly, be verified by a different run and postdate the window. The pinned 41349091113-1 record keeps its own validator.

Regression: `tests/operations/terminal-failed-routine-owner.py` (law wrapper `tests/a-terminal-failed-routine-owner-is-released-only-by-proof.law.test.ts`) proves a complete proof is recorded without rewriting history, fourteen missing or unknown witnesses each keep the owner, and a tampered record refuses. #9257's preservation cases and the hourly maintenance suite pass unchanged.
