## V33 Depth Ceiling: Why The Nightly And The Rerun Disagreed

**Cause: the nightly heads-up rows were dealt with the blinds reversed.**
Until #9526 (`8715922322a2`, merged 2026-11-05 10:58 UTC) the two-seat league
simulator posted the small blind from the non-button seat and the big blind
from the button. The live engine (`engine/headsUpButton.ts`) and every solver
key (`gtoV31Position`: dealer = SB) put the small blind on the button, so the
brain played every heads-up league hand from the wrong position. The rerun
harness on #9633 already carries #9526; every nightly row through 2026-11-05
was dealt by an engine without it (engine 71087373, which has it, started at
18:11 UTC on 2026-11-05, after that day's heads-up rows were written at 14:03
to 14:34 UTC). The 2026-11-06 card is the first nightly on the corrected button.

The reversed simulator reproduces the nightly signature: ten times faster per

code. Six-max cards are unaffected (their blinds always followed the button):
v17_river_probe replays at 6.2 ms per hand locally against the nightly's 4.4.
Ruled out along the way: a V31 store in the nightly worker (no dataset is
active; `fn_gto_v31_serving_admission` returns none), the chart and postflop
stores (production holds the same 240 / 7,747 rows the rerun snapshot does),
and the equity governor (the worker forces it off, as the rerun does).

**Which harness reflects production:** the corrected button (#9526) and so the
rerun harness. The nightly harness itself is already fixed on main; what was
still wrong was the pool. Reversed-blind rows carry a smaller stderr than
corrected ones, so `fn_league_pooled` (inverse-variance, 7-day window) would
have let them outweigh the first corrected nights for a week.

**Fix:** migration `20261106052532` makes `fn_league_pooled` leave out the six
heads-up cards' rows dealt before 2026-11-06 (`hu_mind_layer`,
`hu_v16_overlay`, `v29_gto_flop`, `v30_gto_turn_river`, `v32_facing_defense`,
`v33_depth_ceiling_400bb`). Nothing is deleted. Pinned by
`server/src/benchmark/HorseLeagueHeadsUpEpoch.test.ts`; the button itself is
pinned by `HorseLeagueHeadsUpBlinds.test.ts` (#9526). It installs through
apply-merged-migration after merge.

its own lane; this PR does not flip it.

## V55 Omaha River Trips Flat: A Targeted Evaluation

V55 changes only a river Omaha decision holding weak trips (trips through a
board pair, or a set on a straight- or flush-possible board) facing
aggression: the committed branch calls instead of jamming, and the raise
branch calls instead of re-raising. The horse mirror reached it in 2 of 30,000
pairs.

**Evaluator:** `server/src/benchmark/HorseLeagueSpotConditioned.ts`. It deals
the human-field duplicate schedule (same seeds, dealer rotation and hero seat
as `runHumanFieldMatchup`) and plays only the deals whose hero river hand is
trips, a superset of V55's gate. A deal outside the class plays identically in
both arms, so its paired difference is exactly zero; the test proves that on
60 deals instead of assuming it, and proves the evaluator deals exactly the
cards `playHand` deals. The conditioned mean times the class rate is the
per-hand effect over all deals.

**Field:** the human-calibrated population's own Omaha profile, under
`HUMAN_CALIBRATED_OMAHA_DEV_POPULATION_ID`. The calibrated NLH field (#7936)
cannot deal Omaha (`HUMAN_FIELD_VARIANTS` stays NLH), and the Omaha profile
misses its flop facing-bet fidelity criterion (5.6 points against 5), so this
is a development measurement, never a promotion. The nightly worker refuses
the id. The field's raise menu is capped at the pot for pot-limit variants
(it was a no-limit menu). Seeds 2026110641 to 2026110643 (development), six-max, 100bb and 400bb, hero 'balanced' (the
human-field card has no Hybrid option).

**Reach:** the class is 8.8% of PLO4 deals, 9.7% of PLO5 and 10.3% of PLO6.
Inside it, the hero faces a river bet with weak trips about 60 times per 1,000
class pairs, and V55 changes the action in about 1 in 5 of those (raise or
jam becomes call); 0.2 to 0.6 pairs per 1,000 settle differently.

**Result** (conditioned = per 100 in-class hero hands; overall = per 100 hero
hands of every deal):

| Variant | Stack | Class pairs       | Divergent | Conditioned bb/100 | Overall bb/100   |
| ------- | ----- | ----------------- | --------- | ------------------ | ---------------- |
| PLO4    | 100bb | 200,000 (3 seeds) | 117       | -0.31 +/- 0.25     | -0.027 +/- 0.022 |
| PLO4    | 400bb | 175,000 (2 seeds) | 69        | +0.08 +/- 0.50     | +0.007 +/- 0.044 |
| PLO5    | 100bb | 200,000 (3 seeds) | 107       | -0.28 +/- 0.25     | -0.027 +/- 0.024 |
| PLO5    | 400bb | 175,000 (2 seeds) | 40        | -0.52 +/- 0.60     | -0.051 +/- 0.058 |
| PLO6    | 100bb | 175,000 (2 seeds) | 2         | +0.00 +/- 0.04     | +0.000 +/- 0.004 |
| PLO6    | 400bb | 150,000 (1 seed)  | 1         | -0.15 +/- 0.15     | -0.016 +/- 0.016 |

**Switch-on rule:** a significant positive league result. No cell is
significant and none is positive beyond noise; the 100bb cells lean slightly
negative. V55 does not meet its rule. It stays opt-in, now as a measured
result rather than an unreachable one. PLO6 is decided before V55 by the V59
river discipline (the PLO6 set folds), as its scenario test already records.
