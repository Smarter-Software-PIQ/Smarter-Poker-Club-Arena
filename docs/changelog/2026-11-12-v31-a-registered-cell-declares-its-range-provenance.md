# A registered V31 cell declares its range provenance, and the data must agree

2026-11-12. At least 61 of the 100 cells of dataset R8 were solved with the
pipeline model `conditional_history` ("authored_range_preserving_response"):
every earlier bet, check and call is forced and both players keep their full
authored root ranges at the target node, although in real play those earlier
decisions narrow the ranges. Nothing recorded or checked that assumption. The
database had no field for it, the cell key names only a node role, and no gate
tests it, so the round passed every check up to its seal. R8 stays rejected
and is not touched by this change.

Migration `20261112185251` extends the round registration of `20261110213931`
with `cell_provenance`: one entry per registered cell, generated by the World
Hub pipeline (`public_hist215.build_cell_provenance`) and recomputed by
`verify_round_registration`, which refuses any registration whose provenance
differs from the generated one.

| field                     | content                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `range_construction`      | `root`, `natural` or `subgame`. `forced` and anything else is refused.                                                                                                                      |
| `public_history`          | the exact ordered public actions before the node (street, position, solver seat, family, target chips) and, per step, how many actions the betting tree offered there (`available_actions`) |
| `upstream_solve_checksum` | for a `subgame`, the solve whose natural ranges are its inputs; `null` otherwise. Recorded, not verified: no table here holds the upstream solve                                            |
| `unnarrowed_seats`        | seats that took one action with every hand at their earlier decision: `{seat, reason: pure_prior_action, upstream_evidence_checksum}`; empty otherwise                                      |
| `game`                    | variant `nlh`, two players to the flop, pot type, depth bucket, chips per big blind, effective stack, root pot, rake `[fraction, cap]`, betting-tree checksum                               |
| `runtime_mapping`         | the runtime key components the engine may serve it for (street, node role, facing, hero and opponent positions, depth and game keys), the acting solver seat and the seat positions         |

Four checks bind it. The database trusts one declared value, stated plainly:
`available_actions` is a count SQL cannot derive (it cannot read the betting
tree); the pipeline is the check, deriving it from the manifest's `tree_lines`.

1. **Registration** (`gto_v31_round_registration_provenance`). Missing,
   malformed, unknown or `forced` provenance is refused. `root` is refused when
   any earlier step had more than one available action. A bet, call, raise or
   all-in can never be declared a one-action step. `natural` and `subgame` may
   follow any history: a correctly built subgame is not refused because its
   history contains decisions. The runtime mapping must be the cell's own key
   components; its acting seat must sit at the cell's hero position and every
   history step's position must be its seat's. An unnarrowed seat must have
   made a real earlier decision, and a `root` cell can declare none.
2. **Dataset insert** (`gto_v31_dataset_matches_range_provenance`). The
   declared betting tree must be the dataset's manifest checksum.
3. **Source ingest** (`gto_v31_source_artifact_records_range_evidence`, on
   `gto_v31_source_artifacts`, which both ingest functions write after the
   `solved_spots_gold` row). Each source node of a registered cell must have
   been solved after exactly the declared public history, with the declared
   rake, tree, stack, root pot and acting seat, and must carry exact node
   ranges (policy export v4). Each player's range joins a scale group: the
   first earlier board of the cell whose range it is a constant rescale of
   (same support, relative ratio spread at most 1e-4), or a new group. The
   result is kept in `gto_v31_range_evidence`.
4. **Seal** (`gto_v31_seal_matches_range_provenance`). Per player, the
   rescale share is the largest scale group over the cell's boards (0 when no
   two boards share one), so it does not depend on which board was ingested
   first. For `natural` and `subgame`, a player who made a real earlier
   decision is refused when the share exceeds 0.10: that is the forced-line
   signature the pipeline's own `validate_conditional_support` enforces, and
   it is what R8 was; one natural board among 64 forced ones does not hide it.
   A seat declared unnarrowed (pure prior action) must instead show a share of
   at least 0.90, and the seal records the attestation. For `root`, both
   players must have share 1. The runtime mapping must name a runtime cell the
   dataset built. A cell needs two source boards to be verifiable, every
   ingested node must have its evidence, and a refusal consumes no held-out set.

**Tolerance.** `validate_conditional_support` admits a forced board whose
per-combo ratios sit within relative 1e-6 of its first ratio, so two forced
boards can differ by about 4e-6; 1e-4 is 25 times that. Measured read-only on
PokerIQ-Production on 2026-11-12: the forced v4 ranges of R8 and R7 differ
across boards by exactly 0 (all 100 R8 cells on one board pair, nine R8 cells
on all 64 board pairs per seat), and the smallest real narrowing in the
natural v4 datasets R3, R4 and R5 is 2.7e-2, 270 times above 1e-4.

The comparison is between source boards, not against a declared root vector. A
forced line leaves `range(board) = c(board) x root` on every board, so any two
boards agree up to a constant wherever neither board blocks the hand. A
declared vector could be wrong; the boards cannot.

## What does not change

No quality threshold, seal metric or gate. No existing dataset, registration
or source row is modified, relabelled or re-validated: R6, R7 (evaluating) and
R8 (rejected) keep their rows and states, and the migration asserts that it
wrote no provenance and no evidence for them. A dataset without a registration
could already not be sealed; a registration without provenance can no longer
be created.

## What is deliberately left to its owner

- **Engine loader.** RANGE-DESIGN.md section 3 names a third enforcement
  point: the engine loader refusing a cell whose `range_construction` is absent
  or `forced`. It is not in this change (`server/**` is under another owner's
  release custody). It needs `range_construction` and the tree line in the
  cell payload the engine loads.
- **Which checks are step-level tree facts.** `available_actions` is
  generated from the manifest's betting tree by World Hub; SQL cannot read the
  tree and trusts the count. World Hub refuses any registration whose
  provenance differs from the generated one and any forced-line scenario.
- **Subgame upstream.** `upstream_solve_checksum` is recorded, not verified.
  A subgame is held to the same data-evidence rule as a natural cell.
- **The density pre-flight** (`server/src/scripts/gtoV31DensityPreflight.ts`)
  still emits the registration block of 2026-11-10, without `cell_provenance`.
  That block is no longer registrable as it stands; the round writer adds the
  provenance from the manifest.
