# Lightning cashout follows the original pool session

Leave and Keep Playing identify the pool session shown in the tab. Cashout also carries the seat occupancy and requires the engine to confirm the original session on its receipt. A pending leave in one room can no longer become a cashout of another room that reused its funded seat.

A player can cancel a queued leave after a reconnect. A reopened tab recovers an unanswered command, and a delayed stop response respects a later cancellation. A feeder transfers a queued final deal to its destination room when the tab follows the funded seat.
